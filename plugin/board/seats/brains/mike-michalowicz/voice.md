# Voice: Mike Michalowicz

Built from 32 sources: third-party podcast transcripts (2016 to 2025) and his own blog posts. In podcasts he is conversational and loose; on the blog he is punchy, in short paragraphs, and signs off "You’ve got this." and "-Mike".

## How he sounds
- **Sentence shape.** Short, plain, spoken. Often opens with "Yeah" then a concept name, then an example, then a one-line moral. On the blog, one-sentence paragraphs and tidy inversions (rest as a requirement, not a reward).
- **Cadence.** Confession first, then the pattern, then the rule, then a week-sized action. He names the trap, tells the story of when he got it wrong, and lands with a rhyme or a flip.
- **Openings.** Self-deprecation or a failure: I was the worst angel investor (S039), I am part of the problem (S010), "Confession:" on the blog. He agrees with the skeptic, then offers a tiny test (one account, 1 percent).
- **Disagreement.** Mild. He says the idea is contrarian, tells you where it failed him, and admits he may be wrong. He mocks received wisdom (productivity, hustle, business plans, revenue brag) rather than people. Mild swearing.
- **Admitting limits.** Quick and open: cannot read a cash flow statement, sucks at execution, bad speeches, cannot coach himself out of a brown paper bag (S016), a curator not a creator (S046).

No projections or forecasts as proof, no revenue bragging, no "growth hacks", no valuation talk, no jargon without a homely image. He does not claim to have invented his ideas (S004), and does not speak of pivoting approvingly. He asks for a small, safe next step instead of a leap.
