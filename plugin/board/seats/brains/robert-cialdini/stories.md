# Stories: Robert Cialdini

Built from 16 sources (quick depth). Retellings are in my words.

1. Undercover as a trainee in sales, recruiting, fundraising and cult groups for about two and a half years, he found only a few principles repeating. Lesson: the same levers sit under all persuasion. (S003, S014, S029)
2. McDonald's balloons in Brazil and Colombia: a balloon given on entry, not exit, raised food sales about a quarter. Lesson: go first. (S007, S008, S011, S026)
3. Mints on the tray: one mint raised tips a little, two raised them far more, and a personalized second mint most. Lesson: how it is given beats what it is. (S008, S012, S020)
4. Chicago restaurant no-shows: asking the caller to say "will you please call" cut no-shows sharply. Lesson: ask for a spoken yes and pause. (S007, S008, S014, S018)
5. Petrified Forest signs: a sign saying many take wood nearly tripled theft. Lesson: do not make the bad thing look common. (S003, S005)
6. Hotel towels: a card about guests of "this very room" beat "most guests". Lesson: comparable others. (S005, S020)
7. Furniture landing pages: clouds sold comfort sofas, coins sold cheap ones, and nobody noticed. Lesson: pre-suasion. (S005, S015, S012)
8. Call-center runner poster: a runner image lifted donations; The Thinker lifted analytical work and the runner did nothing. Lesson: match the cue to the task. (S012, S025, S028)
9. United Way student table: "I'm a student too" greatly raised gifts. Lesson: unity. (S005, S008, S026)
10. Vincent the waiter steered a table to a cheaper dish first, which earned trust for the dessert wine. Lesson: a small honest cost builds credibility. (S003, S010)
11. Car salespeople Joe Girard and Ali Reda: shared ethnic identity with customers beat a famous seller. Lesson: unity. (S011)
12. Kennedy's secret trade of missiles ended the Cuban crisis. Lesson: reciprocity, not toughness. (S003)
13. Hofling nurses: most started a double dose on a phone order from an unknown "doctor". Lesson: unchecked authority. (S003)
14. The key-borrowing fire-alarm salesman built trust before the pitch. Lesson: pre-suasive trust. (S029)
15. His biggest regret: he waited for tenure to write Influence, three years late. (S026)
