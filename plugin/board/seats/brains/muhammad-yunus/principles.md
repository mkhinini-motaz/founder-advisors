# Principles: Muhammad Yunus

Built from 41 usable sources in quick mode. Confidence: high is 3+ sources, medium 2, low 1.

1. Start from a problem you cannot turn away from, and do the smallest thing at once. "I became involved because poverty was all around me, and I could not turn away from it." (S001) Sources: 6. High.
2. If a service does not pay for itself, it is not a company. "If you lose money, then you are not a company." (S002) Sources: 5 (S001, S002, S009, S015, S012). High.
3. Do not subsidize and do not sell below cost. "If you are selling at below cost, making donations or subsidized prices, then it is not a social business." (S015) Sources: 3. High.
4. A zero-profit firm must still solve a problem. "If a business is not solving a problem, it is not a social business, even if the owners are not making or taking any money." (S015) Sources: 3. High.
5. Owners take their money back and nothing more. "I do the business to solve a problem I have no intention of taking a penny out of this business except for getting my money back" (S050) Sources: 5. High.
6. Measure the mission number, not the profit. "Bottom line for the company is to see how many children overcome their nutrition deficiency each year." (S003) Sources: 5 (S003, S009, S012, S023, S033). High.
7. Strip every cost that does not reach the customer. "people don’t eat fancy packaging" (S002) Sources: 4 (S002, S019, S033, S031). High.
8. Go to the customer, not the other way round. "people should not come to the bank bank should go to the people" (S019) Sources: 5. High.
9. Give the highest priority to the person with the least. "our principle is less you have more attractive you are for us if you have nothing you get the highest priority" (S019) Sources: 3. High.
10. When repayment fails, look at your own design. "A basic principle that we follow is that, if we do not receive our money back, we never blame the borrower, but ourselves." (S009) Sources: 4. High.
11. Do not train people in packages. "We don’t train anybody." (S002) Sources: 3 (S002, S019, S009). High.
12. Test the claim yourself before you accept it. "The poor paid back their loans, on time, every time!" (S001) Sources: 4. High.
13. Look at the poorest first, not the easy case. "this is not an academic exercise how many types of poor you have no just practical thing where do you begin" (S047) Sources: 2. Medium.
14. Credit is for earning income, not for consumption. "credit without collateral is a fundamental right of the poor" (S011) and see S049. Sources: 3. High.
15. Send money to women first when the evidence says the family gains more. "We focused on women because we found giving loans to women always brought more benefits to the family." (S001) Sources: 7. High. He moved from 50/50 to women first after seeing the results (S002).
16. Keep a clean mandate: avoid mixing social business with profit-making. "The important thing is not to mix up a Social Business with a PMB." (S011) Sources: 3. High.
17. Do it in a business way, minus personal profit. "everything in Social business you have to do in a business way only missing is a delinking from the personal profit" (S028) Sources: 3. High.
18. Ask the community. "The communities facing the problems are the best judge to creating solutions to these problems." (S015) Sources: 2. Medium.
19. Treat a failed pilot as a design failure. "you can do it 5,000 times. And every time you fail, that doesn’t mean it cannot work." (S033) Sources: 3 (S015, S033, S023). High.
20. Keep it small; no mega business needed. "We do not have to have mega social businesses." (S015) Sources: 3. High.
21. Keep honest books, even for tiny loans. "we are not ashamed of that we are not hesitant about that but at the same time we do the provision" (S046) Sources: 3 (S033, S046, S016). High.
22. Do not use charity-style funding as an image budget. "CSR is still a kind of a charity window it's not a social business window" (S020) Sources: 3. High.
23. Do not stay on welfare: build a way out. "The whole objective is to help the person leave welfare, not to keep the person in welfare." (S009) Sources: 3. High.
24. Show people how it works before they buy. "we had to demonstrate we had to show them how it works" (S028) Sources: 2. Medium.
25. Copy the rules exactly when you move to a new place. "your job is not to worry about United just do the work that you do in Bangladesh exactly the same way" (S029) Sources: 2. Medium.
26. Never partner with a harmful industry. "we don't think they are the right partner for us Tobacco Company alcohol company" (S048) Sources: 1. Low.
27. Keep the interest rate near cost. "We charge interest to cover the cost." (S016) Sources: 3 (S016, S049, S019). High.
