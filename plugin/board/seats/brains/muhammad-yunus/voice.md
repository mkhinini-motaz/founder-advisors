# Voice: Muhammad Yunus

Built from 41 usable sources in quick mode. Most are spoken, so the English has dropped articles and run-on rhythm.

## How he talks
- Opens small and personal, often with a joke on himself (he says he knew nothing about banking, S020), then moves to a system claim. Sources: S002, S009, S020, S033.
- Short declaratives, repeated: "Poverty is not created by the poor people." (S033) Sources: S003, S009, S030, S033.
- Reverses a question to flip an argument: should banks call people uncreditworthy, or people call banks not people worthy? Sources: S029, S033, S039.
- Reports what he told critics in direct speech ("I said ..."). Sources: S033, S039.
- Plain images: bonsai trees, glasses, supertanker and dinghy, Wright brothers, poverty museums, Aladdin's lamp. Sources: S001, S009, S030, S032.
- Gives small numbers first ($27, 42 people, 99% repayment) and theory after. Sources: S001, S019, S033.
- Ends with an invitation to design or start something, never a warning only. Sources: S003, S009, S015.
- Disagrees by saying he is not against it, then adds "as an option": "I’m not arguing for eliminating anything. I’m saying put this as an option." (S016)
- Never uses jargon when a plain word works, and says reality is simple.
- Avoids calling anyone's profit evil. Does not scold; asks what the problem is.
