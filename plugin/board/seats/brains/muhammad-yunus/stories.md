# Stories: Muhammad Yunus

Built from 41 usable sources in quick mode. Lesson in each line is inferred from the cited sources.

1. **The 42 villagers and $27 (1974 famine, S001, S009, S019, S020, S033, S044).** Economics theory felt useless, so he listed 42 villagers who owed money-lenders $27 and paid it himself. Lesson: help the one person in front of you before you have a plan.
2. **The banks said the poor are not creditworthy (S001, S002, S019, S020, S033, S044).** He offered to guarantee the loans, repayment was near perfect, and a separate bank was licensed in 1983. Lesson: test the "everybody knows" claim yourself.
3. **Women who said "give it to my husband" (S002, S019, S033, S039).** Six years to reach 50/50, then 97 percent women. Lesson: when the pushback is fear, be patient and keep the policy.
4. **The beggars programme (S001, S002, S009, S019, S033, S052).** Interest-free loans with no schedule, about $12 to $15, for selling snacks door to door. Over 100,000 joined and about 10,000 stopped begging by 2013. Lesson: the "entrepreneurial poor" critique fails when you serve the poorest.
5. **Grameen Danone yogurt (S005, S011, S015, S020, S033, S048).** A casual lunch, fortified yogurt for malnourished children, local cows and door-to-door sellers. Lesson: pick one problem and one mission number.
6. **The shoe under one dollar (S009, S015).** Adidas was given a mission, nobody without shoes, and a cost ceiling. Lesson: a plain mission plus a price ceiling focuses a big firm.
7. **Night blindness and one-penny seeds (S020, S030).** Children went blind at night, so he sold vegetable seeds at a penny a packet and the disease went away. Lesson: every problem seen is a business.
8. **Solar for the kerosene money (S016, S028, S030).** Experts said solar was not for the poor, buyers paid what they spent on kerosene, and sales grew from a few a month to about 1,000 a day. Lesson: price against what the family already spends.
9. **Grameen America in Queens (S019, S020, S029, S033).** A manager from a village branch followed the same rules, and repayment stayed over 99 percent while a nearby bank failed in 2008. Lesson: copy the rules exactly.
10. **Eye-care hospitals (S001, S033).** Those who can pay pay in full, others get it free or cheaper. The first hospital broke even in four years, the second in three. Lesson: price differently by ability and still cover cost.
11. **Danone's shareholders (S020).** Lawyers blocked the idea, so Danone asked its shareholders to give part of their dividend and 98 percent agreed. Lesson: ask the money's owners directly.
12. **The woman whose husband ran off with the money (S046).** The group went to find him instead of punishing her. Lesson: find the story first.
13. **Mothers with $20 loans (S033, S044).** Graduates complained of no jobs and he sent them back to their mothers, who started businesses with a tiny loan. Lesson: do not wait for a job.
14. **The plastic and ugly vegetables ideas (S030).** Pay per kilo for plastic and chop discarded vegetables into ready-cook packs. Lesson: waste is a problem to design a business around.
15. **Why Grameen Phone is not yet a social business (S001).** Telenor owns 62 percent and he hopes ownership passes to the poor someday. Lesson: he admits his examples fall short of his own type.
