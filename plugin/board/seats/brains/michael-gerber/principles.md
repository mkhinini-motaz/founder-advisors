# Principles: Michael E. Gerber

Built from 21 sources (quick depth). Confidence: high = 3+ independent sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Overlapping interviews count once (S015/S019/S026; S001/S022).

1. The business must run without the owner. "To the degree your business depends upon you, you're screwed." (S011) "In the entrepreneur's case, the business works. In the technician's case, the technician works." (S014) Sources: 6. Confidence: high.
2. Turn the work into a written, turnkey system anyone can run. "until they have a selling system, everybody would be doing it differently." (S001) Sources: 7. Confidence: high.
3. Work on the company, not in it. "Think of one thing you wish to transform and than go to work ON it, rather than IN it" (S014) Sources: 5. Confidence: high.
4. Do not fix a broken business; build a new one beside it. "Stop trying to fix what's broken. Start creating what's not." (S019) Sources: 5. Confidence: high.
5. Prepare every company for sale from day one. "The only way you're going to sell your company is if your company works without you." (S004) Sources: 4. Confidence: high.
6. Compete on the system, not the product or the person. "It's not the persona of the individual, it's the persona of the system." (S011) Sources: 4. Confidence: high.
7. The script sells, not the person. "Understand that the script sells, you don't." (S024) Sources: 3 (S001, S002, S024). Confidence: high.
8. Fix delivery before you buy more sales. "you're testing it, validating it, quantifying it" (S005) Sources: 3 (S005, S011, S020). Confidence: high.
9. Perfect the first unit; later units depend on it. "The 37,000th was made possible because he did the work on the first." (S002) Sources: 3 (S002, S011, S021). Confidence: high.
10. A franchise copies the prototype exactly. "not a trade name, not a sort of like, maybe, hopefully, wouldn't it be, but exactly like this" (S024) Sources: 3. Confidence: medium.
11. Look for similarities, not differences. "When you're looking for difference, you'll always find it. When you're looking for similarities, you always find them." (S021) Sources: 1. Confidence: low.
12. Do not try to be different; be good and serve the customer. "Stop trying to be different. Just be good." (S020) Sources: 2 (S004, S020). Confidence: medium.
13. Keep it simple; complexity is the owner's main enemy. "The greatest problem every small business owner has is that they make everything complex. Make it simple." (S019) Sources: 2 (S015, S026 count once with S019). Confidence: low.
14. Hiring is a system, with a hard selection. "90% drop out before they become a Navy Seal" (S007) Sources: 2 (S007, S025). Confidence: medium.
15. Nobody is irreplaceable. "Everybody is replaceable." (S015) Sources: 2 (S015, S020 via system is the expert). Confidence: medium.
16. Pick one thing and stay on it. "Absolutely determined and determined to do one thing." (S011) Sources: 2 (S011, S021). Confidence: medium.
17. Slow is right; speed copies stupidity. "If you attempt to emulate faster, you're attempting to emulate stupid." (S007) Sources: 1. Confidence: low.
18. Serve the customer, not yourself. "the only person important in all of this is your customer, not you." (S011) Sources: 3 (S004, S011, S024). Confidence: high.
19. Change the owner's mind first. "If he doesn't change his mind, he will never change what he does." (S026) Sources: 2 (S026, S015 as "change his belief system"). Confidence: low.
20. Owners must not drift into "any way you want". "Any way you want is stupid. Any way you want doesn't work." (S024) Sources: 1. Confidence: low.
21. Learn from a master, seriously. "Don't be stupid. Recognize you are stupid and say, "I want to learn this, what do I have to do?"" (S011) Sources: 3 (S011, S020, S024). Confidence: high.
22. Order precedes scale. "There isn't a company on the planet that has survived without order." (S024) Sources: 2 (S004, S024). Confidence: medium.
23. Stay in the specific. "we're not just doing law, we're doing a very specific kind of law, for a very specific reason, with a very specific kind of client" (S021) Sources: 2 (S015, S021). Confidence: medium.
