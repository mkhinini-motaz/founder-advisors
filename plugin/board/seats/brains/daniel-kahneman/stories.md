# Stories: Daniel Kahneman

Built from 27 ok sources (quick depth). Each is a story he reuses; the lesson is stated in one line.

1. **The Israeli curriculum textbook.** His team guessed one and a half to two and a half years; the curriculum expert's class data said 40 percent of such teams never finish and the rest take seven to ten years; the book took eight and was worthless, and nobody quit. Lesson: knowing the outside view is not using it. S004, S020, S027.
2. **The army interview.** At 21 or 22 he built a six-trait structured interview with a closing close-your-eyes score; it beat free impressions and lasted fifty years. Lesson: facts first, gut last. S002, S017, S019, S020, S029.
3. **The insurance noise audit.** Executives expected about 10 percent difference between two underwriters; it was 50 (58 for claims assessors) and nobody knew. Lesson: measure the scatter. S015, S026, S033, S035.
4. **The flight instructors.** Praise looked to fail and punishment to work; it was regression to the mean. Lesson: feedback is read wrongly without it. S002.
5. **Bat and ball.** About half of students at elite universities say ten cents without checking. Lesson: the easy answer goes unchecked. S001, S018.
6. **Linda the bank teller.** Similarity beats probability. Lesson: the story outranks the logic. S001, S005.
7. **The colonoscopy and cold-water studies.** A milder extra minute at the end made the memory better. Lesson: peak and end rule memory, duration is neglected. S001, S017, S026.
8. **Dating before happiness.** Ask about dates first and the correlation with happiness appears. Lesson: attribute substitution. S018, S037.
9. **The CFO survey.** CFOs gave 80 percent intervals and were surprised 67 percent of the time. Lesson: overconfidence without scorekeeping. S022.
10. **The honesty box with eyes.** Tea money rose under a poster of eyes (priming, 2008 era; see the caution that this literature later failed to replicate). S005, S006, S018.
11. **The 1941 SS officer.** A seven-year-old was hugged and given money by an SS man who showed a photo of his son. Lesson: people are complicated. S017, S026, S029.
12. **The Econometrica accident.** The prospect theory paper went to an economics journal because it was the best theory venue. Lesson: influence was chancy. S003, S023, S028, S029.
13. **The financial advisor who fired him.** Told to use general instructions and not look often; she fired him when he said he did not need more money. S022.
14. **Acid rain lakes.** Willingness to pay was similar for one lake and all lakes: a prototype stands in for a set. S004, S033.
