# Positions: Daniel Kahneman

Built from 27 ok sources (quick depth). Dates are the source dates. Topics with no evidence say so. Newer view marked where he changed.

## Rationality and bias
- **Is he saying people are irrational?** No. "People are reasonable, they’re prudent agents." (S003, 2002). Later: "People are quite reasonable, but they are not logical." (S033). Rationality as consistency is a norm no finite mind can meet (S015, S027).
- **Bias versus noise.** Newer view (2018): "bias has been overestimated at the expense of noise." (S015). In 2017 he also said his work "led people to exaggerate the importance of bias in human affairs and in human thinking" (S026).
- **Debiasing yourself.** Early 2008: his own judgment "has not improved." (S006). 2017: "not a lot has been achieved in de-biasing." (S026). Train people to spot bias in others, not themselves (S026).

## Decisions and judgment
- **Gut versus analysis.** Trust skill in a regular world; check in a minefield. "if you’re a good golfer, just golf." (S022). Delay, do not drop, intuition (S019).
- **Hiring and interviews.** Structured and independent, then gut last (S017, S020). Interviewers decide in the first minute or two and then confirm (S017).
- **Forecasting.** Long-range forecasts by pundits are no better than dart throwing: "The world is probably not predictable." (S018). 2008 crisis: "I think it wasn't knowable." (S022).
- **Planning and timelines.** Outside view, several reference classes (S004, S020, S027).
- **Big strategic decisions.** Joint article (S012): process over genius, "a disciplined decision-making process, not individual genius, is the key to a sound strategy." (S012). Credit three authors.
- **Sunk cost.** Drop what fails; a new CEO has an advantage with no sunk cost (S015, S003).
- **Metrics and scorekeeping.** Little exists and many people resist it: "It's a dangerous activity." (S022). Keep a decision log (S022).
- **Algorithms and AI.** "People are noisy. Algorithms are not." (S039). Human plus machine "is likely to be unstable" (S027); deep learning is System 1 like (S032, S020).
- **Competition and timing.** No recorded position.

## Money and risk
- **Optimism and risk-taking.** Newer view (2017, 2018): "overconfidence, and overconfident optimism, is the engine of capitalism." (S026), but 2017 he doubts his earlier wish to banish it: "I did say that, but I’m not sure I was right." (S026). Most new restaurants quit within a few years (S026).
- **Investing.** "Just don't look too often." (S022). Stock picking is delusion (S039). He says he invests terribly (S022).
- **Loss aversion.** Real, his most useful idea (S002), but weaker for experienced traders in routine deals (S015) and not a law of nature (S027). Weak for ruin-sized stakes in his own theory (S001).
- **Fairness and pricing.** The existing price is a reference transaction people feel entitled to (S002).
- **Raising money, debt, equity, partners.** No recorded position, except partnership: "I've done almost nothing by myself. I've always collaborated." (S028).

## People and organisations
- **Disagreement and dissent.** Joint article: absence of dissent is an alarm (S012). Pool independent views before discussion (S004).
- **Changing minds.** "I like changing my mind, and I look for ways of changing my mind." (S026). Reward it in organisations (S022). Newer note: he also says people rarely change on what matters (S032).
- **Executives and improving decisions.** Leaders talk but will not invest: "there isn't a huge wish to improve decision-making—there is a lot of talk about doing so" (S004). Decision analysis: "it’s a flop." (S004).
- **Leaders showing doubt.** People prefer confident leaders (S022); but firms with decision standards can bear them (S022).
- **Firing, culture, remote work, burnout, work hours.** No recorded position.

## Marketing and customers
- **Advertising.** Works on System 1; repetition makes things feel truer (S018, S022).
- **Environment and priming.** 2008 view: "We have a hugely powerful bias against the environment as a determinant of behavior." (S006). Caution: 2008 priming claims came before the replication problems, and he later said some results in his book are unreliable (S015) and that he retracted the priming chapter (S027). Treat priming as unsettled.
- **Nudges.** "we’ve been too persuasive" (S033); behavioural economics successes are small (S033).
- **Pricing, discounting, brand, paid ads, niche.** No recorded position beyond the above.

## Wellbeing
- **Happiness.** Earlier (2007): the experience alone mattered (S004). Newer (2017, 2023): "What people want is more closely associated with the remembering self." (S026). Focus policy on misery (S017, S019).
- **Work.** Do not overbuild the resume; consider the experiencing self (S022).
