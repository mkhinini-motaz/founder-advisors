# Positions: Brian Chesky

Built from 16 interviews and talks, 2014 to 2024. Dates are the source dates. Where he changed his mind, both views sit side by side and the newer is marked.

## Marketplaces and trust
**Trust.** Strangers can live together if you design for it. "the world moves at the speed of trust." (S016, 2020-06). "Businesses are built on love and trust." (S017, 2020-06)
**Moderation.** Customers always want more of it, and the platform carries it. "The customer wants more moderation." (S034, 2024-10)
**Owning inventory.** No. "we didn’t have to hold inventory or pour concrete" (S021, 2021-11). Hotels: "not near term", never say never (S027, 2021-04).
**Supply exclusivity.** "you come to Airbnb to find something you can’t find anywhere else." (S034, 2024-10)
**Quality at scale.** "hospitality is a difficult thing to industrialize." (S034, 2024-10)
**Competition and clones.** "In a war between missionaries and mercenaries, the missionaries always win." (S014, 2023)
**Moat.** "we preferred our moat to be that we provide the very best experience in the world" (S055, 2014-10)
**Regulation.** Regulated city by city, so treat each city personally. "we had to treat every city personally." (S004). Unintended consequences: "always knowing there’re unintended consequences." (S004). Admits "there absolutely is merit to the concerns" about housing (S004).

## Product and growth
**Focus versus diversification.** Cut transportation, travel content, hotels, luxury. "We turned what had been the equivalent of a ten-division company into a single-division functional organization" (S080, 2021-07). The same man opened ten divisions earlier and called it hubris: "It’s kind of like the second album problem." (S021, 2021-11)
**Niche.** "to focus on a differentiated product to, like, really hone in on a niche category." (S014, 2023)
**Metrics.** Newer view in S091 is to A/B test only with a hypothesis: "if we do an AB test, there has to be a hypothesis." (S091). Earlier: "If you're only doing A/B tests you're never designing with empathy." (S074)
**Design.** "I still approach every problem like a designer." (S077, 2023-04). "Silicon Valley often treats design as a service organization." (S091)
**Product management.** "most product managers in Silicon Valley aren’t actually product managers." (S034, 2024-10). Not eliminated: "We didn't get rid of people helping drive the product." (S091)
**Paid ads.** Spent nearly a billion dollars on marketing ("We were spending nearly a billion dollars on marketing. We took it to zero." S004), later "pretty soulless, like, performance marketing" (S014). "Performance marketing though doesn't create very good accumulating advantages because it's not an investment." (S091). Still spends on it; no recorded position of stopping.
**Brand.** "utilities get sold at commodity prices" (S055, 2014-10). Brand held up with marketing paused: "Our traffic, even before resumed marketing, got to 95% of the traffic from the year prior." (S027, 2021-04)
**Content.** No recorded position.
**AI.** "it's gonna take longer than people think for it to reshape society when it does, it's gonna be more profound than people realize." (S073, 2024-05)
**Timing.** "I like to either ride a trend or ride the opposite of a trend." (S021, 2021-11)
**Pricing and discounting.** No recorded position.
**Side projects.** No recorded position.

## Money
**Raising money.** "We raised too much money, and most startups raised way too much money." (S019, 2023-10). "investors should invest in entrepreneurs, not markets." (S019)
**Debt.** Took emergency debt in 2020 rather than dilutive equity: "we took this really big gamble to do debt" (S019). Early credit cards: "I don't recommend doing this." (S077)
**Profit.** "I never focused on trying to make a lot of money." (S004). "I was just trying to become incredibly efficient." (S019, 2023-10)
**Selling the company.** No recorded position on selling Airbnb. On buying: "I just didn't like the culture." (S055, 2014-10), about the clone.
**Equity.** Gave 9.2 million shares to a host endowment (S080). "A private company is like wet cement: it has not hardened yet." (S080)
**Going public.** "it's easier to run a public company than a late stage private company." (S019, 2023-10)

## People and organization
**First hire.** "your first engineer was like bringing in DNA to your company" (S055, 2014-10). "would I want to hire 100 people like this person?" (S017)
**Firing and layoffs.** "I’m not sure there’s a way to do a layoff well" (S018, 2020-12). "You should be all in on people or be all out but not put them in a type of purgatory." (S080)
**Partners and cofounders.** "people that are so good they almost intimidate you" (S004). Weekly Sunday meeting since 2009.
**Delegation.** Older view (about 2010 to 2019): empowerment through autonomy; "this was all about me delegating responsibility." (S091). Newer view: "I don’t believe that the way to empower people is to give them autonomy." (S021, 2021-11), "I am the co-hiring manager." (S034, 2024-10)
**Culture.** 2014: write values early (S055). Newer, 2023: "your culture is the shared way you do things" (S039). Strong or weak, not good or bad: "there's no such thing as a good or bad culture." (S055)
**Diversity.** "Every early stage company should over-rotate on diversity, it will make you more successful." (S017, 2020-06)
**Remote work.** No mandate. "I didn’t need to mandate people going back to an office." (S091). "Any company that is not flexible is probably gonna lose out on talent." (S027, 2021-04)
**Work hours and burnout.** Worked 16 to 18 hour days; stayed in crisis mode about two years. "I actually get less productive if I don't step away" (S073, 2024-05). "my biggest regret would be the time I didn't spend with people I love" (S039, 2023-10)
**Company as family.** "it is true that a company's not a family." (S073, 2024-05)

## Society
**Capitalism and shareholders.** "The best thing for shareholders is for society to want us to exist." (S080, 2021-07). "I think capitalism needs a little bit of an evolution." (S080)
**Growth.** "Be very careful about being addicted to growth that is not good for the world." (S017, 2020-06)
**Loneliness.** "We’re probably living in the loneliest time in human history and probably only going to get worse." (S018, 2020-12)
**Tourism.** "I'm actually anti-tourism." (S019, 2023-10)
**Competition from hotels.** Coexist: "the longer you're away from home, the more you want" (S077, 2023-04)
