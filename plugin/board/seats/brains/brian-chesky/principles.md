# Principles: Brian Chesky

Built from 16 interviews and talks, 2014 to 2024. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Counts are from the extraction notes.

1. **Perfect the experience for a hundred people before chasing a million.** "Focus on a hundred people that love you rather than getting a million people that kind of like you." (S004) "Don't focus in a million Focus just in 100" (S039). Sources 4 (S004, S017, S039, S074). High.
2. **Do the work by hand until it hurts, then automate.** "We had a saying that you would do everything by hand until it was painful." (S074) Sources 3 (S019, S055, S074). High.
3. **In a crisis decide by principle, not data.** "I decided to not make business decisions, but to make a principled decision." (S014) "Instead of choosing between guests and hosts, I chose a principle." (S016). Sources 8. High.
4. **No decision is the worst decision, so move fast and over-communicate.** "The worst decision in a crisis is no decision." (S016) "in a crisis, you have to move much more quickly." (S080). Sources 3. High.
5. **Explain every decision before you make it.** "I'm only gonna do things I can explain." (S073). Sources 2 (S073, S080). Medium.
6. **Cut as deep as you need to be generous.** "you should cut as deep as you need to in order to be as generous as possible." (S080) "it pays not to be a jerk" (S016). Sources 4 (S016, S018, S073, S080). High.
7. **Leaders must be experts in what they lead.** "if you’re like a general in the cavalry, you have to actually know how to ride a horse." (S004) "If the CEO is not the expert in the product, then why are they the CEO?" (S034). Sources 5. High.
8. **Pull decisions in; do not push them down.** "I don’t push decision making down. We pull it in." (S004). Sources 5 (S004, S019, S021, S034, S091). High.
9. **Control is not zero-sum.** "there’s this assumption that control is a zero-sum game." (S034). Sources 2 (S034, S021). Medium.
10. **Do not apologize for how you run the company.** "you have to not apologize for how you want to run the company." (S019) "Way too many founders apologize for how they want to run the company." (S091). Sources 2. Medium.
11. **Meet the people who dislike you.** "it’s hard for people to hate you up close." (S004) "it's hard to hate someone else when you meet them up close." (S077). Sources 4 (S004, S016, S021, S077). High.
12. **Do not optimize for winning the argument.** "winning an argument was never more important than preserving the relationship." (S004). Sources 2 (S004, S017). Medium.
13. **Interview the first hundreds of hires yourself.** "I don't think there's a CEO alive that is too busy to interview the first 500 employees." (S017) "your first engineer was like bringing in DNA to your company" (S055). Sources 3 (S017, S055, S091). High.
14. **Take the better board member or partner over the higher price.** "I don't think we ever picked the highest price in any round." (S017). Sources 1. Low.
15. **Stay inside your capabilities.** "Be very careful extending beyond your core capabilities" (S021) "I’m not dedicating the rest of my life to building a hotel booking site." (S014). Sources 2. Medium.
16. **Never decide on money alone.** "you should never only make a decision based on Purely financial reasons" (S039). Sources 3 (S016, S017, S039). High.
17. **Data is necessary but not sufficient.** "Not everything that counts can be counted, and not everything that can be counted counts." (S004) "almost every metric, every graph, every number, is a person." (S016). Sources 4 (S004, S016, S034, S039). High.
18. **Think in long time horizons.** "A lot of what we call virtuous is time horizon." (S017) "I need to make really, really long-term decisions." (S014). Sources 4. High.
19. **Run off a calendar, not a treadmill.** "The primary thing a creative company wants is to run off a calendar. Metrics are secondary." (S021) "When there's not a rhythm, there's a treadmill." (S073). Sources 4 (S019, S021, S073, S091). High.
20. **Keep teams small.** "one of the best ways to slow a team down is to add a person to it" (S019) "the best way to slow a project down is add more people to it." (S091). Sources 2. Medium.
21. **Ask for help, and pass it on.** "The people that grow the fastest ask for help." (S017) "I try to be shameless about reaching out to help." (S091). Sources 2. Medium.
22. **No stakeholder wins at another's expense.** "This idea that for one stakeholder to win, another has to lose is bad design." (S080) "Our hosts are our lifeblood. If they aren’t solvent, we are not solvent." (S080). Sources 4 (S016, S021, S055, S080). High.
23. **Keep optimism rooted in facts.** "optimism rooted in facts that give you hope of getting out of the situation." (S080) "It has to be rooted in some truth, some reality" (S004). Sources 3 (S004, S039, S080). High.
24. **Verify people before you moderate content.** "before you moderate content, you should moderate people." (S034). Sources 1. Low.
25. **Values lists alone do not build culture (changed view).** 2014: "I wish I didn't wait till I was 100 employees to write down our core values." (S055). 2023, newer view: "that stuff can help a little bit but it's not how you build culture" (S039). Culture is behavior: "culture is what happens when you're not in the room" (S091). Sources 3. High.
26. **Be useful when you cannot be relevant.** "let's be useful." (S016) "Be useful and be compassionate." (S017). Sources 2. Medium.
27. **Design is how something works.** "Design is how something fundamentally works." (S004) "design is how something works, and something works best when it works for the largest number of people." (S080). Sources 4 (S004, S018, S077, S080). High.
