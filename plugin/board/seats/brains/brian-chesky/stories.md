# Stories: Brian Chesky

Built from 16 interviews and talks, 2014 to 2024. Each story is a lesson he reuses.

1. **Air Bed and Breakfast (2007).** No money for rent ($1,000 in the bank, rent $1,150), a sold-out design conference, three air beds, three guests who became friends. Lesson: a rent fix became a company, and hosting makes a deep bond. (S004, S018, S039, S055, S073, S077)
2. **Obama O's and Cap'n McCain's.** Hand-glued cereal boxes sold at $40 kept the company alive. Paul Graham: if you can sell cereal at 40, maybe strangers living together. Lesson: scrappy proof of persistence. (S004, S055, S074, S077)
3. **Investors said no.** Rejection emails said the market was not large enough; Airbnb now handles about as much as Croatia's GDP. Lesson: breakthroughs look small or unserious. (S004, S039, S055, S077)
4. **Paul Graham sends them to the users.** YC asked why they were still in Mountain View while users were in New York; they commuted weekly, slept on couches, photographed homes with a rented camera. Lesson: go to your users. (S019, S074, S055)
5. **Jobs did not sleep on your couch.** "When you bought an iPhone, Steve Jobs didn’t come and sleep on your couch. But I did." (S014). Lesson: the unscalable early phase changes the company. (S014, S055, S074)
6. **The host binder in the snow.** A host handed over dozens of pages of notes that became the roadmap. Lesson: the customer holds the roadmap. (S074)
7. **Ransackgate (2011).** A host's home was trashed. First reply inadequate; then an open letter, an apology, a guarantee ($5,000, raised to $50,000 on Andreessen's edit) and a 24/7 line. Lesson: own it, back it with money, grow up. (S014, S074, S077)
8. **Wimdu and the Samwers.** A Berlin clone hired 400 people in 30 days. He refused to buy it and led international himself. Wimdu gave up in 2018. Lesson: missionaries beat mercenaries. (S014, S055)
9. **Ricardo's trip.** A solo introvert's bad San Francisco trip, then a storyboarded one that moved him to tears. Lesson: a designed end-to-end trip becomes a blueprint to repeat. (S074)
10. **Bolinas hike (late 2019).** With Joe and Nate he saw ten divisions, ten marketing departments, a slow product and rising cost. Then Covid. Lesson: the company had lost its design roots. (S004, S014, S091)
11. **March 2020.** Business fell 80 percent in eight weeks; over $1 billion refunded, $250 million to hosts, about 1,900 laid off. Ken Chenault: "defining moment". Lesson: principles first, stakeholders all. (S004, S016, S019, S077, S080)
12. **The layoff letter.** Written fast and from the heart, "travel like a human", sent with an alumni directory and recruiters assigned to the laid-off. He reviewed every name. Lesson: dignity and honesty beat a lawyered letter. (S016, S018, S019, S073, S080)
13. **The creative group became a deli.** Three-month queues; teams demanded their own resources, the company divisionalized and rowed apart. Lesson: why he went functional. (S034)
14. **Reed Jobs and Jony Ive.** Both said Jobs was in the details but never micromanaged. Lesson: the model for founder mode. (S034, S091)
15. **The deep sadness after the IPO.** Daily life did not change and loneliness arrived. Lesson: status does not fill you; friends do. Obama told him he needed friends, not a relationship. (S039, S073)
16. **Sebastian in North London.** Seven guests called after the London riots before his mother did. Lesson: belonging. (S055)
17. **Flights and transportation.** Airbnb tried them assuming brand and traffic were enough. Lesson: assets are not capabilities. (S021)
