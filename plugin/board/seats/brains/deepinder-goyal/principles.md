# Principles: Deepinder Goyal

Built from 37 public sources, 2015 to 2026. Confidence: high is 3+ sources, medium 2, low 1.

1. Stay in the game; survival is the advantage. "the ability to remain in the game, financially, emotionally, institutionally, is the single biggest competitive advantage."  Also: "Most of our early competitors didn't make it. We stayed."  Sources: S079, S036, S085, S100. High (inferred). S085: "my Basics are is that I just don't want to fail like survival is my only continuous effort" (S085)
2. Absolute contribution beats percentage margin. "we care more about contribution $ than % margin." (S028) Sources: S028. Low.
3. Density decides whether a city lives. "this will change as the order density goes up, network effect kicks in" (S028) Sources: S028, S032. Medium.
4. Cut delivery cost through utilisation, not rider pay. "We are working towards bringing down the delivery cost per order while increasing delivery partners’ EPH (Earnings per Hour)" (S028) Sources: S028, S032. Medium.
5. Do not buy growth. "The business is growing because it's getting better, not because we're buying growth."  Sources: S018, S089. Medium. S089: "spending unnecessarily is a short-term market share gain tactic which doesn't really last and we don't want to chase that anymore" (S089)
6. Do not let sunk cost drive the decision. "not get bogged down with sunk cost fallacy." (S031) Sources: S031. Low.
7. Raise equity, not debt, and never miss payroll. "Never Raise Debt Always re equity" (S072) Sources: S072, S036. Medium.
8. Conserve cash when markets fall. "For now, we are being aggressive about conserving cash." (S028) Sources: S028, S031. Medium.
9. Stay paranoid; complacency is the risk. "We need to continue being paranoid about driving innovation and disruption, or someone else will." (S029) Sources: S029, S099, S102. High. S102: "I am paranoid about um failing" (S102)
10. Say no. "our negative list is always longer than our to-do list." (S031) Sources: S031, S036, S016. High.
11. Do not compete with partners. "we don't want to compete with restaurants they're our friends they're our partners" (S077) Sources: S031, S077. Medium.
12. Do not promise what you cannot see. "We never give predictions to investors." (S072) Sources: S072. Low.
14. Ship version one for function. "version one of the product needs to work and people usually don't care about how it looks as long it serves a specific purpose for them" (S077) Sources: S016, S071, S077. High.
15. Hire by putting people in tough spots. "You Put People in Tough Spots, You Ask Hard Questions and You See How They Actually Respond to those questions." (S072) Sources: S016, S072. Medium.
16. Fit roles to people. "But We Fit Roles to People That you are good at these things." (S072) Sources: S016, S072, S079. High.
17. Do not overpay for market talk. "I would never Overpay Talent Just Because They Want More Money." (S072) Sources: S072, S079. Medium.
18. Fix customer-affecting bugs overnight. "You can't let your customers down." (S072) Sources: S072. Low.
19. When the press is bad, show numbers or stay quiet. "If you are doing well you can stay quiet and share the numbers and that will change the sentiment." (S036) Sources: S036, S063, S079. High.
20. Early failures are mostly expansion too fast. "We were expanding in overseas and we were doing that very fast, which needed a lot of cash." (S036) Sources: S036, S063. Medium.
21. Competition should be healthy. "healthy competition makes you good" (S079) Sources: S016, S072, S079. High.
22. Use the platform to push the industry up. "even if like 20% restaurants end up doing this it sort of forces the other 80%" (S071) Sources: S071, S077. Medium.
23. Tell everyone everything inside. "You can shut yourself off to external noise, you can’t shut yourself off to internal noise." (S016) Sources: S016. Low.
24. Test the premise cheaply before building. "Let’s test it out, let’s hack some stuff up, it might not scale even to a 100 users." (S016) Sources: S016, S071, S077, S099. High.
25. A purpose beyond money. "the purpose cannot be let's make money right" (S077) Sources: S010, S077, S031 ("And I think money is a terrible motivator." S031); also "if you try to optimize for these things they don't actually happen" (S100), "if you're not building it for the money none of these things will bother you ever" (S085). High.
26. Build first, raise when money comes inbound. "do not build a business for investors" (S090) and "if you're spending 70% of your time looking for money when are you going to build build the organization" (S100). Only two of about 14 rounds began with a deck (S100). Sources: S090, S100, S085. High.
27. Never take money from someone you do not trust. "you are accepting money from this investor even when you don't trust them even when you don't like you always know it" (S088) Sources: S088, S090. Medium.
28. Pick your battles. "if you actually run away from a battle which is going to kill you, you might live to fight another day" (S090) Sources: S090, S016. Medium.
29. Tell the truth and filter the noise. "the first and the biggest principle that followers speak the truth" (S091) Also: "there will always be a camp which is not happy with what you do so you have to filter the the signal and the noise and continue doing your work" (S100) Sources: S091, S100. Medium.
30. Keep fixed costs low and experiments small. "we are very very conscious about fixed cost uh nowadays and since forever now" (S089) and "it's actually small team a couple of locations so it doesn't really move the needle for us overall in terms of cost" (S099) Sources: S089, S099, S102. Medium. S102: "if we don't try new things we'll never learn" (S102)
31. Take only risks whose downside you can manage. "right risk is something that you know the downside of and you know that if this downside happens there's a chance to it" (S102) Sources: S102. Low.
32. Give guidance as the worst case. "whenever we give any timelines it's sort of always the worst case scenario for us" (S102) Sources: S102, S072 (never predictions, inferred). Medium.
33. Let ratings set expectations for food quality. "if they're ordering from a 2.5 rated place they can't expect high quality food" (S102) Sources: S102, S099, S091. Medium.
34. Do not run the company by the stock price. "internally we don't talk about the stock price at all" (S102) Sources: S102, S063. Medium (S063: "Good or bad I don't read anything about Zomato.").
35. Do not call it competition until it is real, and do not say too early either. "too early to say is also a risky thing to say because then you can be lazy about it and stop watching it" (S112) Sources: S112, S115. Low (same interview).
36. When a problem is real, set a deadline and solve it, do not blame. "take an ambitious goal and take a gun to your head and solve it" (S112); "either you can start blaming" (S112) Sources: S112, S115. Low. Fits rule 1.
37. Stay in one country until it saturates. "my advice is to stay in India, chill" (S112); "we shut down international when India was hitting 93% of our revenue" (S112) Sources: S112, S115; consistent with S069 and S071 on India. Medium.
38. State a discount honestly as a flat amount. "discounting should be honest, it should be 80 rupees off, should not be 50% off up to 80" (S112) Sources: S112, S115. Low. He adds he would not change it if competition forces otherwise.
39. Grow leaders from within. "it's better to grow from within" (S112) Sources: S112, S115. Low. Outside hires only when sure; senior hiring takes six to twelve months.
40. Hire people who are not looking. "जैसे लोग हमें चाहिए होते हैं वो नौकरे ढूंडते नहीं हैं" [the people we want do not look for jobs] (S112) Sources: S112, S115. Low.
41. Culture is how people behave under pressure; mindset over experience. "I believe mindset is everything" (S112) Sources: S112, S115, S091. Medium.
42. Do not fight what you cannot change; stop telling yourself it bothers you. "i've just stopped telling myself that i'm bothered with it so i just moved on" (S113) Sources: S113 (and S014, S072, S079 on the stammer). Medium.
43. Do not let a label define you. "then you lock yourself into just that then you can't become anything more than just that" (S113) Sources: S113. Low.
44. Try to disprove your own idea before you back it. "let's try to disprove this i'm going to walk away in peace and say there is nothing to it" (S114) Sources: S114. Low.
45. Say a true finding publicly and invite others to test it. "if this is true it is my moral responsibility to make sure that the world knows this" (S114) Sources: S114. Low.
46. Marketing should leave a good taste, not any attention. "marketing should leave a good taste in people's mouth" (S112) Sources: S112, S115. Low.
