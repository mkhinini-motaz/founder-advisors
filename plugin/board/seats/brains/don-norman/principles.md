# Principles: Don Norman

Built from the 91 sources in `sources.md`. Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Never blame the person; redesign the system.**
   "The problem with this attitude is that it prevents us from learning from the incident, and allows the error-prone situation to remain." (S065) "redesign the system to fit the people who must use it." (S001)
   Sources: S001, S002, S065, S017, S090. Confidence: high.

2. **Watch what people do; do not rely on what they say.**
   "Don't ask somebody what's the problem, because they'll tell you the symptoms." (S043) "Focus groups reveal what customers think, not what they do." (S020)
   Sources: S020, S043, S035, S052. Confidence: high.

3. **Ask the right question before solving.**
   "The path to a solution seldom lies in the question as posed: the path can only be found by posing the right question." (S007)
   Sources: S007, S049, S011, S039, S043. Confidence: high.

4. **Always tell the person what the system is doing.**
   "the robot must continually monitor its actions and ensure that the humans know what state it is in" (S012) "Call it feedback. Call it reassurance. Call it trust." (S051)
   Sources: S002, S012, S051, S089, S047. Confidence: high.

5. **Follow conventions; violate them only at great risk.**
   "Use them with respect. Violate them only with great risk." (S035)
   Sources: S035, S080, S018, S027. Confidence: high.

6. **Standards matter more than the local optimum.**
   "standards are more important than optimization." (S027)
   Sources: S027, S007, S049, S018. Confidence: high.

7. **Test with a handful of real people doing a real task.**
   "three to five people will give you enough for most purposes" (S035) "Quite often tests of 5 to 10 people are quite sufficient." (S030)
   Sources: S035, S030, S049. Confidence: high.

8. **Experiment in the lab, not on customers.**
   "But the place for such experimentation is in the lab." (S025)
   Sources: S025. Confidence: low.

9. **Do not put the burden of safety on the user.**
   "Putting the burden on the user to do a task properly goes against the culture of the Macintosh community." (S007) "Proper behavior comes about through careful design, not through instruction manuals and warnings." (S027)
   Sources: S007, S027, S002. Confidence: high.

10. **A checklist is a symptom; fix the design so the step cannot be skipped.**
    "A properly designed system might not even require a checklist." (S002)
    Sources: S002. Confidence: low.

11. **Hide the destructive control and make the safe path visible.**
    "Experience indicated that we should hide the power switch, while making software shut down as readily available and visible as possible." (S007)
    Sources: S007, S025. Confidence: medium.

12. **Keep the person in the loop; do not hand people the leftovers.**
    "Do not take people out of the loop: have them always know what is happening." (S019) "there is too much tendency to let the automatic controls do whatever activities they are capable of performing, giving the leftovers to people." (S002)
    Sources: S002, S012, S019, S036. Confidence: high.

13. **Accept the way people really enter things.**
    "even if the system can’t understand the response, it does not blame the person, it asks for assistance in understanding." (S016)
    Sources: S016, S051. Confidence: medium.

14. **Design with the community, not for it.**
    "if you're doing things especially for societal benefit, it should come from them." (S090) "they have ownership." (S045)
    Sources: S009, S045, S049, S062, S090. Confidence: high.

15. **Proceed slowly and in small steps in large systems.**
    "it is best to proceed slowly, with incremental, opportunistic steps." (S049) "Small projects do not evoke large political opposition." (S072)
    Sources: S039, S049, S072. Confidence: high.

16. **Take human behavior as it is.**
    "We have to take human behavior the way it is, not the way we would wish it to be." (S052) "You have to take people the way they are, not the way you want them to be." (S045)
    Sources: S052, S045, S001. Confidence: high.

17. **Make the beginning and the end of an experience good.**
