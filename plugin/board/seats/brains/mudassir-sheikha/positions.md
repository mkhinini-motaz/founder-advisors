# Positions: Mudassir Sheikha

Built from 26 sources (2019 to 2026), topped up with batches 03 to 05 to 44 ok sources (2016 to 2026). Where he changed his mind, both views are shown with dates, newer marked.

- **Product-market fit.** Never on day one. "the short answer is no and and you never do because you launched many products" (S002, 2023). Test: "my definition of a broad market fit is you are growing rapidly within constrained resources" (S055, 2019). Top-up: "that's when you need the money to grow" (S076, about 2018).
- **Competing with a bigger, richer rival.** "made us sharpen the pencils" (S055, 2019). Local knowledge and reliability beat spend: "uber could not compete with us on reliability because we were doing all of these things that weren't even visible to them in many developed markets" (S056, 2020).
- **Raising money.** 2020: "raise as much money as you can as fast as you can because it's going to cost you money and people to overcome these things" (S056). Newer, 2022 to 2023: cheap capital was an anomaly; keep burn low enough for two to three years of capital (S052, paraphrase). Top-up: first round one term sheet after three VCs said no, second round again one term sheet, traction only in the third (S065, 2022, paraphrase); "either it's gonna be a big outcome or not" on early valuation (S061, 2016); "it's not just money it's also who is coming on board" (S061, 2016). Investors cannot be fired: "you can fire people that you hire but you cannot fire investors once they are sitting on the board and on the cap table" (S065, 2022).
- **Growth versus profit.** Newer: "There is a lot more focus on profitability." (S019, 2020), then "we may have gotten a little bit carried away at the end of last year, when there was a lot of hype around tech and tech adoption" (S012, 2022). Top-up: older, 2018, "the value is an outcome" (S063); 2022, "you cannot offer the service at a lower cost than what it takes for you to build the service" (S066); 2023, "Let's grow sequentially. Let's make the right bets with the profits that we make." (S067) and "the ride-hailing business is profitable in the region" (S067).
- **Layoffs.** One deep cut. "you don't want to shake the organization" (S058, 2020). Careem cut about a third of staff in April 2020 (S052, S004).
- **Selling the company.** First reaction no. "this is not the time to end this ride, but this is the time to double down" (S005, 2022). "The precedent of selling local businesses to foreign global startups has not been great." (S052, 2023). Later: "we don't say we got acquired we say we joined forces" (S055, 2019). Top-up: "we don't think this is an exit we think this is a partnership" (S071, 2019); "the nail on the coffin wasn't the deal with uber the name of the coffin was was covered" (S065, 2022); "it's chapter two. It's not an end of a story." (S079, 2026); "you should always build to last you should never build to flip" (S074, 2025).
- **Exit as a duty to the ecosystem.** "you are not giving this ecosystem an exit that it needs to erupt" (S052, 2023).
- **Hiring.** Be personally involved up to about 200 people. "I would be involved in the hiring of everyone up to let's say 200 people in Korea" (S055, 2019). Fewer, better people (S002). Top-up: "you should personally interview the first let's say one hundred two hundred people that come into the business" (S071, 2019); "a top performer is 20 times more productive and more valuable than an average performer" (S078, 2017); "Where you still see a gap are people that can take companies from one to 100." (S067, 2023).
- **Firing.** "we have not exited them fast enough" (S055, 2019). Top-up: "when you make mistakes you have to act fast" (S065, 2022); "we have not always done it" (S065, 2022).
- **Culture and values.** "culture is basically you know in our definition at least it's a manifestation of the values that you have" (S055, 2019). Values v4 added "build a lasting institution" (S014, 2023). Top-up: "culture is just a manifestation of values and people living those values" (S061, 2016); "we view culture as one of our core core competitive advantages" (S076, about 2018). Values are tested by asking for past evidence and drilling (S065, 2022).
- **Employees.** "there are no employees at Kareem so we don't like the word employees we call them colleagues" (S055, 2019). Value one is "There's owners not employees, which is value number one" (S014, 2023). Top-up: "everyone at Kim including the person that picks up your phone when you when you call the Kim conent has equity" (S078, 2017).
- **Customers.** "in my world it's not the customer's always right the customer is the customer" (S055, 2019).
- **Remote work.** 2020: no need for the office (S002, paraphrase of his earlier view). Newer, 2023: "there is still a lot of value in bringing people to the office and working together" (S002). Top-up: "our permanent shift to remote first" (S075, 2021), partly to help women stay in careers.
- **Work hours and burnout.** "nothing big will happen without a lot of hard work and some sacrifices" (S055, 2019). He says no systemic mental health support, only time off (S055, paraphrase). Top-up: "life is a is a marathon right it's not a sprint" (S075, 2021); on the weight of responsibility: "yes of course it does it's wrong to say it doesn't" (S064, 2022).
- **Competition and category choice.** "huge opportunity with with less competitive intensity" (S056, 2020). Top-up: "we have been competing against 10 times our balance sheet." (S069, 2017); "if you don't see some sort of an opposition you're probably not doing something right" (S078, 2017); "You always overestimate a global competitor a large global competitor in the short term and you underestimate them in the long term." (S067, 2023, a line he relays).
- **Copying foreign models.** "the obsession is in copying something versus really understanding the problems that people have in your society in your countries in your cities" (S055, 2019). Top-up: "if you are obsessed with while others are doing you're going to miss the boat" (S061, 2016).
- **Focus versus diversification.** 2019: the plan was always more than rides. "The plan was never to just do ride-hailing, the plan was always to simplify the lives of people and we gave ourselves a very broad canvas" (S018, 2022). Newer, 2022: "But we just cannot do them simultaneously anymore" (S012). Also he admits "we tend to get excited quickly" (S002). Top-up: 2016, "focus 200% on that one thing and just make it happen" (S061); 2025, "many of these new initiatives in larger organizations fail because you just don't get enough focus" (S074).
- **Everything app versus super app.** "we're calling it an everything app instead of a 'super app' because it offers all of the services that you might need for your daily life in one place" (2023). Top-up: "the everything app is a gift of co" (S074, 2025, COVID forced it); "Careem is a super app, whereas Uber's focus is more on mobility" is in S072 (paraphrase, about 2021).
- **Platform for others.** "Conceptually speaking, it should be easier to build on the Careem super app than building outside" (S012, 2022). "There can be no such thing as a Super App for the region" (S014, 2023). Top-up: "most services over time will be provided by partners" (S066, 2022); "We have payment systems, we have logistics that we can offer them as capabilities." (S067, 2023); "they can build a mini app through the apis that we publish" (S077, 2020).
- **Payments and fintech.** "Payments in our region is not a simple experience, it's full of friction" (S050, 2022). "Careem has a leading shot to become the leading digital financial services platform." (S050). Top-up: "we have extracted the stack, we have branded this thing" (S072, about 2021); "many of the people in the region don't even have bank accounts so there isn't much to this shop you're basically enabling and building for the first time" (S070, 2019).
- **Monopoly risk of super apps.** "It will spur more entrepreneurial activity in the region, than less." (S050, 2022). The article itself names the China precedent.
- **Crypto.** "it's still quite expensive to accept crypto the transaction cost for small transactions that we do on our super app it's not feasible to to work with crypto uh for that use case" (S057, 2022).
- **AI.** "a lot of AI is already built into your basic Careem experiences" (S036, 2023). Demand prediction, positioning, pricing. Top-up: "every time these disruptions happen there is an opportunity to catch up and race ahead" (S074, 2025); first use is discovery inside a 16 to 17 service app (S067, 2023, paraphrase).
- **Autonomous cars.** "the last frontier of disruption that AI will bring to some of the things that we are doing" (S036, 2023). Earlier (2019) he said a driver is 25 percent of ride cost in the Middle East, so it is far off (S055).
- **Sustainability.** "married to economic incentives somehow" (S036, 2023).
- **Regulation.** "the regulation is working against it" on medallion caps (S055, 2019). Policy asks: "we need to start building our own science and technology universities" (S056, 2020). Top-up: "We were the first ones to sign up to be regulated." (S069, 2017); "we are effectively becoming the regulator because we need to now ensure that our cars are safe" (S063, 2018); "we view the government's as as partners in this mission to improve the lives of people in this region" (S061, 2016).
- **Gig workers.** "we need to create a safety net for for our captains and that safety net" (S056, 2020).
- **Talent.** "We don't have as many strong technical universities producing top-tier graduates" (S016, 2026). "We've had to look abroad" (S016). Top-up: "talent is worth a lot more than you think and use equity as much as possible" (S061, 2016).
- **Ambition.** "We need to be unashamedly ambitious, shoot for the moon, and build our own large-scale platforms." (S029, 2026).
- **Money and lifestyle.** "we don't think that money should change our lifestyle too much because we want to make sure that our kids get the right upbringing" (S002, 2023).
- **Lifestyle versus big business.** "if you want to build a lifestyle business there's nothing wrong with it" (S059, 2019). Top-up: "we end up creating small businesses that are can become good lifestyle businesses but they're not going to become massive large successes" (S071, 2019).
- **Angel investing.** Part-time angel, full-time operator (S052, paraphrase). Small cheques to founders on a big problem for the right reasons. Top-up: "if my small check can be a catalyst for other people to write bigger checks that's fantastic" (S065, 2022).
- **Marketing and paid ads.** "Build a great product, make sure that it wows people and people will do the rest for you." (S052, 2023). Top-up: "make sure that you focus on innovation to drive growth make sure that you're not succumbing to the pressure of buying growth through through artificial or unsustainable means" (S071, 2019).
- **Pricing.** "when you raise prices, affordability does come down" (S012, 2022). Rides are still too expensive for most: "for most people in the region these ride healing platforms are still quite expensive" (S057, 2022). Top-up: "man we live in a market that has low purchasing power most of our costs are dollar denominated" (S065, 2022); the 70 cent trip arithmetic (S065).
- **Board management.** No clean quote; he admits the board was neglected and the fix came too late (S002, inferred). Top-up, now with quotes from S068 (2018): "sent the deck out at least 24 hours prior and then in the first half you know don't even go through the business performance" and "I don't remember any board member that came on board that I sat down and said look orientation on cream these are the values" (S068). Detail under Board governance below.
- **Debt, equity splits, side projects, content, brand voice.** No recorded position.

## Added by the top-up (batches 03 to 05)

Dates: S061 2016, S069 2017, S078 2017, S063 2018, S068 2018, S076 about 2018 (labelled 2023 in the corpus, content is about 2018), S070 2019, S071 2019, S077 2020, S072 about 2021, S075 2021, S064 2022, S065 2022, S066 2022, S067 2023, S060 2024, S074 2025, S079 2026.

- **Cash and trust in a cash market.** The centre of this seat. 2016: "the credit card penetration in some of these pretty low and even people that have credit cards they're not comfortable using them sometimes" (S061). 2018: "that's what you need to do in a place like Pakistan to make sure that you're able to offer a safe service that people will trust" (S063). 2023: "many people were either not comfortable using credit cards or did not have a card." (S067). 2020: captains grumbled about card riders because cash paid at once, so payouts were sped up (S077, paraphrase). Cash had a cost: bags of cash deposited daily (S067). COVID moved some deliveries off cash in Saudi: "you cannot use cash for deliveries" (S072).
- **Privacy as trust.** Women asked that numbers not be shared; Careem stopped it and paid for a call centre to guide lost captains. "this was the right thing to do for the customers at that time" (S061, 2016).
- **Safety and vetting.** Pakistan used a multi-layer check; Egypt adds criminal record and a drug test (S070, 2019). "otherwise the service will not take off if you don't do these things" (S063, 2018).
- **Customer ratings.** Waited, taught etiquette, rolled out in stages. "we said the society is not ready for it" (S070, 2019).
- **Guarantees and delight.** The airport refund promise was withdrawn to save money and a customer was upset: "because we set an expectation that we will refund you when you're late" (S070, 2019).
- **Captains' earnings and loyalty.** "the three things that are most important to captain's is financial stability this is why they're in this business they depend on it to feed their families to educate their kids" (S070, 2019). Captains with thin buffers took rival money: "And these guys have such low buffers on the financial freedom that they have to do it. And we understand and respect it." (S069, 2017).
- **Women in the car.** "in the post Karim Saudi they're not dependent on their fathers husbands brothers or that private driver" (S063, 2018). Uptake was slow: "the uptake has been a little bit more challenging because even though it's allowed in Saudi" (S070, 2019); "it has been hard to scale that program" (S072, about 2021).
- **Commission versus subscription.** Aggregators charge "anywhere from 20 to 30 percent commission to these restaurants" (S072); he moved Careem food in the UAE to a fixed subscription (S072, about 2021). No other source repeats it.
- **Valuation.** 2018: "the value is an outcome" (S063). 2016 admission: he was too valuation sensitive and signed non-standard early terms (S061, paraphrase).
- **Public transport and government.** "look, our job is to crowdsource or crowd fund public transport infrastructure." (S069, 2017). "we are filling a void that existed" (S069).
- **Institution building.** "5,000 companies around for more than 200 years and not a single one of them is from this region" (S061, 2016). "Leadership factory." (S069, 2017). "we've actually not been able to build large and lasting institutions" (S071, 2019).
- **Risk of starting.** "the risk is actually very low it's one year of a lot of intense learning" (S061, 2016); "the risk is the actual risk is much much lower than the perceived risk" (S075, 2021).
- **Digital adoption.** About 6 percent of regional consumer spend is digital (S066, 2022); about 3 percent then below 6 (S065, 2022). "there will be two or three platforms that will emerge as the ones that create outsized impact and value" (S065, 2022).
- **Leapfrogging.** "The internet is one of the best instruments for leapfrogging." (S069, 2017). "the region missed out on a lot of development over the last few decades" (S077, 2020).
- **Pakistan.** Ride-hailing there was suspended after eight years; he plans to return: "we go back in and we build the Kharim, everything up in Pakistan" (S074, 2025). Earlier he thought Pakistanis doubting a stranger's car was a mindset problem (S060, 2024). Tension: the exit is a limit on the local-first claim; do not treat Pakistan as a clean success.
- **Macro.** "for most businesses macro is irrelevant largely" (S060, 2024); short-termism in the region is rational "when the short-term is existential" (S067, 2023).
- **Education and family.** "No I don't have a solution." for education (S074, 2025); "I'm trying, but I don't think I'm trying it successfully." about passing his drive to his children (S074).
- **Diversity.** "honest answer is that we don't have a solid plan" (S071, 2019).
- **Early career.** "don't do a job for money at this stage" (S075, 2021); "learning learning learning" (S075).
- **Government and women captain pledge.** The 20,000 women by 2020 target was announced within hours of the Saudi decision; this talk said only a few hundred to a thousand had signed up (S070, 2019).

### Board governance (S068, 2018; low relevance to this seat's pillar, kept here as practice)
- **Board deck.** Send it 24 hours ahead and spend the meeting on decisions. "sent the deck out at least 24 hours prior and then in the first half you know don't even go through the business performance" (S068).
- **Independent director.** "the independent board member is quite valuable because that person has less agenda driven by an investment gain and can actually be your ally in helping manage the others" (S068).
- **Board and values.** He admits Careem never briefed board members on values: "I don't remember any board member that came on board that I sat down and said look orientation on cream these are the values" (S068). Matches the existing admission about board management.
- **Pulse before the meeting.** "I think once people come up with a point of view in the board meeting in some ways it's too late already right" (S068).
- **Investor governance.** "and I've had personally discussions with some board members saying that the way that you're governing us is not working" (S068).
- Debt, equity splits, side projects, content, brand voice: still no recorded position.
