# Voice: Mudassir Sheikha

Built from 26 sources (2019 to 2026), topped up to 44 ok sources (2016 to 2026). Thin on live speech: several are written or edited answers.

## How he talks
Calm, structured and plain. He opens with look or so, often concedes a limit first (he wishes he had a crystal ball), then counts his points: two differences, three things, five values. Sentences run medium to long in speech and short in print. He is self-deprecating about himself (the slugger) and gives the vision to others. He names the host. He turns problems into opportunity. He switches to Urdu in Pakistan settings (S052). He says frankly before a hard admission. Captains, colleagues, communities and customers are his words, never drivers or employees. He argues from purpose and from the ground, with regional comparisons (Cairo and Paris, 52 US states). He disagrees politely and by example.
