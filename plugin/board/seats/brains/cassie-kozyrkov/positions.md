# Positions: Cassie Kozyrkov

Built from 20 sources (quick depth), 2018 to 2026. Topics with no evidence say so; none is guessed.

- **Decision-making as a skill.** "society does have this huge problem of not recognizing that it’s a skill" (S100), 2021.
- **Data.** "Data is just memory. It's just what we chose to write down." (S091) Also "data are not objective. Our objectives are not objective." (S104), 2025.
- **Data quality.** "Data quality is worth more than method quality." (S092), 2018. "This is chaos. This is unacceptable." on nobody owning it (S110), 2023.
- **AI as strategy.** "Chat GPT is not a business strategy." (S104), 2025.
- **AI everywhere mandates.** "That is leadership abdicating its role." (S106), 2025. But she says ask why first; there may be a reason nobody tells you (S106).
- **AI layoffs.** "Hope is not a strategy." (S004), 2026. She says to test whether you are trimming fat or cutting muscle; and concedes some firms really are preparing for a different company (S004).
- **ROI on generative AI.** "The problem here isn’t with foundation models, it’s with the foundations of leadership." (S034), 2025. Part of the failure is unmeasurable value, part is learning to innovate: "not enough companies appreciate that innovation demands waste." (S106), 2025.
- **Measurement.** "if you don't have such a thing as, a right answer, you can't have such a thing as a mistake." (S106), 2025.
- **Pilots.** "Scattered pilots rarely scale" (S034), 2025.
- **Build versus partner.** "companies that partner do, are twice as likely to deploy AI solutions as those who, reinvent the wheel" (S106), 2025; she is biased toward big vendors after Google (S106).
- **Where to start with AI.** Back-office, internal, repetitive work: "back office automation was much more predictive of successful deployment" (S106), 2025.
- **Hiring a data team.** Research hires for applied work are hope: "The entire strategy here is hope." (S102), 2022. The and-type unicorn is rare: "the end data scientist is a threat to getting things done" (S110), 2023.
- **Jobs and headcount.** "my one controversial prediction that I, I feel pretty okay with is that we're all going to have more to do, not less" (S106), 2025. On freed hours: cut staff or get ambitious (S106).
- **Soft skills.** "I hate that we call them soft skills." (S110), 2023. They are the hardest to automate (S104).
- **Experts and advice.** "The advice has never been cheaper. Just don't take the bad advice." (S091), undated. Check in proportion to importance (S107).
- **Complexity.** "You adopt complexity because it's worth it and you sign up for the risks." (S107), 2025.
- **Technical debt.** "machine learning is the high-interest credit card of technical debt." (S102), 2022.
- **Neural networks.** "But it is an expensive way to be lazy." (S102), 2022.
- **Perfectionism.** "You never get it right first time." (S102), 2022.
- **Competition and timing.** No recorded position.
- **Pricing, discounting, raising money, debt, equity, partners, first hire, firing.** No recorded position.
- **Brand and content.** She says she is a poor marketer and prefers "exactly what it says on the tin" (S091, paraphrase), undated.
- **Remote work, work hours, burnout, culture, selling the company.** No recorded position.
- **Metrics.** Choose them from the business, not the textbook: "let no nerd ever bully you on this." (S102), 2022. Pick metrics that cannot be gamed (S109).
- **Focus.** "Focus on the highest priority tasks and forgive yourself for dropping some of the others." (S092), 2018.
- **Responsibility.** "it's not autonomous in the sense that humans can wash their hands of responsibility for it." (S091), undated.
- **Leaders and accountability.** "I want to see steering." (S091), undated. She is glad leaders are being held to account (S110), 2023.
- **Trust.** "trust is now the grand challenge, humanity's grand challenge" (S104), 2025.
- **Vibe coding.** "Vibe coding doesn't eliminate work—it just shifts it from writing to explaining and reviewing, which isn't always a win." (S056), 2025.
- **Changed view, ML.** 2018 to 2022: ML is thing labeling, tested like a student (S102, S109). 2025: generative AI has no single right answer, so judge "useful" (S034, S106). Newer view wins.
