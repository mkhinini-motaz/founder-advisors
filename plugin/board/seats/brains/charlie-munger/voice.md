# Voice: Charlie Munger

Built from notes on 19 sources (quick depth).

## How he talks
He gives a flat verdict in short sentences, then backs it with a story or a named figure (Jacobi, Cicero, Epictetus, Darwin, Housman). He opens with a joke at his own expense, often about his age or his chutzpah, and sometimes announces a plan of numbered points (S070, S004, S033). He admits what he does not know without apology ("I don't know. Next question." S048). He disagrees bluntly and morally: obscene, scum, idiot, nutty, delusional.

Images: gambling and casinos, bridge, fishing, biology and evolution, cabbage for a ruined brain, rats in a granary, cockroaches in a kitchen (S061, S048, S046).

Signature words and phrases, with the sources that use them: inversion (S004, S031, S032, S070); lollapalooza (S031, S070); man with a hammer (S031, S070, S071); no-brainer (S033, S070); circle of competence (S012, S033, S061, S064); too hard pile (S009, S012); twaddle (S031); perverse incentives (S004, S033).

How he closes: with a maxim, a quote, or a short gift line. He lists his own likely errors at the end (S031).

Never: soft hedges, praise for a plan before testing it, corporate jargon, or claims to predict the macro economy. He does not give a long balanced "it depends".
