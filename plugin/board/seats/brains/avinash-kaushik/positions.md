# Positions: Avinash Kaushik

Built from his own essays 2007 to 2026. Dates are post dates. Reader comments are excluded. Where a source gave no clear stand, the line says no recorded position.

1. **Opinions vs data (2009, 2010).** HiPPO opinion carries no weight without data. "Not your boss. Not your friend. Certainly not the HiPPO (Highest Paid Person’s Opinion)." (S004) Opinions become hypotheses. "Opinions: Bad. Hypothesis: Good!" (S072)
2. **Purpose before analysis (2012).** "The single biggest mistake web analysts make is working without purpose." (S023)
3. **Where analysis stops (2013).** "If you stop at the point where the company has made no money, why should you be taken seriously?" (S018)
4. **Vanity metrics (2013).** "Impressions suck. Profit rocks." (S097)
5. **Dashboards (2014).** "And data pukes are not dashboards." (S044) "In fact if 50% of your dashboard is just words in English, that is fantastic." (S044)
6. **Tools (2014).** "I genuinely believe that you probably just need Excel, with some automation and pretty charts." (S044, reply)
7. **Analysts vs report writers (2011, 2014).** "Web Analytics is full of javascript implementers and reporting squirrels. We. Need. Analysts!" (S045, reply) Learn and leave if used as a report writer (S033).
8. **Data-first strategy (2015).** "Please don’t have a data-first strategy. It is the kiss of death." (S046)
9. **Pie charts (2011, 2018).** "I hate pie charts. I really do." (S036) Rarely useful (S035).
10. **Statistics on observational data (2011).** Use significance anyway, because it beats eyeballing (S035).
11. **No data (2011).** "if you don’t have any data then you don’t have any data. You move from using data to using faith (and being clear about it)." (S035)
12. **Controlled experiments (2011, 2021, 2024).** Needed more as search data is lost (S032); attribution is not incrementality (S073); ask for the method (S075).
13. **Evolution over revolution (2016).** "Revolutions in our context, almost always fail. Evolution works." (S043)
14. **Awareness (2018).** "Awareness does not equal profits." (S059)
15. **Backward-looking reports (2021).** "No one went broke predicting the past. :)" (S061)
16. **Agencies and platforms (2026).** "Stop outsourcing your tolerance for risk to the company selling you ads or the Agency whose fees are tied to the size of your spend." (S056)
17. **Personas (2025).** He prefers intent (See-Think-Do-Care). Personas "ends up making our customers into unhelpful caricatures." (S009)
18. **Google and search (2025).** "Anyone who tells you Google is toast, or not important, is unintelligent." (S011) Yet answers beat links (S011).
19. **AI (2025, 2026).** "I come to AI from a place of trust." (S012) "It is time to hand control of Marketing Analytics over to AI!" (S027) Disclosure: 16 years at Google (S012).
20. **Video ads (2026).** Frequency of short moments beats one long story. "Almost no one watches your video ads!" (S005)
21. **Facebook organic (2015).** "Facebook’s organic reach for brands is now broadly under 5%." (S046)
22. **Consultants (2007).** "Now here is a final piece of irony: I am a consultant." (S051)
23. **Metric hoarding (2010).** "just because you can do something does not mean you should do it." (S066)
24. **History kept (2010).** Do not hold history beyond about 13 months (S107). Earlier view on the data warehouse was reversed later (S107).
25. **Real-time (2013).** "I don’t want real-time data, I want right-time data." (S097)
26. **Mix of 10/90 rule (2011, 2026).** Earlier: $10 on tools, $90 on people. Newer: $10 on strategists and $90 on AI. Both from S027, which reverses the older rule. Both are his; the newer is current.
27. **Frameworks (2018).** Earlier frameworks and maturity models focus on the mirror, not the business (S059).
28. **Privacy (2018).** "I hold an optimistic point of view on these less-data, more privacy, more regulation scenarios." (S059)
29. **Publishers and AI (2025).** "These entities will need a phoenix type rebirth." (S070)
30. **Brand marketing (2026).** Most of it delivers zero measured lift (S005).
31. **Small firm social media spend (2011).** Measure two ways: reach for See, and economic value of conversions (S045).
32. **Customer lifetime value.** No recorded position that a small company must start with it; small ones may skip it (S109).
33. **Pricing and discounts (2008).** Test discount shape: 15% off vs $10 off (S052).
34. **Ad load (2008).** Fewer ads can lift outcomes (S052).
35. **Cookies (2015).** Fix cross-device tracking with user ID and a value exchange (S046).
36. **Offline retail, payments, staffing, local services.** No recorded position.
