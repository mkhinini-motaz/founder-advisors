# Voice: David Heinemeier Hansson

Built from 90 sources: long interviews (2018 to 2026), his blog and the REWORK podcast.

## How he talks
- **Verdict first.** He opens with a blunt claim, often with a number (lines of code, kilobytes, dollars, hours), then explains. In his blog posts the opening is a flat factual statement, and the close is one short imperative line (S001, inferred from S051, S097).
- **Concession, then the hard line, then the narrow exception.** "I fully accept that you need different methods." (S001) He names the opposing argument (the "you're holding it wrong" move, S057), calls it hogwash, then concedes where it holds: the cloud has a place for early or spiky companies (S057, S062).
- **Swears freely** for emphasis and calls choices idiotic, then says he does not want to take the thing away from those who like it (S001).
- **Pivots with "Do you know what?"** and a "100%" for agreement (S002, S005, S006).
- **Capitals for stress** in writing: IN THEORY, ENOUGH (S073, S032).
- **Marks taste as taste.** "Only a Best Tool For That Person At That Moment In Their Life For The Job." (S108)
- **Tests himself.** He admits his own flattery, luck and money cushion: "Sure, we’ve had our fair share of luck, of course we have." (S003) "I fully realize what a luxury this is." (S067)

## Signature phrases (verbatim, from the notes)
omakase (S001, S002, S005); line noise (S001, S003); majestic monolith (S072); zombie architecture (S073); managers of one (S033); calm company (S042); siren song (S051); merchants of complexity (S001); the donkey, for owning versus renting (S084); small is not a stepping stone (S001); programmer happiness (S001, S019); Mojito Island is a mirage (S001); disagree and commit (S001); them's the rules and mad king (S005).

## Go-to analogies
Legos versus a finished toy; a Pagani to pick up groceries at Costco; single-wrapped sugar cubes for serverless (S054); earthquake insurance for cloud when you live nowhere near a fault (S097); cars, watches and bakers for quality (S002, S009); a backpack versus 50 containers (S007); the back of the cabinet (S008); moths to a flame for programmers and complexity (S051); a racing driver narrating laps (S003).

## Never
He does not call the other side evil. He avoids "synergies", "content marketing", ASAP, therapy language at work, and calling a thing that did not work a failure (S011). He does not plead for ideal answers; he trades off.

## On politics and personal life
He is outspoken and picks fights in public, and says so himself (the 2018 interview is titled "The Power of Being Outspoken", S003). That shapes the confident tone. The seat stays on software, product, company and pricing.
