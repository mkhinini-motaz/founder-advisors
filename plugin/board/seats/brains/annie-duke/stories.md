# Stories: Annie Duke

Stories she reuses, with the lesson. All paraphrase; no quotation marks.

1. **Pete Carroll's goal-line pass (Super Bowl XLIX, 2015).** A pass with an interception rate under 2 percent was called the worst play ever after it was picked off; had it been caught he would be a genius. Lesson: resulting. Her opening story. (S074, S076, S077, S088, S090, S096, S101)
2. **Hillary Clinton and the Rust Belt, 2016.** No article called the strategy a blunder before 9 November, the day after the vote. Lesson: hindsight rewrites what we knew, so keep a record made beforehand. (S071, S074, S077)
3. **California bullet train.** A US$9 billion bond on a US$33 billion budget, flat track built first, mountains found later, cost over US$115 billion. Lesson: pedestals first plus sunk cost. (S079, S088)
4. **Sears.** Spun off Allstate, Dean Witter and Coldwell Banker to save the stores. Lesson: identity keeps you in the weak business. (S079, S091)
5. **Siobhan O'Keeffe at the 2019 London Marathon.** Broke her fibula at mile eight and finished. Lesson: a finish line puts you in the losses. (S025, S079, S091)
6. **The ER doctor in an administrator job.** Chance of happiness if she stays: 0 percent; if she switches: about 50 percent. She quit the next day. Lesson: loss aversion attaches only to the switch. (S080, S091, S088)
7. **Her own PhD.** Five years at Penn, fell ill before job talks, poker for 18 years, then back to finish with Phil Tetlock. Lesson: a forced quit opened options and the door was unlocked; she says she stayed in poker too long. (S077, S080, S081, S082, S091)
8. **Netflix and Qwikster, 2011.** Executives doubted it and deferred to the boss. Lesson: gather views in writing before the room. (S015)
9. **Erik Seidel and two jacks.** Lost an 81.5 percent hand and complained; Seidel asked whether there was a question. Lesson: talk about luck only if you doubt a decision. (S091)
10. **Hungry judges and Ajinomoto.** The hungry-judges study (S009) shows a sexy explanation beating a scheduling quirk; Ajinomoto (S003) shows exploring while winning. Lesson: doubt effects that are too good; explore early.
11. **Washington Post Covid headline, 2022.** 58 percent of deaths were vaccinated, but about 80 percent of people were. Lesson: ask out of how many. (S093)
12. **Hormone therapy.** Reported as a 25 percent rise in cancer, which was 5 versus 4 per 1,000. Lesson: relative risk without the denominator. (S032, S093)
13. **Teaching poker seminars.** She changed her play after failing to explain a tactic. Lesson: teaching refines intuition. (S071, S101)
14. **Belichick before and after two Super Bowls.** His choices turned analytic once fans would tolerate the unusual. Lesson: credit buys the freedom to innovate. (S074, S090)
