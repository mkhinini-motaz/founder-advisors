# Positions: Seth Godin

His stance on common founder questions, from 32 sources (2014 to 2026). Dates are the source dates. Where he changed his mind, both views are shown, newer last. No recorded position means the corpus has none.

## Pricing
- Price is a signal of who it is for; do not discount to be affordable to everyone. "Price is a story. It is not an absolute number." (S001, 2018) Exception: housing for low-income people and mass healthcare should be cheap.
- Charge for worth, not cost. "It's more useful to price things based on what they're worth to those that might want to buy them." (S037, 2021)
- Newest: "“You’ll pay a bit more but you’ll get more than you paid for” is almost always a winning market position." (S044, 2026)

## Discounting
- Not a growth tool. "You don't need a squeeze page. You don't need an urgency. You don't need a fire sale." (S016, 2018) Cutting price for new customers does not help (S006, 2025).

## Raising money
- Funding is not success. "Too often we give entrepreneurs credit for raising a lot of money from venture capital." (S006, 2025) Funding source decides the company: "if you raise money from dentists in Iowa, you could build a different company." (S006). Not against money: size the log to the kindling.

## Debt
- No recorded position.

## First hire
- Freelancers hire themselves as the cheapest labor. "You keep hiring yourself to do the jobs." (S010, 2026) Hire by paying for a small project (S004, S006).

## Hiring
- Credentials and interviews are false proxies. "being good at interviewing is a false proxy." (S004, 2023) Hire for attitude, train for expertise (S038, 2022). He says he was arrogant and thought he was good at hiring (S006).

## Firing
- Fire the bully, even the high performer. "If I let you do that, I have made a statement about what it’s like around here." (S006, 2025) Fire lousy clients (S001, S019).

## Partners
- Work with people who keep promises. "We need to work with people who will keep their promises." (S017, 2023)

## Equity
- Equity and option tickets are weak alignment. "If we actually want alignment, perhaps we could write down precisely what success looks like." (S042, 2025)

## Focus versus diversification
- Focus works. "Focus works. A sharp edge cuts through the clutter." (S031, 2017) Greed drops the basket (S001).

## Side projects
- Do not let a business ruin a hobby. "Don’t let a business ruin your hobby." (S010, 2026) Earlier, the reverse: "When you turn your hobby into a business, you ruin both of them." (S011, 2026).

## Niche
- Always narrow. "if your motto is, you can pick anyone and I’m anyone, then you’re doomed." (S010, 2026) False niches are excuses (S001). Newest: "The mass market is powerful, but it’s only slightly interested." (S044, 2026)

## Content
- Show up daily, but only where it gets you what you set out to get. "The goal is to achieve what you set out to achieve." (S013, 2025) Short pieces only open the door (S014). He does not use TikTok.

## Paid ads
- Direct response is not how brands are built. "it's essential that you reject direct-marketing tactics as a shortcut." (S029, 2015) Advertising people would not miss is doomed (S028).

## Brand
- A brand is a promise, not a logo. "that brand is the promise we make" (S010, 2026) The founder should usually not be the face: "It should be about the customer." (S010)

## AI
- Early view 2023: it will blow up work. "AI is going to blow this up so much that we won't even recognize it." (S017, 2023) Newer 2025: use it daily, never to write for him. "Claude is one of my closest compatriots." (S013, 2025) Average work will be replaced. "get AI to work for you or be prepared to work for AI." (S006, 2025)
- Newest 2026: answers are cheap, questions are dear. "In an age of ubiquitous AI, answers are no longer in short supply. But questions are more valuable than ever." (S044, 2026)

## Remote work
- Favours asynchronous work. "a meeting is a delivery of information for the convenience of the person who called it." (S004, 2023) Connection online still needs enrollment (S037).

## Work hours
- Clear the day of meetings and feeds. "if you can figure out how to clear out six hours of day for your life, that’s an enormous ROI." (S001, 2018)

## Burnout
- Stop the pattern before it breaks you (R.E.M. toured less and swapped instruments, S003). Make rules that let you ship and rest (S005). No recorded position on burnout beyond this.

## Selling the company
- "Going public isn’t the purpose. Selling your company isn’t the purpose." (S002, 2018) Public ownership pressures decisions: "When everyone who works for you and around you is watching the stock price, it's hard to decide to do the right thing." (S036, 2021)

## Culture
- Make promises and keep them, not "family" talk. "What are our promises? And let's keep them." (S017, 2023) Everyone on the bus should want to go to Cleveland, and turnover shows clarity (S017).

## Metrics
- Ignore vanity numbers. "So, the first step is to ignore the useless metrics." (S012, 2018) Best signal for a startup: "Customer traction is the single best indicator of whether a startup is going to succeed" (S011, 2026).

## Competition
- Pick your competitors. "when you pick your competitors, you pick your future." (S006, 2025) Refer out. "That generosity of being specific pays all these enormous dividends." (S010)

## Timing
- Trust takes years, not a launch. "the way I did it was I showed up every day for 10 years." (S016, 2018) People show up when they are ready (S035). Scale is a late signal (S042).

## Growth
- He declined growth himself. "veering away from the next two zeros of upside is really expensive and the single best way for me to live the best life I want to live." (S003, 2020) A bigger company is only a different kind of status (S011).

## Customer service and apology
- An apology needs compassion and contrition (S028, 2014). "When in doubt, be human." (S028)

## Marketing ethics
- Serve, never trick. "marketing is the act of serving. It’s something we do with people and for them, not to them or at them" (S016, 2018) Placebos and stories are fine when they work and do no harm (S001, S028).

## Research
- Overrated for great marketing. "Research is overrated, because Jackson Pollock didn’t do any research." (S016, 2018) Yet test with a few people (S002, S019).

## Scarcity
- Scarcity creates value, and ethical scarcity is a choice. "There's only one printing, and when the book is gone, that's all there is." (S030, 2016) Allocate it by need or fit, not first come first served (S037, S039).

## Platforms and lock-in
- Cautious. Networks decay once locked in; advertising speeds it (S006, S011). "Amazon has lost their way about who they’re serving." (S011, 2026)

## Purpose of a company
- Rejects Friedman. "Because we don’t have a profit shortage, we have a meaning shortage." (S004, 2023) Newer: "The purpose of culture isn't to enable capitalism. The purpose of capitalism is to enable culture" (S013, 2025).

## Money and wealth
- "I'm not, down on money. I think money's fine." (S015, 2010) Also: serving abundance probably does not build a big public company (S019).

## Remote tribes and community
- Community keeps ideas alive. A book is the pit and the community the fruit (S006). Charge something so people show up (S006).

## Anxiety and fear
- "Fear isn’t stupid." (S002, 2018) It never goes away; dance with it (S010, S011).
