# Frameworks: Seth Godin

Built from 32 sources (Ferriss, Robbins, Bartlett, Cowen, Motley Fool, Mixergy, On Being interviews and 17 months of Seth's Blog, 2014 to 2026). Most-used first. "Scoring" lines are guidance for the council, not his words.

## 1. Smallest viable audience
- What: pick the few people you can delight so much that they come back and tell others. Not "too small": the smallest group that starts the conversation that lets you grow. Mass and average are the same thing.
- Parts: choose the people first; say what they believe and want; delight them; forgive everyone else; send misfits to a competitor.
- When: any launch, pricing, channel or feature call.
- Scoring (guidance): can you name the group in one sentence, and would they miss you? If the answer is "anyone", score it 0. If you can list 10 real names, score it high.
- Quote: "If I can find that group, the smallest viable audience, and delight them, they will engage with me, and they will tell the others." (S001)
- Quote: "That’s all you need. You pick that group, you delight them, and you forgive everybody else." (S006)
- Used by: S001, S006, S010, S011, S016, S019, S029, S035, S036, S039, S040, S042, S043, S044 (14 sources; S029 credits Brian Clark for the "minimum viable audience" term).

## 2. Who is it for, and what is it for?
- What: two questions asked of any product, meeting, ad or hire. If you cannot answer both specifically, start again.
- When: first, before tactics.
- Scoring (guidance): a specific who plus a specific change passes; "everyone" or "growth" fails.
- Quote: "this work I’m doing, who’s it for and what’s it for?" (S010)
- Quote: "it really helps to understand who’s it for and what’s it for. What is the change we are trying to make?" (S012)
- Used by: S004, S010, S011, S012, S040, S044 (6 sources).

## 3. Would they miss you? (permission and traction test)
- What: the test of an earned relationship. A startup's real assets are customer traction, permission, distribution, network effect and the smallest viable audience.
- Parts: ask whether more people would miss you each day; yes means permission, no means spam.
- Scoring (guidance): count customers who would miss you. He says a pizza place needs about 100.
- Quote: "How many customers would miss you if you shut down tomorrow?" (S011)
- Quote: "If the answer is no, then you're a spammer, and if the answer is yes, you've earned permission." (S014)
- Used by: S011, S013, S014, S032, S039 (4 independent pieces of evidence; S013 and S014 are one interview).

## 4. Race to the top, not the bottom
- What: an organization raises its numbers by creating more value or by doing just enough for less. Public-company entropy pulls to the second. Pick the first on purpose.
- Parts: charge more, promise more, keep it; "you might win" the race to the bottom.
- Scoring (guidance): can you name what the customer gets that costs more and is worth more? If your only edge is price, fail.
- Quote: "The problem with the race to the bottom is that you might win." (S028)
- Quote: "Race to the top or race to the bottom, it's a choice." (S031)
- Used by: S004, S006, S028, S031, S033, S034, S035, S044 (8 sources).

## 5. Brand is a promise
- What: a brand is the promise made and the expectation it creates, shown by people paying extra and giving the benefit of the doubt. A logo is not a brand.
- Quote: "Hyatt has a logo, but Nike has a brand." (S013)
- Quote: "your brand is the promise that you make implicitly or explicitly." (S002)
- Scoring (guidance): do customers pay extra or forgive a slip? If not, there is no brand yet.
- Used by: S002, S010, S013, S014, S044 (4 independent).

## 6. Pick your customers, pick your future
- What: your clients decide what your days look like. A freelancer moves up only by getting better clients, and a business by choosing its customers and even its competitors.
- Quote: "When you pick your customers, you pick your future." (S006)
- Quote: "Don’t let your clients decide this. You decide." (S010)
- Scoring (guidance): would you be proud of the customers you would have in three years if you win?
- Used by: S001, S004, S006, S010, S019, S041, S043 (7 sources).

## 7. Tension and release (marketing as change)
- What: a promise creates tension; people buy to relieve it. Awareness without tension is useless; tension without a path is stress.
- Parts: assertion, tension, release; tell ten, create tension so they act, each tells ten.
- Quote: "Because awareness without tension is useless." (S039)
- Quote: "The only way to launch something new is to create tension" (S011)
- Used by: S003, S005, S011, S016, S039 (5 sources).

## 8. People like us do things like this (tribe, status and affiliation)
- What: people choose by belonging and status, not features. A tribe has a shared interest, a leader and something to act on. Find an existing one and serve it.
- Quote: "people like us do things like this." (S002)
- Quote: "The key drivers of widespread marketing impact are status and affiliation." (S040)
- Used by: S002, S006, S011, S013, S016, S031, S035, S040 (8 sources).

## 9. Strategy: systems, time, games, empathy
- What: four things to look at before tactics. Systems are invisible; time asks what quitting means over a longer horizon; games have several players and scarcity; empathy is knowing who it is for and why they want it.
- Quote: "there are four things we’re looking for. We’re looking for systems, we’re looking for time, we’re looking for games, and finally, empathy." (S006)
- Quote: "You don’t have a tactics problem. You have a strategy problem." (S042)
- Used by: S006, S013, S014, S033, S042 (4 independent).

## 10. The Dip and the cul-de-sac
- What: the dip is the hard part between starting and getting good; it ends. A cul-de-sac or treadmill does not. Quit the second and push through the first.
- Quote: "The dip is the hard part in between starting and getting good at it." (S011)
- Scoring (guidance): can you point to people who got through it? If nobody has, treat it as a cul-de-sac.
- Used by: S010, S011, S013, S019, S031 (4 independent).

## 11. Sunk cost is a gift from your former self
- What: you may decline what past spending bought. Ask what you would choose today knowing what you know.
- Quote: "That ticket is a gift from your former self." (S011)
- Quote: "learning to ignore sunk costs is the single most useful thing I can point out to people." (S003)
- Used by: S003, S006, S010, S011, S044 (5 sources).

## 12. Good decision versus good outcome (from Annie Duke)
- What: judge a choice on its reasoning at the time. Write the prediction down before you invest.
- Quote: "Would a good decision maker choose what I chose?" (S010)
- Quote: "Don’t play games you can’t reliably win." (S006)
- Used by: S006, S010 (2 sources).

## 13. Freelancer versus entrepreneur
- What: a freelancer is paid when working and cannot scale; an entrepreneur builds assets that run without them. The 8 to 30 person zone is where you do all the jobs.
- Quote: "If you are doing the work, you’re probably a freelancer." (S010)
- Scoring (guidance): ask what you do that only you can do; delegate or build the tool for the rest.
- Used by: S001, S004, S006, S010, S038 (5 sources).

## 14. Kindling and logs
- What: money raised is kindling; the market is the log. Match the size of the start to the kindling, and let the funding source shape the company.
- Quote: "The money you’re raising from investors is kindling, and the logs you’re starting are the markets you’re trying to get to." (S006)
- Used by: S002, S006 (2 sources).

## 15. The Knot: name the "but"
- What: we are stuck when we want two things that cannot both be true. Say the and aloud, pick one, forgive the other. Separate a problem (has a solution) from a situation (live with it, choose within it).
- Quote: "A knot happens when we want two things that can’t both be true." (S044)
- Quote: "Gravity is a situation, getting to the moon and back is a problem." (S043)
- Used by: S011, S040, S043, S044 (4 sources).

## 16. Jenga: short-term extraction
- What: once a young business has traction, a profit maximizer pulls out sticks (the receptionist, the staff, the service) until the tower falls. Ask whether you generate value or extract it.
- Quote: "Too often, we slip into the lazy mindset of extraction." (S044)
- Used by: S038, S040, S044 (3 sources).

## 17. False proxies
- What: a label used to guess quality (a famous college, a good interview, follower counts) is expensive when it stops tracking the real thing. Judge the work, or pay for a small project first.
- Quote: "there are false proxies in our life and they’re expensive." (S004)
- Used by: S004, S006, S012, S038 (4 sources).

## 18. Throw, do not catch
- What: practice and effort are yours; outcomes are not. Ship bad ideas in volume and good taste follows.
- Quote: "It’s about throwing." (S011)
- Used by: S003, S011, S015 (3 sources).

## Books

### Books: Seth Godin

He has written over twenty books; the corpus teaches these. Summaries are in my own words, not book text.

- Permission Marketing: earn the right to talk to people who want to hear from you. He felt he could never match it, and in 2025 calls himself naive about the race to the bottom it suffered. (S003, S013)
- Purple Cow: be remarkable so people tell others; sold first in a milk carton. (S013, S014, S019)
- The Dip: know when a hard stretch leads somewhere and when it is a slog; written in an 11-day fugue. (S010, S011, S013)
- Tribes: gather a group with a shared interest and a leader. (S002, S016)
- Linchpin: become hard to replace through emotional labor and art; the lizard brain fights it. (S010, S015)
- The Icarus Deception: we are flying too low. (S019)
- Stop Stealing Dreams: school is an industrial artifact; teach problem solving and leadership. (S002, S028, S035)
- Survival Is Not Enough: his dense book; sold 14,000 copies. (S003, S013)
- The Practice: ship creative work even when you do not feel like it, as a generous act. (S003, S036, S037)
- This Is Marketing (2018): marketing is serving a chosen group, seeing them first; the premise, empathy, tension and trust. (S001, S016, S019, S033)
- This Is Strategy (2025): systems, time, games, empathy; a deck of 200 cards. (S006, S013)
- The Song of Significance (2023): a manifesto for teams on significance, enrollment and promises. (S004, S012, S017)
- The Carbon Almanac: a volunteer book on climate. (S004, S011)
- The Knot (2026): stuckness is wanting two things that cannot both be true. (S011, S043, S044)
- Titan collection: an 800-page printed edition of four years of his blog, a single printing. (S030, S031)
- Others named only in passing: Free Prize Inside. (S013)

He writes a book only when he has no choice (S004, S013), and he does not offer step-by-step maps (S002, S015).
