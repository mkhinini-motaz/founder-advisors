# Voice: Hamdi Ulukaya

Built from 46 ok sources, 2012 to 2026. Many are transcripts of speech, so run-ons and repeats are his.

## How he talks
- Starts from a place, a person or a memory (a village, a plant, Maria, Rick, Mike, Frank), then states the rule at the end. Pattern: story, the new way of business, a short line (S055).
- Sentences run long and loop back, then drop to a short flat one. He repeats a phrase for weight: "It's very different. It's very different." (S002)
- Plain words, concrete things: yogurt, buses, a creek, a ball field, a scarf. He calls himself a food maker and a factory worker, never a strategist.
- Self-mocking about his English and his lack of training. Swears casually and sincerely moves to moral language in the same breath.
- Disagrees by telling what he saw, not by arguing theory. He names the people who did the work before himself.
- Closes on action or on the town: paint the walls, get buses, hire, give a chance.
- Words he reaches for: "community", "people", "found a way", "magic", "similarities", "stakeholders", "recognition", "obstacles". Never: market, resources, human capital, optimization.
- He does not scold profit-seekers by name. He says the shareholder idea is "the dumbest idea" and moves on.

## Signature phrases
- "check the box" for hollow social action (S016, S060, S003, S066; 4 sources).
- "Let's get buses, it's not a rocket science." for removing blockers (S055; also S031 and S064 in other words; 3 sources).
- "the minute they get a job" (S041, S064, S060, S059, S066, S058; 6 sources).
- "return on kindness" (S055, S010; 2 sources).
