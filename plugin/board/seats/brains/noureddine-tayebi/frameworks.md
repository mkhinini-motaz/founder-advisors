# Frameworks: Noureddine Tayebi

Built from 18 sources (quick depth, thin). Most-used first. Names are his only where marked; otherwise a short name marked *(unnamed)*. How-to-score lines are guidance for the council, not quotes.

## 1. On-demand services as the road to payments *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Serve an immediate daily need (rides, food, grocery), win a large user base that trusts you without thinking about it, then offer payments.
- **Parts:** pick the need; execute well; trust builds; add payments through the same channels.
- **When to use:** any financial or super-app idea in a cash market.
- **How to score:** 5 if the first service is a clear daily-spend need with no strong rival; 3 if the need is real but crowded; 1 if payments come first with no trust base.
- **Quote:** "really using on demand Services as kind of like a trojan horse to get to it" (S020)
- **Also (added, S051, S052, S053):** S051 gives the four steps in one line: "once you have that trust then it's actually easier to uh to start providing payment and financial services" (S051). S052: "on est en train de créer la confiance d'une manière subconsciente" (S052). S053 names financial services as the goal from day one. Household spend over 80 percent on food and transport (S051, S052).
- **Sources (15):** S009, S016, S017, S019, S020, S034, S037, S038, S040, S044, S046, S021, S051, S052, S053.

## 2. Cash agents *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Drivers, couriers and shops collect cash and turn it into electronic money, with a partner bank behind it. No payment-processing cost.
- **How to score:** 5 if cash is over 90 percent of transactions and your field staff already handle cash; 1 if you rely on cards.
- **Quote:** "Nos chauffeurs et livreurs deviennent des agents mobiles bancaires." (S016)
- **Sources (8):** S016, S019, S020, S021, S037, S041, S044, S046.

## 3. Cash is king because of distrust *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** The region is not unbanked for lack of banks. People and small merchants avoid traceability and tax, so trust must come first.
- **Quote:** "it's not because of a lack of a banking system banking system is here it's just that people don't trust it" (S046)
- **Also (added, S051, S052):** "e-payment all that stuff is total nonsense that's not a real problem" (S051); "c'est pas le manque du système bancaire" (S052). Africa figures in S052: over 72 percent of GDP in the informal cash market.
- **Sources (9):** S016, S019, S020, S021, S034, S037, S046, S051, S052.

## 4. Four-sided marketplace with cross-selling *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Consumers, drivers and couriers, merchants, and FMCG wholesalers and distributors. A user won in one vertical enters the others at almost no cost, so acquisition stays cheap.
- **How to score:** 5 if a second vertical reuses the same users and supply; 1 if each vertical needs new buyers.
- **Quote:** "we have the ability to actually get them into the other verticals pretty much for free" (S021)
- **Sources (5):** S020, S021, S038, S044, S046.

## 5. Embedded finance beats a pure fintech play *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** A wallet pushed alone gets no adoption. Inside an order flow, a top-up discount gives a reason, and repeat use makes the habit.
- **Quote:** "you could do a lot more like in terms of creating virality and adoption when you have um you know like embedded Finance" (S046)
- **Sources (2):** S044, S046.

## 6. Chicken and egg, vertical by vertical *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Start with ride-hailing, recruit drivers fast while marketing brings customers, then move the same customers to food and grocery, then to payments. One vertical for two to three years first.
- **Quote:** "sans qu'on ait un coup d'acquisition associé à ça" (S044)
- **Sources (2):** S020, S044.

## 7. Algeria as lab, then the local-champion playbook *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Test each product in the home market, write the playbook, hire engineers in each country, work with regulators on legal gaps, repeat.
- **Quote:** "Algeria was our first Market it was kind of our lab" (S020)
- **Also (added, S050):** replicate to other countries with automation, standard processes, the same marketing channels (S050, paraphrase).
- **Sources (4):** S019, S020, S021, S050.

## 8. Unit-economics discounting *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** A welcome offer for new customers, segmentation by value per vertical, and discounts that stop once target growth is reached. Check that customers return without a subsidy.
- **Quote:** "il va arrêter ces remises là" (S044)
- **Sources (4):** S019, S021, S044, S046.

## 9. Values as capital, the equilibrium idea *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** From physics: a system at minimum energy is in equilibrium. A society or company with a strong value system gets a big impact from a small effort.
- **Quote:** "avec un minimum d'efforts on peut créer un grand impact aussi simple que ça" (S034)
- **Also (added, S050, S052):** "avec un minimum du d'effort, tu crées un grand impact" (S052); "c'est un système de valeurs" (S050). He ties it to the second law of thermodynamics; culture is a capital a person inherits at birth.
- **Sources (6):** S019, S027, S034, S044, S050, S052. Inferred reading: a worldview, not a scoring tool.

## 10. Local champion who comes home *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** In Latin America, Southeast Asia and China, a founder with Silicon Valley success returned and made a model others copied.
- **Quote:** "il y a toujours eu un champion local" (S034)
- **Sources (4):** S019, S020, S034, S046.

## 11. A startup is exponential growth *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Not tied to technology. Growth of about 7 percent a week or more draws capital, so profit is not first sought.
- **Quote:** "une startup c'est un juste un phénomène économique d'une entreprise à croissance exponentielle aussi simple que ça" (S034)
- **Also (added, S049, S051, S053):** "le mot clé la startup c'est la croissance exponentielle" (S049). S051: a small tech firm without that growth is not a startup, and a big one can still be one. S053: startup is wrongly translated as emerging company; it means a fast-growth company (شركة ذو نمو سريع).
- **Sources (7):** S034, S037, S040, S048, S049, S051, S053.

## 12. Check the market size before a city or country *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Before entering, run a study of the city or country. Either adapt the service to its particulars or do not invest.
- **How to score:** 5 if the market repays the entry cost; 1 if it clearly cannot.
- **Quote:** "حجم السوق يعني أمر مهم جدا" (S048)
- **Sources (1):** S048.

## 13. Penetration KPI per commune *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Aim for at least 10 percent of the population in every commune served, with KPIs from the CEO to the last operator.
- **Quote:** "au moins 10% de pénétration en population dans chaque commune où on opère" (S034)
- **Sources (2):** S034, S044.

## 14. Product-market fit through necessity *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Solve a problem where people have a necessity and they adopt even an imperfect product; raise money and improve.
- **Quote:** "when you have a necessity you know like even if your product is not great like you'll have adoption" (S046)
- **Sources (3):** S020, S034, S046.

## 15. Push the limits, then go to the regulators *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Prove value first, then bring jobs, taxes and local talent to the decision makers; stay inside the law.
- **Quote:** "it's the burden of like companies like ours to be proactive" (S027)
- **Also (added, S053):** regulators should frame an idea as it runs; a three-to-four-year framing means the train has gone ("في ثلاث سنين يعني القطار يمر خلاص", S053).
- **Sources (6):** S019, S020, S027, S044, S046, S053.

## 16. What investors need at each stage *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Early stage is the founder and the story; growth stage is story plus numbers; late stage is a big organisation.
- **Quote:** "growth stage it's still about storytelling but you have to show the numbers" (S046)
- **Also (added, S050):** a raise follows a roadmap: each milestone needs a growth target, which unlocks the next round; fundraising becomes a full-time job (S050, paraphrase). S051 lists the three VC criteria (below, 18).
- **Sources (4):** S019, S034, S046, S050.

## 17. Marketing 360 *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Performance channels are not enough; add TV, influencers, TikTok and Snap to build the brand and cut acquisition cost.
- **Quote:** "il faut toujours avoir une stratégie 360" (S044)
- **Sources (1):** S044.

## 18. The three VC criteria *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Venture firms judge (1) who the founders are, (2) the idea and its defensibility, (3) the market size. Doubt about the founder ends the deal even if the idea and market are great. He says this is based on data, not feelings. Checks: psychological read of ambition and commitment, plus reference checks.
- **How to score:** guidance only: 5 if the team has done it before and the founder is proven; 1 if the idea is strong but the founder is untested.
- **Quote:** "like they have three criteria the first one is uh who is who who are the founders" (S051)
- **Sources (1):** S051. Same view in S050 (paraphrase: investors back execution, not only the idea).

## 19. Multi-sided marketplace launch, chicken and egg *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Onboard drivers first (classified ads, a pitch of what they can earn, training); open to users only once supply is enough; with product-market fit both sides arrive organically and a network effect forms.
- **Parts:** supply recruit; train; open demand; organic loop.
- **How to score:** 5 if supply can be recruited by a clear earnings pitch before demand exists; 1 if both sides need paid acquisition.
- **Quote:** "so it's kind of like a chicken and egg question" appears in S051 as his phrase; use the S051 section on drivers, not a quote.
- **Sources (3):** S051, S020, S044.

## 20. Need-based product versus a created need *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Two product types: one answers a need already felt (ride-hailing; word of mouth drives adoption and growth is organic), the other creates a need people did not know they had (social networks). Yassir's success rests on the first; the team is building blocks toward a bigger product he will not name.
- **Quote:** none verbatim (S050 caption is garbled); paraphrase of S050. Related verbatim rule in S051: "don't build something that you think people want build something that people tell you I want or I need" (S051).
- **Sources (2):** S050, S051.

## 21. The growth-lever model *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** If you can control the levers you can control growth: acquisition cost, bringing users back to use more often, spend per segment. Use machine learning to find customer segments, target and retarget each one differently; raise the share of a friend group that uses the app (for example from 10 percent toward 30 percent).
- **Sources (1):** S050 (paraphrase of a garbled caption; the numbers may be misheard).

## 22. Three factors for impact *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** (1) intellect, (2) implication in the environment: strategy plus execution, (3) money. He says one should not be embarrassed to say money matters, and he "half believes" the line that you need no money to make money.
- **Quote:** "Il faut avoir de l'argent pour avoir de l'impact" (S052)
- **Sources (1):** S052.

## 23. Network-introduction rule for partners and investors *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** A small company should not approach a big partner or a VC cold. Find a person who knows the decision-maker and get introduced; pitch a win-win. Once the brand is known, big companies take you seriously. Cold emails to VCs almost never get answers.
- **Quote:** "il faut toujours essayer d'avoir un réseau et de toujours l'élargir" (S052)
- **Sources (2):** S050 (paraphrase), S052. Also: S051 and S053 say to surround yourself with people who have done it before.

## 24. Where to incorporate *(unnamed)*
- **What it is:** Choose the country of incorporation by the market and the investor. Foreign investors hesitate over an Algerian entity (opacity, bureaucracy), so incorporate in the US and receive the money there; do not confine yourself to Algeria.
- **Sources (2):** S050 (paraphrase), S051 ("don't constrain yourself also so you know just to the Algerian Market").
