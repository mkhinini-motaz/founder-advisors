# Positions: Noureddine Tayebi

Built from 18 sources (quick depth, thin). Topics with no evidence say no recorded position. Dates are the source dates; "undated" where the source has none.

## Cash and payments
- **Cash.** An opportunity, not a problem. "pour moi c'est pas un problème sincèrement c'est beaucoup plus une opportunité qu'autre chose" (S034, 2022-12-25). Also "cash is really King over 90% of the transactions are still happening in cash" (S020, 2023-12-05).
- **Why cash stays king.** Distrust of banks and tax traceability. "the number one reason is actually all these like Merchants want to evade paying taxes so so cash remains King" (S046, undated).
- **Card payment.** Not suited to the region. "qui n'est pas adapté à la région" (S037, 2021-12-24). Earlier (S048, 2020-12-13) he says cash is simple and technology adds traceability.
- **Mobile money.** The Maghreb is more interesting than sub-Saharan countries like Kenya; no one has executed well yet. "it doesn't go beyond uh you know like peer-to-peer and Merchant payments" (S046, undated).
- **Payment adoption.** An incentive at the point of order. "si vous mettez de l'argent par exemple dans votre portefeuille monétaire vous recevrez 30 % de réduction" (S044, 2022-11-22).

## Market and competition
- **Competition.** Welcome it. "بالعكس انا نشوفها كامر جيد" (S040, 2026-01-28). Global rivals do not worry him: "on n pas peur de la concurrence euh sincèrement même s'il y a des mondiales" (S037, 2021-12-24).
- **Winner takes most.** "des monopoles hyper locaux" (S044, 2022-11-22).
- **Moat.** Bundling. "we're the only ones that provide multiple services uh in the region and that gives you power because you start bundling between the different products that you have" (S046, undated).
- **Is it an Uber copy?** No. The business model goes past transport (S048, 2020-12-13; S040, 2026-01-28: "احنايا خلقنا يعني ايكوسيستم د سيرفيس").
- **Expansion.** Algeria alone is not enough. "se focaliser que sur le marché algérien c'est pas assez" (S034, 2022-12-25). No recorded position on the Gulf beyond "not on the road map" (S041, 2023-02-23).
- **Taxis.** In Tunisia they stay a strategic partner. "pour nous les taxis ça reste un partenaire extr ment stratégique" (S038, 2023-02-08).

## Money, raising and listing
- **Raising money.** Early rounds were painful. "initially honestly like it was always painful to uh to raise money" (S019, 2024-06-10); later "fundraising was easier because we grew so much that VC firms could not ignore us anymore." (S017, 2022-11-07). A raise is "une responsabilité" (S037, 2021-12-24).
- **Debt.** No recorded position.
- **Unicorn label.** "dire qu'on est licorne ou pas n'a pas vraiment de de valeur ajoutée" (S038, 2023-02-08).
- **Listing.** Ambition of a US listing. Earlier (S048, 2020-12-13): maybe in three to five years. Later (S044, 2022-11-22): "bien sûr à 1000% en fait c'était un petit peu l'objectif et la mission", and "peut-être que qu'on pourra pas le faire mais c'est pas grave" (S044). Ten-year goal in S019 (2024-06-10, recorded Dec 2022): about 40 billion dollars market cap.
- **Selling the company.** Impossible: Yassir is a mission and nearly the whole team owns shares (S040, 2026-01-28). In 2020 (S048) he called the Careem buyout rumour false: "لا هذه كانت دعاية خطيئة في الحقيقة" (S048).
- **Equity.** About 15 percent for the team (S019, S044). He will not bend on it.
- **Pricing.** No recorded position beyond discount and wallet-incentive rules above.
- **Profitability.** Cash-flow positive within about a year and a half (S019). First-year figures in S020 (company claims).

## Team and culture
- **Hiring.** Never compromise on quality, even at 200 hires a month (S019). Hire months ahead (S044).
- **Hiring in the region.** The biggest operating weakness is "one is the lack of ambition uh and two is the lack of attention to details" (S019, 2024-06-10).
- **Reverse brain drain.** "نجلب هجره عكسيه" (S041, 2023-02-23).
- **Culture.** Values are the most important thing. "les valeurs c'est c'est le truc le plus important" (S044, 2022-11-22).
- **Partners.** Do not go alone. "يد وحده ما تسفقش" (S040, 2026-01-28).
- **Firing.** No recorded position.
- **Remote work.** No recorded position.
- **Work hours and burnout.** Days from 5 or 6 in the morning to midnight; "ما عنديش مكتب" (S040, 2026-01-28). Stress exists but enjoyment replaces it (S019).

## Region, regulation and ambition
- **Biggest problem.** The entrepreneur, not funding or bureaucracy. "لي أكبر مشكل كان في الميدان هذا هو المقاول بذاته" (S048, 2020-12-13); same view in S037, S046, S034. In 2026 he says they were still problems: "ماش نقول باللي هذوما ما كانوش مشاكل" (S040).
- **Regulation.** "il y a des vide juridiques" (S044, 2022-11-22); the Maghreb usually waits for law before technology, the US the reverse (S038).
- **Drivers' legal status.** An admitted gap. "il y a un vide par rapport à ça et il faut bien sûr trouver un cadre" (S038, 2023-02-08). In 2020 he said the service runs under existing car-rental-with-driver law (S048).
- **AI.** Dispatch and batching, and fraud detection: "des décisions en temps réel sans qu'il y ait une intervention humaine" (S044, 2022-11-22). "we own our data" (S027, 2025-12-30).
- **Data centres.** Working toward its own to meet local-hosting rules (S027).
- **Timing.** Launched food delivery in many cities in March 2020 and called it "a lifesaver" (S019).
- **Mission.** "ياسير مهمه اكثر من منها شركه" (S040).
- **Focus versus diversification.** Ride-hailing alone for two to three years, then cross-sell (S020).
- **Niche, content, paid ads, side projects, brand.** Brand: build with the 360 approach (S044). No recorded position on the others.

## Added from S049 to S053 (topup, batch 05)

- **Equity, both views with dates.** About 15 percent for the team (S019, recorded Dec 2022; S044, 2022-11-22). Later he says close to 20 percent (S051, 2023-05-11) and 20 percent (S052, 2025-06-02): "c'est pas une faveur que je donne c'est en fait c'est leur droit" (S052). Vesting and protections apply to everyone, himself included (S050, 2020-11-24; S051).
- **Founder control.** He is an employee of his own company; the board could fire him. "aujourd'hui je suis employé de l'entreprise" (S052, 2025-06-02).
- **Startup definition.** Not tied to technology; exponential growth. "le mot clé la startup c'est la croissance exponentielle" (S049, 2020-11-19); same in S051 (2023-05-11) and S053 (2022-11-19).
- **Why cash.** Tax evasion and no traceability. "c'est pas le manque du système bancaire" (S052, 2025-06-02). He adds, for e-payment: "e-payment all that stuff is total nonsense that's not a real problem" (S051, 2023-05-11). This sits beside his earlier line that the card is not suited (S037).
- **Mobile money.** M-Pesa in Kenya proves cash can be replaced: it is a solution problem, not a mentality problem, is the host's summary and not a quote (S052, 2025-06-02). Earlier view in this file (S046) says the Maghreb is more interesting than Kenya and unexecuted.
- **Competition and Uber.** "on competing with Uber we don't compete with Uber" (S051, 2023-05-11); Uber is not in his markets, and ride-hailing is one segment toward payments (S051, S052).
- **Expansion, newer facts.** By 2022 six countries and 45 cities (S053, 2022-11-19). By 2025, 150,000 drivers and couriers, South Africa and Senegal began, and the French quick-commerce firm Flink's activities bought; focus is the Middle East and Africa (S052, 2025-06-02). Recent view: the Europe angle must be in synergy with the Maghreb business.
- **Raising, 2025 view.** Last round was 150 million dollars from Silicon Valley funds; his network helped (S052). In 2022 he said the round has three aims: grow the 300 engineers four times, new apps in current countries, new continents (S053, 2022-11-19). By 2025 about 500 engineers (S052).
- **Hiring: freelancers.** Prefer an in-house team: "I think it's best to have your own team" (S051, 2023-05-11), with a path to convert freelancers.
- **Hiring: fire.** If someone is not good, let them go (S051, paraphrase).
- **Hiring: profile.** Hustlers with attention to detail and stamina; product management mindset (S051, 2023-05-11).
- **Founder quality.** Investors judge founder, then idea, then market. Founder doubt ends it (S051). Average successful founder in Silicon Valley is over 40; Google added Eric Schmidt, Facebook added Sheryl Sandberg (S051, 2023-05-11).
- **Startup events.** "nonsense events" of pitching and photos: a startup must make money (S051, 2023-05-11).
- **School and startups.** Finish the degree before starting; he could be wrong (S051).
- **Partners.** Seek introductions through people who know the other side (S050, 2020-11-24).
- **Education systems.** The exam culture teaches fear of risk (S050); the French-derived system leaves the weaker student behind while the US personalises (S052).
- **Diaspora.** Helpful, but stays in its own culture bubble and does not open to the new place (S052, 2025-06-02).
- **Europe.** Only continent with negative growth by 2050 (S052); this is a reason for his Africa and Middle East focus.
- **IPO.** In 2022: an exchange listing "from day one" ("نعم نعم هذا من من اليوم الأول", S053, 2022-11-19); the New York exchange put Yassir's logo on its screen without being asked.
- **Pricing and discounting.** No further recorded position in this batch.
- **Debt, remote work, burnout, AI.** No recorded position in this batch. S050 and S052 note the team working at 1 am or 2 am as a sign of belief.
