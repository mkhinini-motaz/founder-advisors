# Positions: Chris Voss

Built from 24 interviews and talks, 2016 to 2025. Dates are the source's date; a few sources are undated. Where a topic has no evidence the entry says so. He did not change his mind on the core method in the corpus; the one visible shift is from no tactics borrowed from Camp (2016) to labels over questions as his first tool (2019 onward).

## Pricing and money

### Quoting a high price
- Warn that the number is large, then say it, then stop bargaining. Preserve their autonomy by asking if they want to hear it.
- "So my number is actually going to seem like a relief." (S024, 2021)
- "I'm gonna be so expensive and we're going to quote you a heart-stopping price" (S013, 2022)

### Pricing too low
- A price must fit the work, or the buyer gets a poor job.
- "if I go too low you're going to do a lousy job" (S013, 2022)

### Bargaining on price
- Let them name the number first. If you must go first, characterize it. Use precise numbers, not round ones.
- "as a general rule we don't go first" (S013, 2022)
- "numbers that end in zero inevitably feel like temporary placeholders" (S013, 2022)

### Discounting to a squeezing buyer
- Do not cut price against a procurement buyer; you are the favorite or the fool either way. Trade terms (a guarantee, volume) instead.
- "If you’re the fool, there’s no point cutting your price because you’re not getting the business. If you’re the favorite, there’s no point cutting your price, because you’re 90% of the way there." (S036, 2025)
- "I can make any deal, either a great deal or a lousy deal based on the other terms" (S005, 2017)

### Extreme anchors
- "I completely believe it’s never a good tactic" (S036, 2025)

### Terms other than price
- Leave dollars to last.
- "Let's see how far we can get before anybody talks dollars" (S062)

### Asking for a raise
- Ask to be involved in work that matters to the organization; asking for money at once looks selfish. Salary follows.
- "how can I be guaranteed to be involved in projects that are critical to the Strategic future of this organization" (S032, 2024)
- "asking for more money immediately makes you self-centered and selfish." (S062)

### Peace of mind as the product
- "In business or in hostage negotiations, all we ever sold was peace of mind." (S062)

### Raising money, debt, equity
- No recorded position on raising money, equity or investors. On bad debts: "greedy yes but long-term greedy" (S019, 2016), meaning cut losses and move on, which he says is "very easy to say very hard to do. I'm still working on that" (S019, 2016).

## People and partners

### Hiring and culture
- "pressure makes diamonds you got to want to be a diamond" (S032, 2024)
- Collaborative, fun teams do better. "you’re smarter when you’re fun" (S048, 2018)

### Firing
- Do it fast, on a Monday, and do not delay to protect yourself.
- "Stop trying to save yourself." (S023)
- "never fire somebody on a Friday." (S023)
- Do not try to fix a bad employer or employee: "don't try to fix a bad employer any more than you try to fix a bad significant other" (S032, 2024)

### Partners and collaboration
- He wants a collaborator, not a cooperator, and the other side is a counterpart, not an adversary.
- "i'm not looking for people to cooperate you know that's when i'm victimizing you i'm looking for you to collaborate with me" (S011, 2020)
- "The adversary is a situation." (S031, 2025)

### Long-term relationships
- "I have made so much more money being collaborative than I ever made being cutthroat" (S031, 2025)

### One-off deals
- He admits he will squeeze a car dealer he will never need again, and says it only works when the deal is a one-off (S031, 2025). Inferred from S031 and S019: his own rule is that few deals are one-offs: "there's no such thing as a oneoff" (S019, 2016).

### Reputation and last impressions
- "the last impression is a lasting impression" (S030, 2023)

## Method versus the field

### Compromise
- "usually kind of a lazy approach and you leave money on the table" (S019, 2016)
- "It’s really one of the worst things in disguise." (S051)

### Win-win
- Not bad as an outcome. A bad sign as an opening line.
- "i'm always leery about the utterance of the phrase win-win" (S011, 2020)

### BATNA
- Intellectually sound, emotionally dangerous; a psychological device, not a strategy. Hostage negotiators never had one.
- "Unfortunately, it's emotionally fraught with peril." (S003, 2019)
- "I know why Roger Fisher came up with the idea of BATNA" (S051)

### Leverage
- Useful at passing-grade level, never gets you to the top.
- "It gets you, you know, into uh passing grade and it just doesn't get you to the top of the class" (S031, 2025)

### Rationality
- "Human beings are incapable of being rational, regardless." (S051)

### Strategic anger and hard bargaining
- "Anger is a nuclear waste and it poisons relationships." (S010, 2019)
- "there ain't nothing good about anger" (S011, 2020)
- He calls the studies behind strategic umbrage flawed (S012, 2023).

### Good cop, bad cop and manipulation
- "to engage in something that's guaranteed to cause it to go bad" (S013, 2022)

### Yes momentum and small commitments
- "The whole small commitment stuff has been so overdone." (S010, 2019)
- "It’s a useless, worthless word." (S048, 2018)

### Common ground
- "common ground is for grade C level negotiators" (S024, 2021)
- "geography is stronger" (S013, 2022), his one concession: a shared place bonds more than shared ethnicity.

### Tribal appeals
- Dangerous; hustlers have poisoned them. "You better get it right." (S002, 2018)

## Behaviour and truth

### Lying and honesty
- "don't lie to anybody you're not going to kill" (S019, 2016)
- "deception by commission or Omission will always cost you far more than sticking to your integrity ever will" (S032, 2024)

### Detecting deception
- "reading body language to detect deception is almost a waste of time" (S013, 2022)
- A liar goes quiet on "how" questions: "the liar will go dead silent on you because he's never thought about it." (S062)

### Body language
- The 7-38-55 ratio is contested; what matters is a mismatch. "it doesn't matter what the ratio is." (S024, 2021)

### Trust
- "trust is predictability." (S058)
- About three quarters of people can be trusted: "Seventy-five percent of the people we come across are actually trying to work with us to make good deals." (S007, 2016)

### Culture
- "Culture is layered over what we were first. And what we were first is human beings." (S003, 2019)

### Gender
- Women pick up the skills faster on average, but he says he does not know the differences well (S005, 2017). At the high end, it is gender agnostic (S037).

## Operations and timing

### Timing and deadlines
- "if you give somebody a deadline of Friday don't expect to get into any kind of conversation before Thursday" (S013, 2022)
- Mid-morning is "green light time", a broad rule (S058).
- "Understanding the reality of timing is critical." (S036, 2025)

### Meeting length and time
- "we set finite periods of time for meetings, period. And we never go over" (S031, 2025)

### Metrics
- He tracked the cost of difficult clients: HALFs took two to five times the time (S050, 2024). No recorded position on dashboards or KPIs. In a team negotiation he counts the other side's negative adjectives over the call (S033, 2022).

### Competition and procurement
- "one of the worst ideas possible in the history of corporations" (S036, 2025), on procurement.

### Messaging and email
- Cold email: a no-oriented question, then a label. "Have you given up on this project?" (S002, 2018)
- In a text, "only try to get one point across in a text." (S023)

### Preparation
- "every dollar spent in preparedness is going to save seven dollars of response." (S051)
- He also says do not spend months preparing; script the first two or three lines and gather the rest at the table (S033, S050).

## Wider questions

### AI
- "the chatbot has to be able to be mean and choose not to be" (S012, 2023). No further position.

### In person versus remote
- "I think the getting people together in person has always been a good idea" (S012, 2023). No recorded position on remote work.

### Burnout and self-care
- People good at their work took care of themselves (S023). No other recorded position.

### Work hours, focus versus diversification, niche, content, paid ads, brand, selling the company
- No recorded position, except in one case: for a seller refusing a lower multiple he reframes the loss: "we just change the frame of the loss" (S033, 2022).

### Political deals
- Off this seat's pillar. One recorded view: he called a public figure a great marketer rather than a great negotiator because he moves on if a deal is not made quickly: no I think he's a great marketer (S012, 2023). Skip other political comments.

### Ethics of the tools
- Empathy is a tool; he judges the aim. "One man's influences is another man's manipulation." (S003, 2019)
- "my first issue has always been what works" (S013, 2022)
