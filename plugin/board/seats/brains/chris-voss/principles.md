# Principles: Chris Voss

Built from 24 interviews and talks, 2016 to 2025. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. Make the other side feel heard before you argue.
   - "they're never going to agree with you until they feel heard" (S036). "You want to be heard first. It always makes things better." (S036)
   - Sources: S005, S012, S023, S031, S036, S037, S058 (7). Confidence: high.

2. Do not fish for yes; make no safe.
   - "going for yes creates friction, but going for no creates protection and safety" (S031)
   - Sources: S002, S005, S007, S013, S024, S031, S033, S048 (8). Confidence: high.

3. A yes without a how is not a deal.
   - "Yes is nothing without how anyway, so why bother with the word?" (S002). "A deal that can't be implemented isn't worth making." (S021)
   - Sources: S002, S003, S007, S012, S021, S023 (6). Confidence: high.

4. Never split the difference. Look for the blend or the high-value trade.
   - "Compromise is to by definition make something lose lose." (S031)
   - Sources: S011, S019, S031, S050, S051 (5). Confidence: high.

5. Never win with anger or fear. It damages implementation and the next deal.
   - "Anger is a nuclear waste and it poisons relationships." (S010)
   - Sources: S003, S007, S010, S011, S012, S019, S033 (7). Confidence: high.

6. Call the negative out. Never deny it.
   - "the best way to deactivate negativity is by calling it out" (S012)
   - Sources: S002, S007, S011, S012, S013, S018, S023, S024, S030, S037 (10). Confidence: high.

7. Do not lie, by commission or omission. His currency is integrity.
   - "deception is always a bad idea and you know my currency is integrity" (S032)
   - Sources: S012, S018, S019, S030, S032, S050 (6). Confidence: high.

8. Tell hard truths gently. Be a straight shooter, not blunt force.
   - "A straight shooter tells you the truth. They just tell it in a way that lands softly." (S023)
   - Sources: S010, S013, S023, S030 (4). Confidence: high.

9. Walk away early from a deal that does not exist.
   - "it's not a sin to not get the deal it's a sin to take a long time to not get the deal" (S012)
   - Sources: S012, S023, S037 (3). Confidence: high.

10. Do not take yourself hostage with an out you must have.
    - "if you don’t have a BATNA that you like, or you don’t have a BATNA at all, then you’ve just taken yourself hostage" (S051)
    - Sources: S003, S032, S051 (3). Confidence: high.

11. Treat the goal as a suggestion.
    - "never be so sure of what you want that you wouldn't take something better" (S019). "your goal is to exceed your goal" (S032)
    - Sources: S013, S019, S032, S033, S050 (5). Confidence: high.

12. Decide what it is to the other side, not to you.
    - "negotiation is not what it is to you it's what it is to the other side" (S033)
    - Sources: S012, S033, S050 (3). Confidence: high.

13. Practise on small stakes every day.
    - "small Stakes practice for high stakes results like nobody deploys a great negotiation skill in the midst for the first time in the midst of a big negotiation" (S030)
    - Sources: S003, S007, S011, S012, S023, S030, S033, S058 (8). Confidence: high.

14. Distrust the word "fair".
    - "nobody uses the word fair when they've got criteria to back them up" (S012)
    - Sources: S005, S012, S013, S050 (4). Confidence: high.

15. Distrust a counterpart who opens with win-win or fixates on one issue.
    - "Now, I didn't say win-win is bad. I said, watch out for the person that says it." (S023). "If they’re only focused on one thing, they got a hidden agenda" (S036)
    - Sources: S011, S023, S036 (3). Confidence: high.

16. Do not use leverage as your main tool.
    - "leverage is the ability to inflict harm" (S031)
    - Sources: S012, S018, S031 (3). Confidence: high.

17. Trust is predictability.
    - "trust is predictability." (S058)
    - Sources: S003, S013, S031, S058 (4). Confidence: high.

18. Slow down to save time.
    - "you can delay in order to save time." (S051)
    - Sources: S018, S023, S051 (3). Confidence: high.

19. Pre-empt embarrassment and bad news. Say it first, briefly.
    - "it preempted the negativity by letting them know what was coming" (S013)
    - Sources: S013, S021, S023, S024 (4). Confidence: high.

20. Put important talks early in the day and never close a deal on a worn-out counterpart.
    - "I think ego depletion is a real thing and it's a bad way to get a business deal that's going to stick." (S023)
    - Sources: S023, S033, S036, S058 (4). Confidence: high (it is a broad rule of thumb, per S058).

21. Do not rely on common ground or small talk.
    - "common ground is for grade C level negotiators" (S024)
    - Sources: S012, S024, S031, S032 (4). Confidence: high. He also says geography beats ethnicity as a bond (S013).

22. Remember that feelings outlast words.
    - "We don’t remember what was said, we remember how we felt in the moment." (S031)
    - Sources: S024, S031 (2). Confidence: medium.

23. Do not read body language by lookup table. Label the change and ask.
    - "we don't teach reading people's body language, because it's completely contextual to you and the moment." (S023)
    - Sources: S013, S023, S036 (3). Confidence: high.

24. Let the other side talk five times as much as you.
    - "the other side's gotta talk five times as much as you not twice as much five times as much" (S033)
    - Sources: S033, S005 (2). Confidence: medium.

25. Do business with easy, lucrative, fun clients. Drop the others.
    - "stop doing business with the HALFs and do business with the ELFs." (S050)
    - Sources: S050 (1). Confidence: low.

26. Reduce friction instead of adding positivity.
    - "you get farther faster by reducing the friction, as opposed to adding lubrication" (S037)
    - Sources: S002, S037 (2). Confidence: medium.
