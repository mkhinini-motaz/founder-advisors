# Principles: Jonah Berger

Built from 47 sources (2013 to 2026). Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Make the sharer look good, not yourself.** Ask how talking about you will make the customer look, not whether they like you.
   - "So the best way to get people to talk about us is to make them look good." (S087)
   - "we tend to focus on ourselves right we tend to focus on what we offer" (S078)
   - Sources: 12. Confidence: high.

2. **Be thought of before you can be talked about.** Liking is not enough; attach to a frequent cue that fires before the decision.
   - "It's not just how much we like that thing, it's whether we're thinking about it or not." (S087)
   - "You can't buy something that you're not thinking about." (S087)
   - Sources: 10. Confidence: high.

3. **Pick a frequent trigger over a rare one.**
   - "More frequent triggers are better than less frequent triggers." (S087)
   - "Because you're not thinking about it when you leave the house." (S087)
   - Sources: 6 (S059, S066, S067, S087, S090, S104). Confidence: high.

4. **Study psychology, not platforms.** Channels come and go; the reasons people share stay.
   - "Technologies will come and go. Why people share things will pretty much stay the same" (S059)
   - "The technology will change, the psychology won't, right?" (S087)
   - Sources: 9. Confidence: high.

5. **Most word of mouth is offline.** Do not build a plan on online metrics alone.
   - "only about seven percent of word-of-mouth is actually done online" (S058)
   - "most word of mouth is still offline, face to face" (S087)
   - Sources: 11. Confidence: high. The exact share varies (see positions).

6. **Message, not messenger.** Do not hunt the influencer; build something many people want to pass on.
   - "It's not about who delivers the word of mouth." (S098)
   - "it's not about the messenger necessarily. It's the message." (S104)
   - Sources: 4 (S059, S098, S104, S060). Confidence: high.

7. **Count shares, not followers.**
   - "if they don't share our stuff it's it's not necessarily going to matter" (S078)
   - "It's not about accumulating followers and social connections." (S087)
   - Sources: 6. Confidence: high.

8. **Do not look like an ad.** People avoid sharing persuasion.
   - "The more something feels like an ad, the less interested people are in sharing it." (S087)
   - "No one wants to share something that looks like an ad or seems like a persuasion attempt." (S104)
   - Sources: 5. Confidence: high.

9. **Show, do not tell. Put the proof inside the story.**
   - "no one wants to seem like a walking advertisement but they're happy to tell stories" (S090)
   - "nobody tells bedtime facts. They tell bedtime stories." (S083)
   - Sources: 8. Confidence: high.

10. **Boring is not an excuse.** Find the inner remarkability.
    - "It's not about whether you're an exciting product or a boring product." (S087)
    - "nothing is less exciting than a blender" (S066)
    - Sources: 6. Confidence: high.

11. **A bad product has no word-of-mouth problem; it has a product problem.**
    - "if your things terrible you don't have a word of mouth problem or a marketing problem you got a product or service problem right right" (S099)
    - "if customers have a great experience they'll tell others" (S094)
    - Sources: 3 (S099, S094, S100). Confidence: high.

12. **Do not push. Find the barrier.** Information is rarely the barrier.
    - "Information is rarely enough to get people to change." (S102)
    - "rarely is information the barrier we think it is more often the barrier is" (S077)
    - Sources: 14. Confidence: high.

13. **Diagnose before you prescribe.** The doctor asks before the cast.
    - "doesn't start by saying hey let me put a cast on your leg doctor starts by saying well wait tell me about the problem" (S077)
    - Sources: 6 (S054, S060, S062, S077, S085, S102). Confidence: high.

14. **Give a small menu, never one option, never a decoy.**
    - "But second, you didn't give them a choice." (S102)
    - "you're not giving them 50 options or a thousand options because that's overwhelming" (S085)
    - Sources: 9. Confidence: high.

15. **Ask for less, then more.**
    - "we have to ask for less and ask for more" (S072)
    - Sources: 9. Confidence: high.

16. **Let people try it so they convince themselves, and the trial must be reversible.**
    - "I've resolved that uncertainty myself. I've convinced myself." (S054)
    - "lowering the upfront barrier to trial" (S062)
    - Sources: 10. Confidence: high. Free works only if the product is good (S065, S100).

17. **Make the cost of doing nothing visible.**
    - "inaction always feels safe it always feels easy but it often isn't as safe as we might think" (S072)
    - Sources: 5. Confidence: high.

18. **Do not tell people what not to do.** A warning can become a recommendation.
    - "a warning became a recommendation telling people not to do something made them more likely to do it" (S091)
    - Sources: 3 (S091, S094, S102). Confidence: high.

19. **Tell people what their neighbors do; do not trust what people say they will do.**
    - "all these appeals sounded great when people heard them no one actually did what they said they were gonna do" (S103)
    - "We measured what they actually did." (S083)
    - Sources: 7. Confidence: high.

20. **Do not reward only the top.** Close comparisons motivate; far ones demotivate.
    - "that works really well for the close but it's demotivating for a lot of the people that are not so close" (S080)
    - Sources: 5 (S053, S061, S076, S080, S095). Confidence: high.

21. **Words matter as much as ideas.**
    - "we spend a lot less time thinking about the specific words we want to use to communicate those ideas" (S084)
    - Sources: 9. Confidence: high.

22. **Drop hedges unless doubt is the message; name where the doubt sits.**
    - "When we hedge, we undermine our impact." (S084)
    - Sources: 11. Confidence: high.

23. **Ask for advice; it makes you look better, not worse.**
    - "people like thinking their advice is good right" (S101)
    - Sources: 9. Confidence: high.

24. **Data over opinion, and over one famous example.**
    - "what I like better than your opinion or my opinion or anybody else's opinion is data" (S096)
    - "any one example can be right or wrong" (S082)
    - Sources: 8. Confidence: high.

25. **Odds, not guarantees.**
    - "We can't guarantee that something will go viral." (S059)
    - "nobody hits a thousand" (S077)
    - Sources: 6. Confidence: high.

26. **Do not fake the special feeling.**
    - "You don't want to do something disingenuous or or tell people they have something they don't actually have" (S104)
    - Sources: 1. Confidence: low.

27. **Spot influence first, then choose it.**
    - "How by understanding that can I choose my own influence?" (S053)
    - "the first step to taking advantage of influence is realizing it occurs, spotting it in the world around us." (S076)
    - Sources: 6. Confidence: high.

28. **Referral bonuses can crowd out the wish to share.**
    - "it sort of crowds out their intrinsic motivation or their willingness to share without some reward" (S097)
    - Sources: 1. Confidence: low.
