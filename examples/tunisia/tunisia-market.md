# Tunisia: market, channels, payments and logistics

General information, not legal, tax or financial advice. Check the official text and a licensed professional before relying on it.

**As of 30 Sept 2026.** Every figure cites a public source listed at the end. Figures marked **(approx.)** are estimates.

## Economy

- Central bank (BCT) policy rate **7%**, held on 29 July 2026. Money-market rate (TMM) 7.00% in August 2026. Inflation **5.3%** in June 2026, core 5%. [1]
- Budget for bank credit at roughly TMM plus a bank margin of a few points. **(verify with a bank quote)**
- Population about 12.4 million. [2]

## People online

- **10.4M internet users (84.3%)** and **7.83M social media identities** (87.3% of adults). [2]
- Ad reach: **Facebook 7.80M** (53.5% male), YouTube 7.83M, **TikTok 6.00M adults** (58.2% male, fastest-growing), Messenger 5.85M, **Instagram 4.05M** (52.6% female, +15.7% a year), Snapchat 2.05M (62.6% female). [2]
- **Facebook groups are the marketplace.** Meta withheld Marketplace from Tunisia in 2018, so buying and selling in groups is a deep habit. [3] Restaurants and small shops use their Facebook page as their storefront. **(verify; no source)**
- **Language** is a mix: Tunisian Arabic in Arabic script, Tunisian Arabic in Latin letters (arabizi), and French. Hashtags are often English. [4] **(verify; source 4 did not open)**

## Paid and earned media prices

- Meta ads (agency guides, not Meta data): **CPM 3–15 TND, CPC 0.15–0.80 TND, cost per lead 1–8 TND**. Recommended test: **10–15 TND a day for two weeks**. SME monthly budgets run 500–2,000 TND. Tunisian and international cards are both accepted. [5][6]
- TikTok self-serve ads opened to Tunisian advertisers around December 2024 to January 2025. No published prices. [7]
- **Creators (2026):** nano (1–10k followers) **50–300 TND a post**, micro (10–50k) 300–1,200, mid 1,200–5,000, macro 5,000–25,000. An Instagram story costs from 100 TND and a 15–30 s TikTok from 200 TND. The guide says 5–10 micro-creators beat one macro-creator. [8]
- Radio (Mosaïque FM) publishes no rate card. [9] The only broadcast benchmark found is 2016 TV at 700–3,000 TND per 30-second spot. [10]
- Printers quote flyers, stickers and banners on request only. [11]

## Payments

- **5.85M bank cards** (end 2025), but only **4.7% of card value is spent online**. [12] Mobile payments grew 81% in 2025 and are still below one transaction per person a year. [13]
- **Cash on delivery carries about 68% of e-commerce** (industry guide). [14] Only **8.7% of Tunisians buy online** (CONECT, June 2026). [15]
- Wallets (Flouci, D17, Konnect) charge merchants about 1.5–3.5% plus 0.2–0.3 TND. [14] Flouci had 67,000 active users in December 2024. [16]
- Tunisians cannot pay foreign shops for goods. See `tunisia-business-law.md`.

## Food delivery market

- **Glovo** is the near-monopoly: 8 cities (including Tunis and Sfax). [17] It has 1,400+ partners (98% local SMEs) [18], about 2,000 self-employed couriers, and ~2% of orders hit a problem. [19] Tunisia was Glovo's fastest-growing market in 2024. [19] Its customer service fee is 7–8%. [20]
- **Yassir Express** is #2 [21] and bought KooL in June 2024. [22] Regional players include **Zigzag** (Sfax HQ, "same prices as in the restaurant", −50% launch coupon) [23], and small local apps such as MenuTium (near-dormant). [24]
- **Exits:** Jumia Food closed on 15 Dec 2023 [25], citing aggressive rivals with deep pockets. [27] Jumia's whole Tunisian e-commerce arm followed on 31 Dec 2024. [28] Bolt was struck off the register on 24 March 2025 and its ride-hailing app shut on 9 May 2025; Bolt Food never launched here. [29][30]
- **Market size** (May 2024): about 60M TND a year and **6,000–7,000 orders a day** across all major platforms. [31] The best-sellers are sandwiches (chicken), tacos, tuna pizza, makloub and salads. [31]
- **Commissions:** no Tunisian rate is published anywhere (Glovo's Tunisian pricing page lists services but no percentage). [32] Internationally headline rates cluster at 15–30%, and effective rates run higher once fees are netted; 15% is the challenger floor (Jahez, Yassir Algeria, DoorDash Basic). [33][34][35][36]
- **Riders:** Glovo ran about 1 courier per 1.75–2.25 partner restaurants (2023–24). [18][37] The July–August 2026 strike demands are in `tunisia-business-law.md`.
- **Ramadan:** Tunis restaurants run iftar delivery menus. [38] Saudi platforms see +62% daily orders, peaking after iftar. [39]

## E-commerce and parcels

- Jumia left Tunisia (Tunisia was 2.7% of group orders). [28]
- **Carriers:** Intigo, Aramex, First Delivery and others. Professional prices run **4–9 TND per parcel**. [40]
- **Intigo** (intigo.net and its guides, read 19 Nov 2026): next day in 24 governorates from 11 warehouses. [41]
  - Small parcel **9.00 TND incl. VAT**, down to 6.50 by the shipper's recent volume; L 20 TND, XL 40 TND.
  - **Returns are free at 5% or less of an account's parcels over 30 days, then 4 TND each.** The rate counts per shipper account. A refused small parcel costs about 14 TND (9 + 4 + 1).
  - The merchant prints the label before pickup; one account holds several pickup addresses; cash is payable the day after the courier hands it to Intigo, on the merchant's request. Partner API with webhooks; members invited with permissions.
  - Not published: delivery attempts before return, days held, COD remittance delay, and the API terms. Put them in the contract.

## Wages

- A field sales rep earns 800–1,200 TND gross as a beginner and 1,200–2,000 at 3–5 years, with a variable that can double it. A key-account manager earns 3,000–7,000 TND. A confirmed rep's all-in cost is about **2,000–3,500 TND a month** (estimate from these sources). [42][43]
