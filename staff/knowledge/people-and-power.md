# People and power: a defensive reading

How to read people and recognise power moves: judging a partner, a manager, a hire or a supplier, and handling a conflict. Built only from Robert Greene's free interviews, opened 21 Nov 2026. His three books are paid and are not used. Lines marked "Application" are our own reasoning, not his. Rewritten 21 Nov 2026.

## Caveat first

- Greene's laws come from historical anecdotes, not tested findings. Where research (Kahneman, Cialdini) covers the same ground, the research is the stronger source. This is our judgement, not a claim from his interviews.
- Many of his tactics are manipulation and fail the honesty filter in `persuasion-and-offers.md`. Use the ideas only to recognise a move others make on the company, never on customers, suppliers, partners or staff.

## Greene on human nature (interviews)

- **We deny our nature.** Greene says "the primary law of human nature is to deny that we have human nature". People also oversimplify and trace behaviour to a single cause. Source: Daily Stoic interview, no date shown on the page, https://dailystoic.com/robert-greene/ (opened 21 Nov 2026).
- **Emotion comes first.** "Generally in life, we are slaves to our emotions", and rationality is an ideal. Same interview. Application: sleep on any hire or commitment made in a heated moment.
- **We judge others harder than ourselves.** He notes that we give ourselves context for our own behaviour and tend to call others bad people. Same interview. Application: before you label a partner, list what you do not know about their situation.
- **Empathy is an exercise.** He says to see in other people the same nature you have: irrational and aggressive impulses, and fear of death. Same interview.
- **Impulses and wishful thinking.** He says people have aggressive impulses, are largely irrational by nature, like the path of least resistance and find flattering pictures of themselves seductive. Source: Ryan Holiday's interview in Quillette, 1 Jan 2019, https://quillette.com/2019/01/01/stop-assuming-that-everything-you-feel-or-think-is-right-an-interview-with-robert-greene/ (opened 21 Nov 2026; the rest of the page is paywalled and was not used). Application: an offer that dangles an easy, impossible return gets more scrutiny, not less.
