# Principles: Fadi Ghandour

Built from the same 44 ok sources. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. Build the team first; nobody makes it alone. Quote: "I haven't met a single entrepreneur that has made it alone it is always about the team" (S046). Also "we don't like single single single founders" (S049). Sources: S010, S046, S049, S062 (4). High. Added in top-up: Also: "البناء لا يمكن ان يكون من شخص واحد لوحده" (S089) [building cannot come from one person alone]. New sources: S067, S077, S089.
2. Conserve cash; bootstrap. Quote: "Bootstrap is the key word here. Preserve your cash by spending where it matters to build the business." (S010). Sources: S010, S046, S059 (3). High. Added in top-up: Also: "اول 15 سنه من حياتي العمليه كان عندي صعوبه في النوم" (S089) [the first 15 years I had trouble sleeping over liquidity]. New sources: S089.
3. Do not own what you can rent or partner. Quote: "asset based businesses are expensive you have to borrow money" (S062). Sources: S045, S055, S062 (3). High. Added in top-up: Also: "نحن كنا نقول نحن نملك نملك العلاقه مع العميل" (S088) [we used to say we own the relationship with the client]. New sources: S088.
4. Go regional from day one. Quote: "from day one we went Regional and established a regional presence" (S060). Sources: S050, S060, S018 (3). High. Added in top-up: Also: "we don't invest in companies that are country specific" (S072). New sources: S072, S078, S082, S089.
5. Be a long-run builder; refuse the investor who talks quick returns. Quote: "entrepreneurs are Builders Builders you need to know that you are Builder there is no hit and run" (S046). Sources: S046, S049, S062 (3). High. Added in top-up: Also: "don't build to exit built to last" (S082); "not everything needs to be sold" (S070); "if he starts talking to me about exits I'm not going to invest in him" (S067). New sources: S067, S070, S082.
6. Keep knocking on doors. Quote: "The doors closed, we kept knocking until they opened." . Sources: S010, S046, S055, S062 (4). High. Added in top-up: Also: "فنحن ل 14 سنه نطرق الباب 14 سنه نطرق الباب بدنا ترخيص بدنا ترخيص بدنا ترخيص" (S088) [for 14 years we kept knocking on the door, we want a licence]. New sources: S088, S089.
7. Deliver; loyalty follows delivery. Quote: "loyalty comes with delivery" (S055). Also "Always under promise, over deliver." (S064). Sources: S032, S052, S055, S063, S064 (5). High. Added in top-up: Also: "الواحد مش لازم يوعد اكثر من ما هو قادر" (S089) [one does not have to promise more than one can]; "ما توعد به توفي به" (S089) [what you promise you fulfil]. New sources: S077, S089.
8. Do not wait on regulators. Quote: "Don't worry about government regulations. Go and do it." . Sources: S049, S066 (2). Medium. Note the tension: he spent 14 years getting a Saudi licence (S060). Added in top-up: Newer tellings keep the tension: "if they closed us they will close us the morning we will open in the afternoon" (S070) against 14 years for the Saudi licence (S088). Regulators first refuse, then give way once customers ask: "والمشرع بقول لك لا في الاول بعدين بيجد انه الخدمه مهمه" (S088) [the regulator says no at first, then finds the service is important]. New sources: S070, S088.
9. Find a mentor and give your own time as a mentor. Quote: "while capital is important, what they need the most is advice." . Sources: S046, S050, S060, S064 (4). High.
10. Hire energy over pedigree. Quote: "I would definitely employ somebody that has that that frame of mind over over a Harvard Graduate" (S066). Sources: S055, S062, S064, S066 (4). High. Added in top-up: Also: "we hired hustlers, we hired people from the region" (S070); "mostly we employ on character we employ on inquisitiveness" (S067). New sources: S067, S070, S083.
11. Share ownership with the team. Quote: "Most of our leaders at Aramex have 10 to 15 years in the company and all own stock." . Sources: S046, S062 (2). Medium. Added in top-up: Also "I wanted to make sure that it's not an Institutional organization that becomes slow and lazy because it's big" (S077). New sources: S077.
12. Push decisions to the front line. Quote: "command and control doesn't work" (S062). Sources: S011, S057, S062 (3). High. Added in top-up: Also: "that's Frontline empowerment" (S076); "ثقافه الشركه كانت اهم شغ اهم شيء عملناه بالشركه والثقافه هي العميل اولا" (S089) [company culture was the most important thing we did, and the culture is the customer first]. New sources: S076, S089.
13. A company is judged by its stakeholders, not only its shareholders. Quote: "Will you be profit maximizers only, or will you be people that think of stakeholders as a whole?" (S061). Sources: S016, S060, S061 (3). High. Added in top-up: Also: "the business of business is not only business impacts society and it it needs to impact it positively" (S083); "the stakeholder view of how you manage your organization should be and is becoming even more at the core of this story" (S068). New sources: S068, S074, S077, S081, S083.
14. Buy small. Quote: "we will buy from the small one" (S050). Sources: S016, S022, S050 (3). High. Added in top-up: Also: "افتحوا الابواب" (S089) [open the doors, to public procurement for local startups]; procure from SMEs and startups by policy (S068). New sources: S068, S089.
15. Invest when the market is slow. Quote: "the best time to invest is when the market is slow and nobody is is is running" (S045). Sources: S045 (1). Low.
16. Failure is part of the first years; the speed of failing matters. Quote: "the issue is how fast you're going to fail" (S046). Sources: S046, S049, S060 (3). High. Added in top-up: Also: "الفشل علمني انه هو الطريق الى النجاح" (S089) [failure taught me that it is the road to success]. New sources: S067, S089.
17. If you are profitable, stay private. Quote: "if you're profitable stay private" (S064). Sources: S064 (1). Low. Contrast with his own NASDAQ listing (S055). Added in top-up: Newer: "نحن ادرجنا بسبب احتجنا الفلوس" (S089) [we listed because we needed the money]; do not rush "ما تستعجل انك تدخل السوق قبل ما كون عندك اول شيء الحوكمه" (S088) [do not rush into the market before you first have governance]. Now 3 sources, so confidence high; his own listing story is S055, S083 (dates differ, see stories). New sources: S088, S089.
18. Stay when it is hard. Quote: "Leaders are the people that are going to show up, that are going to stick around" (S064). Sources: S064, S059, S043 (3; S043 inferred). High. Added in top-up: Also: "nobody leaves the the ship when there is rough Seas" (S077); "القيادي ما بيترك يوم الازمه" (S088) [the leader does not leave on a day of crisis]; "ارامكس سمعتها اليوم هي بسبب بقائها" (S089) [Aramex's reputation today is because of its staying]. New sources: S077, S088, S089.
19. Small deals first. Quote: "we need to think about the $2 million deer rather than the $200 million" (S060). Sources: S060 (1). Low.
20. Do not trust your own hype. Quote: "don't fall for the hype certainly not yours." (S010). Sources: S010 (1). Low. Added in top-up: Also: "don't fall into the hype" (S082). Now 2 sources, medium. New sources: S082.
21. Let the next generation lead. Quote: "you need to actually let the second generation come and rule" (S062). Sources: S055, S062, S064 (3). High. Added in top-up: Also: "have a deep bench an incredible bunch of people that can uh actually carry building the business with me" (S077). Counter-note: he calls one succession a failure, two strong people who could not work together, one left after about 22 years (S089). New sources: S070, S077, S080, S088, S089.
22. Quality comes from competition, so avoid monopoly and keep partners honest. Quote: "if you have a monopoly quality is going to deteriorate drastically" (S062). Also "we want competition to be the last statement on who stays and who doesn't" (S052). Sources: S052, S062 (2). Medium. Added in top-up: Also: "we love competition they keep us very Innovative and very creative" (S081). New sources: S081.
23. Never vertically integrate. Quote: "never never never never never" (S062). Also: "not because we were geniuses just because we didn't have enough capital" (S062). Sources: S062, S055 (2). Medium.
24. Be there for the long term of a community. Quote: "Change takes a long time, nothing happens overnight. You need to be in it for the long-term, you cannot just hit and run." (S012). Sources: S012, S053, S063 (3). High. Added in top-up: Also: "this is not charity this is not me saying I feel sorry for you and I want to help you" (S077); "if we have the means, shame on us that we take other people's money to solve our own problems" (S083). New sources: S077, S080, S081, S083, S091.

## Added in the top-up (S067 to S091)
25. Ask first how the company crosses borders; do not back a single-country company. Quote: "we don't invest in companies that are country specific" (S072). Also "the first question that we ask is how are you gonna scale in the region" (S078); "to create jobs you need to create open markets you're never ever going to create jobs by addressing the issue locally" (S078). Sources: S072, S078, S082 (3). High.
26. Solve the local payment problem, not what New York needs. Quote: "I'm solving for my neighbor. So, if he needs to pay cash on delivery, I'm going to create that problem" (S083). Also "اخترعنا شوب ان شيب" (S088) [we invented Shop and Ship]. Sources: S076, S078, S083, S088 (4). High.
27. Serve only the markets you can serve and where there is a gap. Quote: "انا اخدم الاسواق اللي قادر على اخدم واللي فيها نقص" (S088) [I serve the markets I can serve and where there is a gap]. Also "في دول نحن قادرين وفي دول قررنا عدم الدخول فيها" (S088) [there are countries where we can, and countries we decided not to enter]. Sources: S088, S089 (2; S089 says the Arab world of 400 to 500 million rewards whoever understands it). Medium.
28. Hire the founder who failed, honestly, over the corporate star. Quote: "I would always invest in a failed entrepreneur in his second startup" (S067). Also "you're the first one to be hired in any business that I do" (S074). Sources: S067, S074, S076 (3). High. He draws the line between an honest failure and something wrong in the company: "this guy failed honestly" (S070).
29. Enter a new place through a local person, not alone. Quote: "there is no copy and paste you have to customize and adapt to the local Challenge and we have to partner with local with the local community" (S077). Sources: S077, S080, S081 (3; S080 is a cut of S077). Medium.
30. Open borders and harmonise the laws before asking for a Silicon Valley. Quote: "when people say how can we replicate Silicon Valley I say open up the borders allow people money and goods to travel freely across our economies" (S082). Also "خلينا نلاقي اي عوامل مشتركه يمكن ان نوحد القوانين لنمكن شركاتنا الناشئه في التكنولوجيا لان تتوسع داخل العالم العربي باقل عوائق ممكنه" (S089) [let us find any common factors where we can unify laws so that our tech startups can expand inside the Arab world with the fewest obstacles possible]. Sources: S072, S078, S082, S089 (4). High.
31. Do not take foreign aid for your own problems. Quote: "you don't need to wait for foreign aid to solve your problems" (S081). Also "if we have the means, shame on us that we take other people's money to solve our own problems" (S083). Sources: S081, S083 (2). Medium.
32. A board dissents; a director who will only cheer should leave. Quote: "our job is to be nasty. Our job is to ask the difficult questions." (S070). Sources: S070 (1). Low.
33. Do the menial work yourself. Quote: "if you don't clean the warehouse you're not gonna be able to serve your clients properly" (S082). Also "كل مؤسس لشركه لما تكون صغيره بدك تكون تعملي كل شيء" (S089) [every founder of a company, when it is small, you have to do everything]. Sources: S082, S089 (2). Medium.
34. Pay or work with the monopoly you cannot beat, and expect the regulator to say no first. Quote: "والمشرع بقول لك لا في الاول بعدين بيجد انه الخدمه مهمه" (S088) [and the regulator says no at first, then finds the service is important]. Also "المؤسسات البريديه الوطنيه كانت تقولنا انتم غير قانونيين" (S089) [the national postal institutions used to tell us you are illegal]. Sources: S070, S088, S089 (3). High.
35. A founder of a listed company does not sell shares in a crisis or at will. Quote: "لانه في الدقيقه اللي انت بتقول فيها انا بدي ابيع اسهمي السوق حعاقبك" (S089) [because in the minute you say I want to sell my shares the market will punish you]. Also "كنت اشتري لما كان السهم ينزل" (S089) [I bought when the share fell]. Sources: S089 (1; S055 says he never sold until after 30 years). Low.
