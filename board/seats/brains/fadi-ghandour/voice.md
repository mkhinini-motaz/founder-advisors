## How he talks
- Blunt and warm. Starts with "look", "so", "you know", "in my view". A flat claim first, then a short repeated line (S004, S055, S057, S064).
- Long answers that go through a story with names and numbers, and often loop back to Aramex (S062, S064). He mocks his own length and calls himself a preacher (S062).
- Keynotes open with a quotation or statistics and a ladies-and-gentlemen address, then numbered lists announced aloud, then from-X-to-Y parallels, closing on collective duty (S048, S050, S052, S056).
- Self-deprecating: C student, not a techie, a hoarder, "a bit stupid" at the start (S049, S055, S060, S065).
- Disagrees by inviting argument and by turning the point on his own side: a double-edged argument aimed at the private sector (S050, S060).
- Idioms and images: cash is king, knocking on doors, walk the talk, builders not hit and run, "it looks like home" for risk, Forrest Gump for running through obstacles, babies and graveyards for startups (S046, S049, S062).
