# Principles: Noureddine Tayebi

Built from 18 sources (quick depth, thin). Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. Start with a service that meets an immediate need, then add payments once trust exists. "they solve important and immediate needs and of course if we execute well we could have a very large user base" (S046). Sources: 12. Confidence: high.
2. Build what the market wants, never force a product on it. "always build product that the market wants and not the uh not the opposite" (S020). Sources: 2 (S020, S034 by inference). Confidence: medium.
3. Know your customer from day one. "il faut que dès le début tu parles à ton client potentiel" (S034); "you really need to know who your customer is and build your product based on the culture of the region" (S021). Sources: 2. Confidence: medium.
4. Unit economics come before profit. "le plus important c'est les unités économiques" (S034). Sources: 4 (S017, S019, S034, S046). Confidence: high.
5. Raising money is fine while unit economics are healthy. "as long as you know like the unit economics of your business are healthy it it honestly doesn't matter" (S046). Sources: 3 (S034, S046, S017). Confidence: high.
6. Capital is global, so raise it where it is. "the capital is global uh it's not just local" (S027); "Capital now is U uh is global" (S020). Sources: 3 (S020, S027, S048). Confidence: high.
7. Do not wait for help or for a solved system. "if you're waiting for a red carpet then you'll you'll never be able to uh to do anything" (S020). Sources: 6. Confidence: high.
8. Work the supply side first. "c'est beaucoup plus important en fait de travailler sur sur le côté se plat et d'un côté chauffeur que sur le côté client" (S044). Sources: 1. Confidence: low.
9. Check customer quality, not just count. "est-ce que il reviennent que que lorsqu'il y a des subventions" (S044). Sources: 1. Confidence: low.
10. Never compromise hiring quality. "we cannot compromise in the quality of the hiring that we do" (S019). Sources: 2 (S019, S044). Confidence: medium.
11. Give the team shares: about 15 percent. "about like 15% of the total shares of the company uh go go to the team members" (S019); "toute personne qui participe dans dans le succès de quelque chose doit avoir une partie de ce succès" (S044). Sources: 4 (S019, S040, S043, S044). Confidence: high.
12. Do not sell: it would be a failure against the mission. "se faire acquérir c'est c'est vraiment c'est un petit peu un échec" (S044). Sources: 4 (S040, S044, S048, S037). Confidence: high.
13. Do not raise money you cannot deliver on. "if you're if you don't have the basis to actually make it happen, it just it's not only going to fire back at you" (S027). Sources: 1. Confidence: low.
14. Stay frugal because hard fundraising years force it. "That pushed us to be frugal and conscious of unit economics, profitability and burn rate." (S017). Sources: 2 (S017, S019). Confidence: medium.
15. Keep trusting even after betrayal. "il faut jamais s'arrêter de faire confiance aux gens" (S034). Sources: 2 (S019, S034). Confidence: medium.
16. Close access when people leave. Inferred from S037 (the false notification story). Confidence: low.
17. Expand surgically, not broadly. "we're not even City by City we're we're District by district" (S019). Sources: 2 (S019, S034). Confidence: medium.
18. The market must be big enough to repay entry, or adapt the service first. "حجم السوق يعني أمر مهم جدا" (S048). Sources: 1. Confidence: low.
19. Keep metrics visible. "okr and kpis that we include Even in our bi so you know like they're very visible and we can you know like tell what's happening immediately" (S019). Sources: 3 (S019, S020, S034). Confidence: high.
20. Hire six months ahead where notice periods are long. "si je veux recruter une personne d'ici trois mois il faut que je commence 6 mois plus tôt" (S044). Sources: 1. Confidence: low.

21. Do not wait for a red carpet; jump off the cliff. "if you're waiting for a red carpet for you to be made uh you're you're never going to be an entrepreneur" (S051). Also S020. Sources: 4 (S020, S049, S051 and the cliff definition in S049). Confidence: high.
22. Build what people tell you they need and ship the simplest version early. "don't build something that you think people want build something that people tell you I want or I need" (S051). Sources: 3 (S020, S034, S051). Confidence: high.
23. Pay every contributor a share of the success. "it's really important that anyone that actually participates in the success of the business should have that share of success" (S051); "c'est pas une faveur que je donne c'est en fait c'est leur droit" (S052). About 20 percent of Yassir is team-owned (S051, S052); vesting applies to him too (S050, S051). Sources: 7 with the earlier ones. Confidence: high.
24. Widen your network and get a warm introduction. "il faut toujours essayer d'avoir un réseau et de toujours l'élargir" (S052). Sources: 2 (S050 paraphrase, S052). Confidence: medium.
25. Think big; ask first whether the model repeats in other countries. "il faut toujours voir grand" (S052). Sources: 3 (S050, S052, S053). Confidence: high.
26. Help others without asking for anything back. "ça va contribuer à ma réussite aussi" (S049). Sources: 4 (S049, S050, S052, S053). Confidence: high.
27. Keep a global view of the business even as a technologist. "on n pas besoin de tout connaître mais mais on a besoin de d'avoir ce c'est" (S049). Sources: 2 (S049, S050). Confidence: medium.
28. Have a technical co-founder if the product is software. Source: S050 (paraphrase). Confidence: low.
29. Pick active investors who bring network and customers. Source: S050 (paraphrase). Confidence: low.
30. Prefer a small share of a very large company. Source: S050 (paraphrase). Confidence: low.
31. If someone is not good, let them go. Source: S051 (paraphrase of the passage). Confidence: low.
32. Do not start a company while at school; finish the degree first. Source: S051, with his own "I could be wrong". Confidence: low.
33. Be quick to market; someone else may be building the same idea. "فالوقت يعني للـ للواحد يوصل للسوق يبقى يبقى أمر مهم" (S053) [time to reach the market stays important]. Sources: 1. Confidence: low.
34. Think in decades for society and in milestones for a company; do not be in a hurry. S052 (Algeria since 1962 is nothing) and S050 (150 years for Silicon Valley). Confidence: medium.
