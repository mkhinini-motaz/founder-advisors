# Stories: Barry Schwartz

Built from 36 sources, 2010 to 2026. One or two lines each; paraphrase, no quote marks except where verbatim.

1. **Jam study (Iyengar and Lepper).** 24 or 30 jams drew more visitors, but one tenth as many bought as with 6. Lesson: variety attracts, then paralyzes. He does not say it was replicated (S002, S003, S005, S006, S008, S010, S013).
2. **Gap jeans.** He spent 40 minutes to an hour, got the best-fitting jeans he had ever had, and felt worse because expectations rose. "In other words, I did better and I felt worse." (S002). Lesson: doing better can feel worse. Also S003, S005, S006, S014, S041, S042.
3. **401(k) at Vanguard.** About a million employees; for every 10 funds added, participation fell 2 points; 50 funds meant 10 percent fewer joined than 5. Lesson: well-meant options cost people the match (S002, S003, S006, S033, S041, S042).
4. **Organ donation defaults.** About 26 percent opt-in versus near 100 percent opt-out. Lesson: the default triples the result at no cost (S003, S006, S011, S033).
5. **Medicare Part D.** 50 to 90 plans; seniors would not sign up for a gift. Lesson: paralysis blocks free money (S003, S006).
6. **Staples catalogue and the home builder.** Staples cut options to save postage and sales rose; a design-centre builder cut options, time fell from 20 hours to four, upgrades rose. Lesson: accidental evidence; firms cut for cost reasons (S002, S006, S008).
7. **Speed dating and the pen study.** 12 partners gave fewer matches than 6; adding a second pen option cut take-up from 75 to 45 percent. Lesson: missed opportunities make each option worse (S002).
8. **Obama's few suits.** Michael Lewis asked; too many decisions a day to pick a suit. Lesson: shut options in areas you do not care about (S005, S010).
9. **Netflix refused his research.** The company said there was nothing he could do that they had not thought of. Lesson: data-driven firms miss what psychology adds (S028). Also people scroll for an hour and rewatch The Office (S011).
10. **Israeli daycare fine.** Fines for late pickup raised lateness (25 to 33, then 40 and 50 percent) and it stayed after the fine was removed. Lesson: a price drives out a duty (S022, S023, S033).
11. **Swiss nuclear dump.** About 50 percent said yes unpaid, 25 percent when paid six weeks' salary. Lesson: money recasts civic duty as self-interest (S023, S033).
12. **Hospital janitors (Wrzesniewski).** Some follow the list; others do whatever helps the patient and family. Lesson: the mission, not the list (S012, S031, S033, S040).
13. **Mike's Hard Lemonade.** A father bought a drink for his young son at a ballgame; everyone said "we have to follow procedure" and the boy went to foster care. Lesson: rules without judgment (S033, S040).
14. **Chicago kindergarten script.** A scripted lesson for a short book. Lesson: scripts assure mediocrity and wise teachers leave (S033, S040).
15. **Groopman's patients.** A doctor first told the full truth and crushed patients, then hid it and left them unable to arrange affairs; years of practice found a patient-by-patient balance. Lesson: wisdom is learned by erring (S015, S017, S031).
16. **His own life as luck.** A seventh-grade move met his future wife; intro psychology fitted his schedule; Swarthmore was the only job he applied for; a stranger asked for the TED talk. Lesson: he calls himself a lucky man (S029, S035, S028).
17. **TED talk in shorts.** He gave it with ten minutes of prep, it reached about 25 million views; when he dressed up for the second, people said they were disappointed. Lesson: you can't win (S018, S029).
