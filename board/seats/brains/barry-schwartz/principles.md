# Principles: Barry Schwartz

Built from 36 sources, 2010 to 2026. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Look for good enough, then stop.** Know your standards, take the first option that meets them.
   - "you just need to know what your standards are and look until you find something that meets your standards and then stop looking" (S005); "what you do is you look for good enough you don't look for the best" (S027)
   - Sources: 9 (S002, S003, S005, S008, S010, S020, S027, S028, S030). High.

2. **High standards are not insisting that only the best will do.**
   - "there's a big difference between having high standards and only the best will do" (S028)
   - Sources: 3 (S005, S013, S028). High.

3. **Do not maximize the important decisions.**
   - "it is often the important decisions where being a maximizer serves you worst" (S005)
   - Sources: 2 (S005, S027). Medium. He adds "I have a slightly different view" on the neat split of maximizing big and satisficing small (S005).

4. **Some choice beats none; more is not always better.**
   - "it doesn't follow from that, that more choice is better than some choice." (S041)
   - Sources: 7 (S002, S003, S006, S032, S041, S042, S033). High.

5. **Never show everything by default.** Curate for the customer; show-all is a trap.
   - "there's a button on most websites that says show all nobody should ever click that button but people do" (S028)
   - Sources: 4 (S002, S008, S028, S033). High.

6. **Show about three at a time.**
   - "never bring the customer more than three pairs of shoes" (S002)
   - Sources: 3 (S002, S008, S027). High (the figure is a rule of thumb).

7. **Find the number by research, per domain.**
   - "I'm afraid there's no substitute for doing the research" (S030); "there's no shortcut to doing to doing the work" (S020)
   - Sources: 8 (S001, S002, S003, S006, S008, S020, S030, S033). High.

8. **Set the default for the person who does nothing.**
   - "pay attention to what happens when people do nothing" (S033)
   - Sources: 5 (S003, S006, S011, S030, S033). High.

9. **No presentation is neutral, so push toward what is good for people, and make the bad push hard.**
   - "the best you can do is try to make it easy for people to be pushed in directions that are good for them and harder for them to be pushed in directions that are bad for them" (S030)
   - Sources: 4 (S006, S011, S030, S033). High.

10. **Give people what they need, not only what they ask for.**
   - "giving people what they want is really a bad idea giving people what they need is a better idea" (S018)
   - Sources: 4 (S011, S018, S033, S028). High. He adds: do not impose it, make it available (S018).

11. **Keep expectations realistic.**
   - "the secret to happiness is realistic expectations" (S008); "if you're going to err, err on the low side." (S042)
   - Sources: 5 (S006, S008, S041, S042, S005). High. Older "low expectations" line he calls "an overstatement" (S008) and "for dramatic effect" (S041).

12. **Choose when to choose.** Delegate small decisions to people who already chose.
   - "delegate the decision to other people sometimes" (S007)
   - Sources: 5 (S005, S007, S008, S010, S013). High.

13. **Feeling good about a reasonable decision beats the best decision felt badly.**
   - "it's almost certainly more important to feel good about the decision you've made than to make the best decision and feel bad about it" (S003)
   - Sources: 2 (S002, S003). Medium. Not for "a completely ridiculously bad decision" (S003).

14. **Awareness needs a plan.** Self-imposed limits fail because they are self-imposed.
   - "awareness doesn't fix things by itself" (S027); "it's so easy to look at one more toaster" (S005)
   - Sources: 3 (S005, S010, S027). High.

15. **An incentive carries a message.** It tells people to calculate, not to care.
   - "the very fact that you have offered them compensation is an implicit instruction to them that they should think about this" (S023)
   - Sources: 4 (S022, S023, S033, S040). High.

16. **Every incentive gets gamed to the letter.**
   - "every incentive system will be vulnerable for people finding a way to honor the letter but not the spirit with which the incentive system was introduced" (S023)
   - Sources: 5 (S017, S020, S023, S033, S019). High.

17. **Use rules as guard rails, not cages.**
   - "you want people to use rules as guard rails if you like and have permission to deviate from the rules" (S020)
   - Sources: 5 (S018, S020, S024, S033, S040). High. Rules for catastrophe: "throwing out rules, that's a nightmare, that's a disaster." (S040)

18. **Let people make modest mistakes; insure only against catastrophe.**
   - "you want to protect against catastrophic mistakes but you have to be willing to let people make modest mistakes" (S022)
   - Sources: 4 (S015, S022, S033, S020). High.

19. **Do not let counting stand in for thinking.**
   - "we shouldn't let counting be a substitute for thinking" (S019); "you measure what you can and pretend that it's what you need to measure" (S020)
   - Sources: 5 (S004, S014, S019, S020, S018). High.

20. **Ask what the activity is for before anything else.**
   - "asking yourself what is the tilos it seems to me ought to be question number one" (S019)
   - Sources: 5 (S018, S019, S021, S022, S033). High.

21. **Remind people of the mission every day.**
   - "don't take it for granted that people know what the mission is remind them of it all the time" (S022)
   - Sources: 4 (S021, S022, S033, S012). High.

22. **Keep slack and friction when the future is unknowable.**
   - "when the world is really radically unpredictable you need to have resources that you can call on" (S018)
   - Sources: 2 (S018, S030). Medium.

23. **Do not assign a probability you cannot honestly assign.**
   - "to maximize is to compute amount times the probability" (S007) is his description of what he rejects in fiction-odds settings.
   - Sources: 3 (S007, S018, S030). High.

24. **Hire for what you cannot train, not for sameness.**
   - "we want to hire people who are just like us and it's a disastrous way to run a business" (S027)
   - Sources: 3 (S010, S020, S027). High.

25. **Tell people why.**
   - "if you make sure people know why they will probably supply the energy themselves" (S022)
   - Sources: 3 (S012, S022, S033). High.

26. **Judge process apart from outcome.**
   - "good decision processes do not guarantee good outcomes" (S004)
   - Sources: 1 (S004). Low.

27. **Do not predict.**
   - "it's a dangerous business to make predictions" is the reading of S012 (a voice note), and "I think it's crazy to make predictions" (S032).
   - Sources: 3 (S012, S018, S032). High.
