# Positions: Barry Schwartz

Built from 36 sources dated 2010 to 2026. Each stance has a verbatim quote, source id and date. No recorded position means the corpus has nothing; do not guess. Newer views are marked.

## Choice and products

1. **How many options to offer.** No universal number; start with 8 to 10 variants and test. "as a rule of thumb if you're creating a new product i would create eight or ten variants on that product" (S003, 2014). Later he doubts it: "I caution you not to take this too seriously" (S001, 2023). "Somewhere between 6 and 10 everybody seems to be able to find one that they're satisfied with." (S042, 2013).
2. **Focus versus diversification.** Stop diversifying where customers start to be confused. "where is the point where you start confusing frustrating" (S001, 2023). Variety also wastes material resources (S001).
3. **Niche and odd tastes.** More choice helps the odd-taste shopper. "if you have odd tastes uh you're probably going to be better off in a restaurant that has 40 dishes than in one that has 10" (S006, 2012).
4. **Connoisseurs.** "if you love cars if you're really into cars um then there's a certain sense in which there can't be too many options" (S005, 2023).
5. **Why firms keep adding variety.** Shelf-space wars and inventory managers, plus the arms race. "it's just an arms race" (S006, 2012); "I wish I knew." (S002, 2017).
6. **Lost sales.** Missing items are salient, overload is invisible. "the problem of too much choice when people are buying is less easy to detect" (S005, 2023).
7. **Digital age.** "it is now orders of magnitude worse" (S005, 2023); earlier "it's true to the fourth power" (S011, 2021). In 2025: "the problem I wrote about in 2004 would have become an order of magnitude worse" (S032).
8. **Curation and recommendation.** "you can turn any online portal into a boutique" (S028, 2019). Newer caution: "every option you see is a serious candidate" (S032, 2025).
9. **Packages.** "it's a choice among three options" (S008, 2024).
10. **Trusting reviewers.** Prefer non-profit reviewers that explain criteria. "they are not beholden to the product" (S008, 2024).
11. **Pricing and discounting.** No recorded position on pricing a product. On paying for a duty: "Is it worth 15 bucks for me to spend another half hour in the office? Damn right it is." (S033, 2011).
12. **Marketing and paid ads.** Companies profit from agonizing. "we're being pushed along by the companies that make money the more time we spend torturing ourselves, deciding what to buy" (S032, 2025). Algorithms are not worse than old marketing: "everybody knows that half of what we do is a complete waste of time and money" (S032).
13. **Brand.** No recorded position.
14. **Competition.** Producers are in an arms race (S006); no recorded position on a founder's competition.
15. **Focus groups.** "people can't tell you what's wrong and what's right" (S033, 2011). Watch what users do.

## Decisions, happiness, expectations

16. **Settling.** "settling is not a neutral description settling is a criticism" (S005, 2023); same in S004, S010, S030, S032.
17. **Expectations.** Older: the TED-talk line about low expectations, which he says was said for dramatic effect (S041, 2012). S005 has only the host quoting it and Schwartz agreeing. Newer: "that seemed maybe a little too pessimistic. So let me say the secret to happiness is to have realistic expectations." (S042, 2013); "that's a bit of an overstatement" (S008, 2024). The newer view stands.
18. **Close relations.** Older reading: limits of close ties as a price. Newer: "What I now think is that it is actually part of the benefit." (S002, 2017).
19. **Dating apps.** "I think it's terrible" (S027, 2020); "nobody wins in the battle against an infinite number of Alternatives" (S034, 2010). He says he has not done research on it (S007).
20. **Money and happiness.** Above about 75K extra income adds little (S002); "The single most important determinant of happiness is our network of friends, family, loved ones, community groups." (S002, 2017).
21. **Timing and luck.** "this persistent desire to be in control of everything deludes us into thinking that we have more control than we actually do" (S028, 2019).
22. **Burnout.** "you get burnout from feeling like you don't matter and you get burned out from caring so much" (S014, 2026).
23. **Remote work.** Always-on work spoils presence, such as at a child's soccer game (S003, S041, paraphrase). Otherwise no recorded position.
24. **Work hours.** Fine if the motive is making something good. No recorded position on a schedule (S005).
25. **Metrics.** "Be careful what you measure because what you measure is what you'll get." (S004, 2025). "once you've quantified something, it dominates the conversation." (S014, 2026).
26. **Product choices and the poor.** The problem needs a baseline of affluence: "it's a problem that most of the rest of the World wishes it had" (S013, 2015).

## Work, people, institutions

27. **Hiring.** "you hire for what you don't know how to train and then teach what you do know how to train" (S010, 2026).
28. **Culture and firing.** No recorded position on firing. On culture: "you should think of yourself running an organization as being the steward of all the stakeholders in the organization" (S012, 2020).
29. **Equity, raising money, debt, partners.** No recorded position. On owners: "if you own your company you can do whatever the hell you want but if your shareholders own your company you can't" (S023, 2012).
30. **Selling or going public.** Shareholder pressure drives short-termism; private owners have room (S010, S023). "the fatal mistake of agreeing that the job of the company is to maximize shareholder value" (S031, 2019).
31. **Incentives and pay.** "relying on incentives is virtually guaranteed to get the worse that people have to offer" (S012, 2020). "mostly I'd say incentives are bad." (S040, 2012). "rules and incentives have their place" (S022, 2014).
32. **Autonomy and trust.** "the more discretion and autonomy you give people the more they will earn it" (S012, 2020).
33. **AI.** "AI at the moment is simply a tool." (S032, 2025); "it does not replace judgment" (S010, 2026); "I think it's crazy to make predictions" (S032).
34. **Scripts and procedure.** "Scripts like these are insurance policies against disaster and they prevent disaster. But what they assure in its place is mediocrity." (S040, 2012).
35. **Change of view (humility).** After 2020: "it's just extraordinary arrogance to imagine that somebody who has lived a whole life in the position I've lived my life can adopt the perspective" (S024, 2021).
36. **Luck and merit.** "Working hard and playing by the rules is just no guarantee of anything." (S035, 2020); admissions lottery (S026, S028, S029, S035).
37. **Tech platforms.** "I think twitter is a a plague on modern society" (S020, 2021); Facebook features were "a deliberate misstep" (S018, 2021).
38. **Side projects.** No recorded position.
39. **Content and attention.** "you don't build up your attention muscle unless somebody forces you to pay sustained attention" (S018, 2021).
40. **Own limits.** "I'm an old man, though, you know, I I don't think you should take what I have to say that seriously." (S024, 2021).
