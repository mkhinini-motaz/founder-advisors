## Cash and profit

1. **Take profit from every deposit before you pay anything else, and run the business on the rest.**
   - "You take a percentage of those sales immediately stored to our profit and then the remainder is expenses." (S004)
   - "profit is not an event. It’s a habit." (S015)
   - Sources: 7 (S004, S010, S011, S014, S015, S021, S042). Confidence: high.

2. **If you cannot afford your bills after taking profit, the bills are too big.**
   - "If you can’t pay your bills as you take your Profit First, that’s your business telling you, you can’t afford your bills." (S011)
   - Sources: 2 (S011, S021). Confidence: medium.

3. **Judge health by cash in the bank, not by accounting profit.**
   - "Enron was profitable until the day they went under, right?" (S014)
   - "it’s an accounting profit, there’s really nothing in your bank account." (S004)
   - Sources: 3 (S004, S014, S021). Confidence: high.

4. **Revenue is not safety; profit is.**
   - Sources: 5 (S002, S004, S010, S011, S020). Confidence: high.

5. **Protect profit from yourself: a second bank, two people, no borrowing back.**
   - "I needed to protect myself from myself." (S014)
   - "The idea for that second bank is to make it as inconvenient as possible." (S014)
   - Sources: 2 (S014, S021). Confidence: medium.

6. **Never fund a startup or a gap with more money before the business has sold its way in.**
   - "It has to sell its way into existence without raising funds on it" (S010)
   - "The more money we have, the more we spend." (S010)
   - Sources: 3 (S010, S015, S014). Confidence: high.

7. **Pay debt down out of profit, and keep a small reward for the owner.**
   - "the shareholder must still experience a reward." (S014)
   - Sources: 3 (S004, S011, S014). Confidence: high.

8. **A price rise beats doubling sales.**
   - "If you could increase your pricing 20%, you’re doubling your profit." (S011)
   - Sources: 3 (S011, S042, S020). Confidence: high.

9. **Pay the owner a set wage on a schedule, never "what is left".**
   - Sources: 1 (S042). Confidence: low.

10. **Never size fixed costs to a peak month; budget around the lowest months.**
