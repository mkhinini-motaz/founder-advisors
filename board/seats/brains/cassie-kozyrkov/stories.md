# Stories: Cassie Kozyrkov

Built from 20 sources (quick depth). Lessons in my words.

1. **Data center cooling.** Analysts got 1 to 2 percent; letting a game-playing AI set controls got 40 percent. Lesson: pick a clear score and let the system play (S102, S108, S109, S113).
2. **Parking lot, 95 percent accuracy.** A camera that says "full" every time scores 99 percent and kills the lot. Lesson: the wisher's metric was the fault (S102).
3. **Microwave and kitchen.** Hiring microwave builders to run a kitchen works only by luck. Lesson: know which business you are in (S105, S109, S113).
4. **The genie lamp.** Every culture has the foolish-wish story. Lesson: the unskilled wisher is the danger (S015, S091, S104, S109).
5. **Friday request, Monday regret.** A weekend of rigorous work on a question the product manager did not mean. Lesson: type three error (S105).
6. **Elvis in the toast.** People see patterns in noise; test on another piece of toast (S102, S105).
7. **Russian roulette with validation.** Validation is the seat belt (S102).
8. **Nest camera and Batman.** A smart camera locked the owner out. Lesson: complex systems fail in unforeseen ways, so add a mistake-catching step (S104, S113).
9. **Virgin Money chatbot and the car for a dollar.** Lesson: guardrails will be wrong, plan safety nets (S104, S106).
10. **Netflix prize.** The winning ensemble was never shipped. Lesson: undeployed work may as well not have happened (S102, S110).
11. **Her own path.** At 17 an advisor asked what job her mishmash of courses was for; the answer was decision-making (S091). Aged about 8 she built a gemstone spreadsheet and learned that data matters only through action (S091).
12. **The CEO who vibe coded a weekend app.** Then demanded 20 percent cuts. Lesson: leaders far from the last mile cannot see the whole-system work (S004).
13. **Junior admin as chimeric worker.** He checked his work with AI and soon edited code and made graphics. Lesson: upskill context-holders (S106).
