# Positions: Tobi Lütke

Built from 11 sources, 2011 to 2026 (dates are source dates; S003 is a 2018 listing recorded later; S004, S012, S033, S035 dates are approximate). Topics without evidence say so.

## Pricing and fees
- Charge something; paying focuses the customer. "just the fact that you pay money for something focuses the mind in such a way that you really want to make a success out of it now." (S004, about 2010)
- Subscription plus low transaction fee beat a percentage of sales (see frameworks 3). On Shopify's $29 plan he says "It was $29." (S001, 2019) and calls it pride, not skill.
- Best feature to everyone: "We want the small business to compete on even ground of the biggest companies." (S035)

## Discounting
- No recorded position.

## Raising money and VC
- 2008 VCs rejected him and demanded a move to the Valley. "Once you tell me to do something, I don’t want it anymore." (S031, 2025)
- Friends and family: "I would never do friends and family investment like this again." (S004)
- Growth capital: "Then the people who have access to capital — they underinvest in growth." (S003)

## Debt
- "I'm German, so I don't take debt much" (S033, 2026)

## First hire and hiring
- Hire on high potential. "We hire people on future high potential and then try to get them there as fast as possible" (S001, 2019)
- Hire by life story, look for agency. "what we’re looking for is high agency experience." (S033, 2026)

## Firing
- A costly mistake is tuition once. "See it as your tuition." (S002, 2025)

## Partners and co-founders
- First co-founder wanted many products, he wanted one; they parted and he stays friendly (paraphrase, S004).
- Partnerships: "Tech is extraordinarily bad at partnerships" (S031, 2025)

## Equity and compensation
- "I'm a big fan of equity compensation." (S018, 2019)
- Newer (S033, S035): one agreed annual number, with sliders between cash and stock each quarter. "everyone gets a number, this is what you make every year" (S035)

## Focus versus diversification
- "finding one of us is good and not having to meander into some complete adjacency to support it is actually wonderful." (S031, 2025)

## Niche and going upmarket
- "You don’t have to go up market like the way everyone tells you to" (S001, 2019)
- "we are starting with SMB and then some of them grow and hopefully we build good software and then we invite the others over." (S031)

## Content and storytelling
- "We’re storytelling creatures." (S001) Snowdevil told stories of days on the mountain instead of a product grid.

## Paid ads
- Pay per click for Shopify itself: "it is a very boring type of advertising." (S004, about 2010)
- For merchants: "the Meta/Shopify alley-oop has created more businesses than any government policy in history." (S031, 2025)
- "personalized ads is a wonderful thing." (S031)

## Brand
- "We’re pushing other people’s brands from behind." (S002, 2025)

## AI
- 2024 recording: "I have an extremely bullish view on AI, specifically around utilitarian value." (S003)
- April 2025 memo: "Stagnation is almost certain, and stagnation is slow-motion failure." (S017) He admits the earlier call "was too much of a suggestion." (S017)
- December 2025, newer: "I just think AI is actually underhyped right now." (S002)
- January 2026, newer: "If AI wouldn’t have happened, I don’t think I would run Shopify anymore." (S033)

## Remote work and offices
- Digital by design was announced in 2020 (S035). Earlier view: "The Friday afternoon stand-up meeting is just one of the greatest accelerants for businesses." (S003)
- Office design: "No one can be more creative than the space around them" (S033)

## Work hours and burnout
- "I really just do like working a lot." (S002, 2025) In S035 he describes sleeping pills after two years of 14 to 16 hour days, fixed by CBT-I.

## Selling the company
- Doubts acquirers: nobody bought Shopify at $1 billion (S018). On M&A: "a lot of M&A (a surprisingly high percentage) is more like trophy hunting" (S018).

## Culture
- "If you take everyone’s favorite color and blend them together, you end up with mud brown" (S002)
- "I think you try to make your company as different from every other company as possible" (S031)

## Metrics
- Goals: "I don’t like goals, they’re overrated." (S037, 2013)
- Stock price: "the stock market is basically like a polling market." (S033)
- Support happiness: "We try to maintain a 90% happiness" (S012, 2011)

## Competition
- "We have been rivals at times, and I think we inspire each other" (S002, on Amazon)

## Timing and optimism
- "You’ve got to believe that the future is going to be better than today to want to make the future come sooner" (S003)
- Ignorance helps: "you should be ignorant to the low odds in the beginning" (S003)

## Open source and hosting
- Early (S004): hosted beat open source for customers. Later (S002): "The open source is fundamentally positive sum."

## Advice and business books
- "I think advice is overrated. I think gut instinct is underrated." (S002) Also: "the business books are largely written by the people who have time, who are not the people who actually build companies" (S033)

## Incentive pay and bonuses
- "I think that comes from a place of ignorance on how human motivation truly works." (S018, on sales-style incentives)

## Licensing and policy
- "I can get a laptop and start $15 billion Shopify and need to ask no one." (S001)

## Consumerism
- "The thing that solves consumerism is quality products which you want to keep using." (S031)

## Governance
- Dual-class: should move toward those with most context (paraphrase, S033).

## Pricing for agents / agentic commerce
- "my role in this is going to be largely into infrastructure providing." (S031)

## No recorded position
- Discounting; layoffs process; sales team design; branding spend; selling for cash flow. No recorded position.
