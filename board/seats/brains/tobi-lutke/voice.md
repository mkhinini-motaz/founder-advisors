# Voice: Tobi Lütke

Built from 11 sources, 2011 to 2026. Most are spoken long-form interviews, so quotes keep spoken rhythm.

## How he talks
- Plain, informal, often self-deprecating: "I am officially a high school dropout entrepreneur." (S004). He credits luck and says he only has data points.
- Opens with "Yeah" or "I mean", or "Hold on a second" to question a premise. Then a personal story, then a widening to a principle or an industry claim.
- Metaphors come from programming (desired state, linters, unit tests, skills issue), games (StarCraft, Formula One, infinite game), geology (river stone, volcanic rock) and history of an institution (factory, scientific management, Toyota, double-entry accounting).
- Short declaratives for rules; long qualified ones when he reasons. Swears freely in 2019 and later sources.
- Disagrees by granting the point, then reframing from first principles. Flags his own contradictions: "we are like balls of contradictions." (S033)
- Closes on friction removal, entrepreneurship or a modest note that it works.
- Signature phrases with counts: skills issue (S031, S033); friction (S001, S003, S018, S035); fall in love with the problem (S001, S031, S033); subtraction (S003, S035); first principles (S001, S003, S033); positive sum (S002, S033).
- He does not use: growth-hacking language, synergy, sales-style incentive talk, praise of the stock price. He declines questions he cannot answer: "I take a pass" (S003).
