# Positions: Muhammad Yunus

Built from 41 usable sources in quick mode. Dates are the source dates. Politics is left out on purpose. Topics with no evidence say so.

- **Pricing**: cover cost, no more. "We charge interest to cover the cost." (S016, 2017). Cost of funds plus 10% is green (S049, 2015).
- **Discounting and subsidy**: none inside a social business. "In social business, there is no subsidy; everything is self-sustaining." (S009, 2009)
- **Raising money**: promote, do not fund-raise. "Promotion, not fund raising." (S015, 2012). Investors get their money back only.
- **Debt and collateral**: none. "we have no collateral we have no guarantee" (S019, 2008). In 2016 he adds that regulation in the US slows growth (S033).
- **Profit**: not opposed, but not the aim. "I am not opposed to making profit." (S012). "I’m not against prophit" (S020, 2015). Earlier line: "In my work it never occurred to me that I should maximize profit." (S003, 2008)
- **Charity**: useful once. "charity is no solution to poverty" (S024, 2006). "I have never gone through the charity door." (S033, 2016)
- **Philanthropy and foundations**: invest, do not give away. "So why not invest money, instead of giving it away?" (S042, 2022)
- **Training**: sometimes counterproductive. "sometimes training is counterproductive because you get so used to it brought to you in packages" (S019, 2008)
- **Niche and customers**: begin with the poorest. "our principle is less you have more attractive you are for us if you have nothing you get the highest priority" (S019, 2008)
- **Focus versus diversification**: one problem per business, but he has made many. "I have created more than fifty companies along the way" (S023, 2011).
- **Side projects and prototypes**: "If you create just one tiny social business, you will have developed a seed." (S009, 2009)
- **Partners**: big companies come to him. "I'm here to be used please use me for a good cause social business" (S048, 2015)
- **First hire and firing**: no recorded position.
- **Equity**: he never wanted a share. Social business investors take no dividend (S023).
- **Brand and marketing**: strip packaging and gimmicks. "you don't want to show any gimmick to attract people so you save a lot of money in packaging" (S019, 2008)
- **Paid ads and content**: no recorded position.
- **Women as customers**: "Not even 1 percent of the borrowers of the banks in Bangladesh, at that time, happened to be women." (S016, 2017). Policy moved from 50/50 to women first (S002, 2007).
- **Competition**: no hostility. "you want to make money go ahead make money" (S030, 2017)
- **Metrics**: impact. "The impact of the business on people or environment, rather the amount of profit made in a given period measures the success of social business." (S012)
- **Culture and staff**: staff are paid normally (S033). Bank staff go to the borrower's place: "never discuss anything in your office your office is just for you not for your uh customers or anybody" (S047, 2015).
- **AI and technology**: a blessing or a curse. "Technology can be a blessing or a curse and we have to learn to draw a line." (S018, 2019). In 2017 he said AI would hit garment jobs (S016).
- **Jobs and work**: "Human beings are not born to work for somebody else." (S016, 2017)
- **Timing**: use a crisis. "When things don't work, that's the best time to re-do it, to reorganize it, to re-conceptualize it, to redesign it." (S005, 2009)
- **Welfare**: "I find it very difficult to accept a permanent situation of welfare." (S009, 2009)
- **Aid**: put a share into a social business fund. "whatever money you give to a country would you consider using part of that money 10% 20% of that money in creating a social business fund" (S045, 2015)
- **Payday lenders**: not microcredit. "it's a total abuse of the word" (S020, 2015). "even the loan sharks like payday lenders uh they call themselves microcredit" (S049, 2015)
- **Risk and defaults**: "business and risk go together" (S046, 2015)
- **Scale**: start with five people, then repeat. "it's only five people that if you do it five people we can do 5 million people by repetition" (S020, 2015). "One can start a social business to create jobs for just three people." (S015, 2012)
- **Selling the company, remote work, work hours, burnout**: no recorded position.
- **Change of view on microcredit**: in 2016 he says it perhaps should have been called nanocredit: "maybe we should have translated it “nanocredit.”" (S033, 2016). In 2015 he said profit-seeking microcredit users are going the wrong way (S020).
