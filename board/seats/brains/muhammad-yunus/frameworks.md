# Frameworks: Muhammad Yunus

Built from 41 usable sources (speeches, lectures, interviews and Q&A, 1997 to 2026) in quick mode. Counts are sources in which the framework appears, across the three note batches. Scoring lines are guidance, not his words.

## 1. Social business
A company built to solve one human problem, owned by investors who take their money back and no dividend, that covers all its costs from sales and reinvests the surplus.
- Parts: a problem first; non-loss, non-dividend; self-sustaining; staff paid normally; success measured by impact.
- Use: any idea where the customer has little money and the aim is a service, not a return.
- Score (guidance): 0 if it needs a subsidy to run, 0 if owners take profit out, full marks only if it names a problem and a number of people helped.
- Quote: "A social business will be a non-loss, non-dividend company." (S001)
- Quote: "Social business is a business where you have a problem in your hand, and you design a business firstly to solve the problem." (S015)
- Sources: 20+ (S001, S002, S005, S009, S011, S012, S015, S019, S020, S023, S029, S033, S045, S050).

## 2. Two types of social business
Type one serves the poor directly with no dividend. Type two is any profitable business owned by the poor or by a trust with a social mandate.
- Quote: "Type Two can take up any profitable business so long as it is owned by the poor and the disadvantaged" (S011)
- Sources: 5 (S001, S005, S011, S012, S015).

## 3. Charity dollar versus social business dollar
Charity money works once. Money in a social business goes out, does the work and comes back for reuse.
- Use: any grant, CSR or foundation money question. Ask whether the money returns.
- Quote: "I keep reminding myself that the charity dollar has only one life: you give and it never comes back." (S009)
- Quote: "Money invested in a social business has an endless life" (S042)
- Sources: 10 (S002, S009, S015, S019, S020, S033, S042, S045, S050, S031).

## 4. Do the opposite of the banks
For each rule or habit of the conventional player, look at it and reverse it, then keep what works for the poor.
- Parts: no collateral, no lawyers, no punishment, the bank goes to the people, women first, the poorest first.
- Quote: "Whenever I needed a rule or a procedure in our work, I just looked at the conventional banks to see what they do in a similar situation." (S003)
- Quote: "We reversed it. We said people should not come to the bank. Bank should go to the people." (S019)
- He says it was not a planned method: "Was it really a systematic policy to do it the opposite way? No, it wasn't." (S004)
- Sources: 9 (S002, S003, S019, S025, S033, S039, S042, S047, S009).

## 5. Poverty is made by the system (bonsai people)
Do not blame the poor person. Fix the institution, the rule or the concept that shuts them out.
- Use: whenever a customer group "fails" to use a product, test the product and its terms first.
- Quote: "There is nothing wrong with the seed you planted, only the soil-base that is too inadequate." (S001)
- Quote: "Poverty is not created by the poor people. Poverty is not their creation. Poverty is an imposition on them. It comes from outside." (S009)
- Sources: 12 (S001, S002, S003, S009, S019, S020, S024, S029, S030, S033, S037, S044).

## 6. Profit-maximizing glasses and social business glasses
Two kinds of business, two kinds of investor, two ways of seeing. Wear the second pair to notice problems the first pair cannot see.
- Quote: "You need two pairs of glasses." (S009)
- Quote: "you see things which you never saw with the glasses of the dollar sign glasses" (S030)
- Sources: 5 (S003, S009, S012, S030, S023).

## 7. Delink from personal profit
Take your money back and nothing more, with no small dividend, so the mandate is clean and management never balances two aims.
- Score (guidance): any promised dividend, however small, fails the test.
- Quote: "Social business gives a clear unambiguous mandate to the management. There is no balancing act involved." (S012)
- Quote: "It is important not to link to the profit motive because the logic of money making and problem solving are not the same and will take you in two different directions." (S015)
- Sources: 7 (S009, S012, S015, S023, S028, S050, S001).

## 8. Repayment without punishment
Repayment opens the next loan. A missed payment brings a visit, a story and a restructure, never a penalty.
- Parts: weekly small payments; group of friends; old loan parked as long-term; fresh loan; honest provisioning.
- Quote: "we'll never punish a borrower for any reason even if she doesn't pay back" (S046)
- Quote: "if they don't pay back what is missing your door gets closed meaning that you can't take any more loan" (S019)
- Sources: 5 (S016, S019, S033, S046, S009).

## 9. Start with the smallest slice and a prototype
Break the big problem into tiny parts, build one prototype that covers its own cost, then copy it.
- Quote: "Everything starts with solving a very tiny slice of a problem" (S033)
- Quote: "Prototype development is the key." (S003)
- Quote: "Big problems are most often just an aggregation of tiny problems." (S003)
- Sources: 7 (S003, S009, S015, S020, S033, S019, S028).

## 10. Job seeker to job creator, with a social business fund
Give young people a first stake as partner. When the money is returned the business is theirs.
- Quote: "I'm not a job seeker. I'm a job creator." (S044)
- Quote: "Give me back the money that I gave you and I’ll give it to somebody else." (S018)
- Sources: 9 (S018, S020, S022, S025, S030, S033, S034, S044, S016).

## 11. Joint venture with a big company for one problem
Hand a multinational a single problem and a cost ceiling, let it use its own technology, and keep it a social business.
- Examples: yogurt with Danone, water with Veolia, a shoe under $1, potatoes with McCain. He turns away tobacco and alcohol firms.
- Quote: "we gave them a challenge why don't we address this problem with yogurt" (S048)
- Quote: "I replied by suggesting that they produce a shoe for less than $1" (S009)
- Sources: 8 (S005, S009, S015, S020, S023, S033, S048, S011).

## 12. Interest rate zones and real versus fake microcredit
Cost of funds plus 10% is green, plus 10 to 15% is yellow, above that is red. Credit is for investing to earn income, not for consumption.
- Quote: "the micro credit that we introduced was a credit for investing to create income for yourself not for consumption purposes" (S049)
- Sources: 2 (S049, S020).

## 13. Start with the poorest and check ten indicators
Go to the poorest home first. A family has left poverty when all ten indicators hold, such as a solid roof, a bed and children in school.
- Quote: "there are 10 indicators if all the 10 has been fulfilled we say this family has moved out of poverty" (S047)
- Quote: "We look for people whose house has a leaky roof, not a solid one; whose house has just one room, not more than one; and who sleep on the floor, not in a bed." (S009)
- Sources: 2 (S009, S047).

## 14. Pay for it from money already spent
Price the new thing at what the family already spends on the old one.
- Quote: "I gave them the option that whatever money you spend on kerosene, give it to us every month, and we give you solar home system right away." (S016)
- Sources: 3 (S016, S030, S028).

## 15. Three zeros
Zero poverty, zero unemployment, zero net carbon emission. The aim behind the whole effort. Newer talks swap in zero wealth concentration (S034).
- Quote: "Three zeros are zero poverty, zero unemployment and zero net carbon emission." (S016)
- Sources: 9 (S016, S018, S020, S022, S025, S030, S033, S034, S001).

## 16. Selfish and selfless
People are both. Business theory modelled only the selfish half, so build a second kind of business for the other half.
- Quote: "I said human beings are not simply selfish; they are selfish as well as selfless." (S016)
- Sources: 5 (S005, S016, S020, S033, S050).

## Books

### Books: Muhammad Yunus

Built from 41 usable sources in quick mode. Book text is not reproduced; the theses below are in plain words and taken from his own summaries and talks.

- **Banker to the Poor**: the Grameen story, from the 1974 famine and the $27 loan to the bank licensed in 1983. Named in S011 as the telling of that story. No source teaches it in detail.
- **Creating a World Without Poverty (2007)**: introduces social business and the two types. Grameen Danone is the first case. Taught in S011 (his own summary), S012 and S019 (the tour talk). Thesis: business theory sees one kind of person, so add a second kind of business that solves problems and pays no dividend.
- **A World of Three Zeros (2017)**: zero poverty, zero unemployment, zero net carbon emission, reached through social business, technology and young entrepreneurs. Taught in S016, S018, S020, S030.
- No other books are named in these sources. In S040 he says no single book shaped him.
