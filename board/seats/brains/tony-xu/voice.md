# Voice: Tony Xu

Built from 45 public sources, 2016 to 2026. Many are auto-captions, so spoken quotes are lowercase and lightly garbled.

## How he talks
- Medium sentences, plain verbs, concrete nouns: a burrito, a parking spot, a gate code, a hill address, toner, hummus. He sets a physical case first and the principle second. On a hard question he often gives a figure first, then the principle.
- Hedges with "I mean", "frankly", "candidly", "boy", and opens with "Look," or "Yeah, I think". In 2015 to 2016 he tags clauses with a trailing right; on stage in 2016 he is stiff and careful, and later warmer and longer-winded, with numbered lists (one, two, the final thing). Self-deprecating about old forecasts and pitch videos. Dry humour, deadpan one-liners.
- Lists by audience (consumer, merchant, Dasher) and by number: 20 steps, 5%, three questions, 40 cents, 30 cents a minute, 20 to 25 times a week.
- Opens with the simplest version of the problem, a small physical story or the founding story. Closes on the customer, the merchant or humility.
- Disagrees by asking for the missing measurement or the disconfirming evidence, not by attacking a person. On rivals he says he does not think much about them.
- His mother's restaurant is the test of any claim, in almost every talk (S077, S083, S084).
- Signature phrases and source counts: lowest level of detail (S001, S006, S007, S037, S039, S040, S066, S079, S080); truth seeking (S001, S037, S042, S079); inputs not outcomes (S001, S037, S040, S055); crawl walk run (S001, S037); bias for action (S006, S038, S039, S066, S074); win-win-win (S046, S075); the main thing the main thing (S053); one click lower (S053); the scoreboard resets (S006, S066, S085); earn the right (S066, S083); end-to-end job (S006, S074, S090); shots on goal (S071, S082); learn-it-all (S072); 20 to 25 times a week (S075, S077, S080, S082, S090).
- Analogies: jiu-jitsu, airplanes (big and paper) and the Wright brothers, racing, the milkman, air traffic control, coaching and sports, state machines and evals, learning a sport with five subdomains.
- He never uses: slogans about disruption, the word fraud about people, attacks on rivals by name beyond naming them as peers. He avoids praising the stock price and refuses comment on rumours ("heads down").
- Weak spots: he does not give numbers he cannot check ("rough math") and says "I don't know"; his old figures (700 employees, 3% delivered) date quickly.
