# Stories: Avinash Kaushik

Built from the cases and anecdotes in his own essays. Stories told by readers are excluded. Use these as evidence patterns, not as numbers to repeat.

1. **Ads cut, outcomes up.** A client cut the ads on a page by 25% and outcomes rose 40%. More ads does not mean more revenue. "In a memorable test the client actually reduced the number of ads on the page by 25% and the outcomes improved by 40%." (S052)
2. **Free product test.** A company with four products tested giving its $15 product away free to a 5% slice. The CMO, CEO and CFO objected. The free version shifted sales to pricier SKUs. Lesson: test the radical idea at small exposure. (S052)
3. **ZQI University (a pseudonym).** Revenue-level metrics reversed the channel ranking once the real profit point was used. Also a 10% return rate could wipe out all profit from PPC traffic. (S018)
4. **Catalog and email test.** Dropping the catalog cost 17% of revenue but only about $0.05 of net on the email-only comparison, a number a CEO can set against catalog staff cost. (S032)
5. **Retailer controlled experiment.** Four weeks, 11 test markets (128 stores) against 39 control markets (621 stores). Every $1 on non-brand paid search drove $15 in store sales at a 22% contribution margin. (S043)
6. **Credit Karma in ten minutes.** With no insider knowledge he found macro and micro conversions, key sections and a competitor spike. Shows the DMMM mindset. (S023)
7. **Toothpaste brand.** Sold mostly offline, so he measured store lookups, coupon downloads, signups and videos as micro-outcomes. Optimize a portfolio of outcomes. (S043)
8. **The toddler and the Harley.** Giving advanced tech to an organization not ready is the leader's mistake, not the toddler's. "You can’t give a toddler a Harley Davidson motor cycle." (S043)
9. **Global Strategic Analytics at Google.** His team moved from reporting to influence before, during and after spend. The VP of Finance loves Smart Start. Lesson: data's value is greatest before the budget leaves. (S016)
10. **Impact Matrix on a flight.** Sketched on a whiteboard, built on a 16-hour flight to Singapore. "It’s not the ink, it’s the think." (S059)
11. **Eataly with his parents.** ChatGPT gave a custom walking tour; Google said he misspelled Eataly. Answers beat links. He paid for ChatGPT Plus that day. (S011)
12. **Brand lift reading.** Reading Google's own guidance on interpreting lift studies made him "pause. Then sad." He built an Excel model. (S056)
13. **Rebuilding 17 slides.** He took a published survey report, blocked the names and rebuilt it; his own challenge slide defeated him (S036).
14. **Call center incentive.** When taking as many calls as possible was the incentive, customers' happiness collapsed. "Pick up call. Hang up immediately. High efficiency! Hit targets." (S076)
15. **Charity smiles.** He tied a search recommendation to 1.5 extra smiles a month of donations from his book sales to get an executive's attention. (S041)
16. **Coach and Kate Spade.** Coach's gap was position, not visibility; he re-ran Patagonia's page to check the tool and found the same low score. Disclosure: small angel investment in Evertune. (S009)
17. **B2B SaaS segmentation.** A client clustered sessions over 28 dimensions, found four trial segments and customised onboarding. Outcome numbers are his own client figures (S027).
