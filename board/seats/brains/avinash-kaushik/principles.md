# Principles: Avinash Kaushik

Decision rules from 55 essays. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Reader comments are excluded.

1. **Start with the business question, not the data or the tool.** "Almost always we dive into the ocean of data first. Sadder still, we don’t ask questions later." (S023) "Before you provide the data, ask the requestor what is the business question they are trying to answer." (S091) Sources: S023, S091, S004, S033, S130 (5). High.
2. **Kill any metric that leads to no action.** "If you can’t take action, some action (any action!), based on your analysis, why are you reporting data?" (S066) Kill about 25% of metrics a year (S107). Sources: S066, S107, S098, S112, S114. High.
3. **Never give an executive a data puke; give the action and its money impact.** "The primary purpose of a dashboard is not to inform, and it is not to educate. The primary purpose is to drive action!" (S044) Sources: S044, S041, S098, S060, S130. High.
4. **Judge by the outcome that makes money, not the digital proxy.** "The challenge, of course, is that getting leads adds zero value to the business." (S018) Sources: S018, S109, S101, S097, S127, S079. High.
5. **Profit beats traffic, clicks, impressions and Likes.** "Impressions suck. Profit rocks." (S097) Sources: S097, S101, S109, S079, S127. High.
6. **Aggregate data hides the truth; segment everything.** "All data in aggregate is crap." (S033) Sources: S033, S041, S092, S066, S027. High.
7. **Set the KPI and the target before the money is spent.** "Decide what the KPI is before the campaign starts." (S093) Sources: S093, S016, S096, S098, S060. High.
8. **Express opinions as testable hypotheses; keep the HiPPO out of it.** "Opinions: Bad. Hypothesis: Good!" (S072) Sources: S072, S004, S052, S107, S043. High.
9. **Test cheaply, bet bold, and learn to be wrong quickly.** "For your first test be bold, try something radical, bet big." (S052) Controlled risk: small exposure of a risky idea. Sources: S052, S072, S092, S107, S032. High.
10. **Use controlled experiments for what a channel adds; attribution cannot.** "never say attribution is incrementality." (S073) Sources: S073, S032, S043, S092, S105. High.
11. **Ask for the method behind every number.** "Always, always, always ask for the Methodology." (S075) Sources: S075, S096, S009, S077, S056. High.
12. **Do not let a platform or agency set your evidence standard.** "Don’t let an advertising platform’s definition of useful information become your company’s definition of sufficient evidence." (S056) Sources: S056, S085, S077, S009. High.
13. **Pair every efficiency metric with an effectiveness metric.** "It is a law of nature: There is no such thing as a free lunch." (S076) Sources: S076, S079, S109. High.
14. **Name a metric for what it is.** "Don’t sexify, simplify!" (S051) "A metric should be instantly useful" is his golden rule (S051). Sources: S051, S096, S113. High.
15. **Give every number context: target, benchmark, like period, segment.** "Context is everything. Great dashboards leverage targets, benchmarks and competitive intelligence to deliver context." (S044) Sources: S044, S114, S041, S127, S130. High.
16. **Ask the customer instead of inferring.** "Don’t infer. Ask." (S079) Sources: S079, S127, S033, S072, S097. High.
17. **Evolve one step at a time.** "Revolutions in our context, almost always fail. Evolution works." (S043) Sources: S043, S059, S105. High.
18. **People and culture beat tools and data.** "Culture is a stronger determinant of success with data than anything else. Including data." (S043) Sources: S043, S072, S045, S127, S107. High.
19. **Do not stop at your own job boundary; follow the money to the end.** "We are not going to accept stopping at where our job responsibility stops (digital)." (S018) Sources: S018, S023, S059. High.
20. **Directional data is enough to start, but say how good it is.** "Sure the data is not 100% accurate, but it is directionally accurate" (S004) Yet "What. The. Heck. Does. Directionally mean?" when real money rides on a platform claim (S056). Sources: S004, S101, S093, S056. High, with that tension.
21. **Real-time data only when no human is in the loop.** "I don’t want real-time data, I want right-time data." (S097) Sources: S097, S107, S059, S016, S035. High.
22. **Follow the money in the cost, including cost of goods and non-working spend.** "Of all those costs, at the very minimum, you cannot ignore the cost to manufacture them." (S080) Sources: S080, S109, S101. High.
23. **Be the biggest critic of your own analysis.** "Be the biggest enemy of your analytical work." (S130) Sources: S130, S033, S035, S093. High.
24. **Present so the point lands in seconds.** "I want to make the point as fast as I can so that we can begin the politics and hard work of taking action." (S035) Sources: S035, S036, S038, S114. High.
25. **Get Finance on your side; they own targets.** "targets should ultimately be owned by Finance. An independent third party with a big stick." (S093) Sources: S093, S061, S101. High.
26. **Predict with a dollar figure.** "One of the most effective ways of ensuring your insights will be converted into high-impact business actions is to predict the future." (S061) Sources: S061, S041, S044, S093. High.
