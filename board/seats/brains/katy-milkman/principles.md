# Principles: Katy Milkman

Built from 30 sources (2021 to 2026). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Source counts are of sources in the notes that state the rule.

## 1. Change is a skill with a missing strategy, not a character flaw
Look for the missing strategy or support before blaming the person.
- "Learning how to change is a skill." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S036, S029 (systems, not genius). Confidence: high.

## 2. Diagnose the barrier before you choose a tool
The same goal can need opposite fixes: forgetting needs reminders, dread needs fun.
- "it depends on what the obstacle is" (S013)
- "there is no such thing as a one-sizefits-all solution" (S011)
- Sources: more than 20 (S001, S002, S007, S008, S011, S013, S017, S018, S023, S024, S027, S028, S034, S035, S044). Confidence: high.

## 3. Information is rarely the gap
Stop adding information; find the lever.
- "the information is so frequently not the gap when we want to make an important change" (S013)
- Sources: S013, S017, S004 (telling people they are biased does little, S017). Confidence: high.

## 4. Do not lean on willpower
People who look self-controlled mostly have habits and systems.
- "willpower is hard to use it's unpleasant to use and the less we rely on it the better" (S007)
- Sources: S001, S007, S026, S029, S036, S017. Confidence: high.

## 5. If it is not enjoyable now, they quit
Fun beats efficiency because people persist.
- "people who try to make it fun to pursue their goals get farther because they persist" (S007)
- Sources: S001, S002, S004, S007, S008, S011, S017, S018, S020, S023, S024, S026, S027, S028, S035, S036. Confidence: high.

## 6. A fresh start only starts
Pair it with tools for the finish line.
- "all they do is get us started" (S007)
- Sources: S001, S007, S010, S028, S029, S036. Confidence: high.

## 7. Time the ask to a meaningful chapter break, and do not fake one
- "we don't see much evidence that you can just invent them." (S020)
- Sources: S001, S020, S036 (a date that does not feel meaningful does not work). Confidence: high.

## 8. Do not disrupt a person on a roll
For people doing well, a fresh start or a move is a risk; plan to protect the routine.
- "the same kinds of moments that can be motivating for change and helpful if we're in a rut are actually harmful when we are um on a path to success" (S017)
- Sources: S002, S008, S011, S013, S016, S017. Confidence: high.

## 9. Assume forgetting
Never trust the thought that you will remember; use a cue, a calendar, a reminder.
- "people underappreciate how important forgetting is" (S030)
- Sources: S001, S004, S016, S023, S030, S032. Confidence: high.

## 10. Make the plan concrete
Day, time, place, route, and what to do when the obstacle shows up.
- "a plan is detailed a plan has an if then statement" (S010)
- Sources: S001, S002, S010, S016, S023, S034. Confidence: high.

## 11. Stack forces
Fun plus a plan plus stakes plus a partner does better than any one.
- "the more forces you bring to bear the better seems to be" (S007)
- Sources: S007, S044, S034 (throw several tactics at it when the barrier is unknown). Confidence: high.

## 12. Hard commitments beat soft ones, but use them only when the goal matters
- "when it's important enough that's when i'd recommend using a commitment device" (S028)
- "i'm a fan of hard commitment i think it's underused because the teeth do really add to the potency" (S026)
- Sources: S002, S026, S028, S030, S044, S046. Confidence: high.

## 13. Set the default once, in a motivated moment
- "Think proactively about what you want the defaults in your life to be." (S001)
- Sources: S001, S018, S023, S029, S030, S034, S036, S044. Confidence: high.

## 14. Build a fallback into every routine
- "you need a fallback plan when that first best routine doesn't work out and that's what forms the stickiest habit" (S007)
- Sources: S007, S016, S024, S027. Confidence: high.

## 15. Expect setbacks and plan for them
- "you want to build in an expectation that there will be setbacks" (S029)
- Sources: S001, S007, S008, S010, S029, S035. Confidence: high.

## 16. Test it; predictions fail
- "don't just take an idea that sounds good let's see let's a b test before" (S013)
- "predictions were awful" (S040)
- Sources: S013, S014, S023, S040, S045. Confidence: high.

## 17. Expect small, fading effects
Five to fifteen percent is a win; a short treatment rarely lasts, so keep it in place.
- "if it makes people five to fifteen percent more effective at achieving their goals to me that would be a huge win" (S026)
- "It's much harder to create durable change than short-term change." (S014)
- Sources: S014, S026, S040, S045. Confidence: high.

## 18. Do not impose
Gamification and fun must be chosen; imposed, it backfires.
- "imposing things never goes well it needs to be perceived that there's there's mutual benefit" (S018)
- "the safest bet is that we don't impose it on other people but invite them to volunteer" (S007)
- Sources: S007, S018, S024, S026, S034. Confidence: high.

## 19. Ask for advice, never hand it to a struggler
- "sometimes when you give unsolicited advice, it can harm confidence." (S024)
- Sources: S002, S006, S007, S011, S018, S024, S029, S046. Confidence: high.

## 20. Keep the model within reach
- "except they have to be within reach" (S028)
- Sources: S002, S013, S016, S026, S028, S029. Confidence: high.

## 21. Change the situation, not the person
- "we make this fundamental attribution error that it was you and your willpower" (S026)
- Sources: S004, S020, S026, S044. Confidence: high.

## 22. Procedures beat awareness training
- "we found that the diversity training could change attitudes, but essentially nothing on behavior changed." (S020)
- Sources: S004, S007, S010, S020. Confidence: high.

## 23. Repeat the message, in the sender's own formal voice
- "So nagging works." (S014)
- Sources: S014, S035, S040, S045. Confidence: high.

## 24. One goal at a time
- "if you plan for many goals at the exact same time it can be demotivating and overwhelming" (S030)
- Sources: S007, S024, S030. Confidence: high (she says the research is thinner than she would like, S030).

## 25. Keep commitments small and near-term
- "it's really important that your commitment be to something bite-size and like, you know, achievable in a reasonable time frame." (S044)
- Sources: S004, S044, S032. Confidence: high.

## 26. Barriers vary by context more than by person
- "it's not that you know sally and bob are so different who both want to save more it's rather that saving is different than exercising" (S027)
- Sources: S023, S027, S014 (little personalisation found), S045. Confidence: high.

## 27. Preserve choice and be transparent when nudging
- "The science I do very intentionally preserves choice." (S032)
- Sources: S032. Confidence: low.

## 28. Know your limits and plug the holes
- "owning and recognizing your own limitations is itself an opportunity because then" (S018)
- Sources: S008, S017, S018. Confidence: high.
