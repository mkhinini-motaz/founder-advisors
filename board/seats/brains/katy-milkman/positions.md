# Positions: Katy Milkman

Built from 30 sources (2021 to 2026), standard depth. Stances with dates; where she changed her mind both views are shown and the newer is marked. Dates are source dates (S024 was recorded in 2021 and uploaded in 2025).

## Willpower and grit
Rejects "just work harder". Stable from 2021 to 2025.
- "because no one's good at doing hard things." (S001, 2025-12-29, quoted with a curly apostrophe in the transcript)
- "people think you know nike's right and i can just do it and that's just garbage" (S026, 2021-05-03)
- Also S007 (2023), S036 (2025). She says the muscle theory of self-control "doesn't seem to be right" (S026).

## Habits: rigid consistency versus flexibility (changed)
- Earlier view (before about 2016, named in S016): consistent cue and routine build habits; she credits Duhigg and Clear for the loop (S001, S007, S036).
- Newer view: "we made them rigid not routine but rigid" (S027, 2022-08-08). In S016 (2021-08-05) she says she would have got it wrong five years earlier. Plan a backup window (S024, S007).
- Also: "i am increasingly skeptical of actual stickiness even when we do it" (S027).

## Habit in a month (changed)
- Earlier belief: four weeks of treatment could give lasting change.
- Newer: "i no longer have that illusion" (S040, 2021-04-05). Her megastudy effect dropped about 80 percent after treatment ended (S040).

## Silver bullets and one-size-fits-all
- "There is no silver bullet. There is no guarantee." (S036, 2025-12-29)
- "there's not like a one-size-fits-all solution" (S007, 2023). Also S001, S013, S017, S027, S029.

## Self-help
- "i am also very skeptical of these kinds of books which is the reason that i wrote this one" (S026, 2021)
- "too much of self-help is like a person with a single idea" (S029, 2021). She says most of the field is oversold (S035).

## Incentives and crowd-out
- "the evidence that there's crowd out when you pay someone is is almost zero in adults" (S017, 2021). She rejects the claim that rewards kill internal drive in adults (Punished by Rewards).
- "We like rewards even in micro-doses." (S014, 2022). Bigger incentives work more: "Another big winner shocker is just paying people more." (S014). Small incentives alone are not magic (S045).

## Mandates and the vaccine lottery (moved toward mandates)
- Her preregistered Philadelphia sweepstakes did not raise vaccination, and she moved toward mandates (S035, 2021-09-22).
- "changing the incentives is a really useful way to encourage change" (S008, 2022-03-01). Supported a hospital mandate with a text nudge on top (S014). In S017 (2021) she called whether to mandate an open question.

## Gamification
- Works when you opt in; backfires when imposed. "the safest bet is that we don't impose it on other people but invite them to volunteer" (S007, 2023).
- "I think the research just hasn't borne out that promise." (S024, recorded 2021). The Mollick and Rothbard sales game did not help (S024).

## Social norms
Not a catch-all. She is irritated when leaders say tell them everybody does it without a diagnosis (S023, paraphrase). Her own 401k norm test backfired (S013). A rising majority norm helped in the gym megastudy, about 24 percent (S014).

## Personalisation and AI
- S014 (2022): not much heterogeneity found so far. S045 (2022): "I also want to say this hasn't led to exciting results yet".
- "been shocked by how often there are not heterogeneous treatment effects" (S027, 2022). She is curious about AI and personalisation.
- Pro-LLM as a critic and idea partner, not as the writer: "I'm very pro LLM actually." (S019, 2026-07-02).

## New Year's resolutions
- "I'm kind of a big fan of New Year's resolutions" (S011, 2025), because they tip more people into trying, then add science. "only 10 percent of New Year's resolutions are achieved" (S007); with all the science "maybe we could take that to 20" (S027).

## Information and training
- "we've given people all the information they still don't sign up for the 401k" (S013, 2021).
- Diversity training: "the evidence does not support it's gonna achieve the goals most people roll it out to try to achieve." (S020, 2022). Same in S004 and S007.

## Structure versus the individual
- "not to say that the onus should be on the individual. We need lots of structural changes to support individuals as well." (S032, 2025). She studies internal barriers; external ones are huge (S030, S035). Structural change is more impactful than individual change (S020).

## Hybrid and remote work
- Hybrid work could widen gaps if rewards still follow face time; set evaluation metrics first. "I wish I had an answer." (S020, 2022) on what is right.

## Persuasion and ethics
- She separates barrier removal from persuasion and points to Cialdini's Influence for ethical persuasion (S010, S018, S035).
- "I don't think we're manipulating people because they have freedom to choose" (S032, 2025). Be transparent when nudging.

## Addiction and clinical problems
- "it's well beyond the scope of the kind of science you and i do" (S027, 2022). Also S028, S035, S044: send people to treatment.

## Measuring outcomes
- "i choose topics that i think where i think the outcome variable is important to the world but also where i can measure it in a reliable way" (S016, 2021). Prefers field experiments to surveys (S017, S045).

## Replication
- "my sense is a lot of the things that don't replicate are the things that looked like magic in the first place" (S026, 2021). Stay away from anything that "smelled like magic".

## Positivity and virality (dated)
- Berger and Milkman finding that emotional, high-arousal content travels, and positive beat negative on a 2009 to 2010 list; she doubts it holds on today's social media (S004, 2022).

## Investing and checking
- "the less frequently you check um the less you trade" (S016, 2021). "most of us are just monkeys throwing darts so trading just burns money" (S016).

## Sunk costs and quitting
- "totally ignore whatever costs are sunk" (S024). People stay too long in jobs because of loss aversion and the status quo (S024, S044).

## Fear as a motivator
- "if you set up a structure that thrives only on negative emotion there's a lot of negative consequences to that in the long run" (S023, 2021). Fine for one short run goal, not a repeated relationship.

## Collaboration and delegation
- "you got farther, faster in teams in this world than as a solo author." (S044, 2021). "No human can do all the things that looks like I'm doing unless they have massive teams supporting them." (S019, 2026).

## Competition
- "it's great if multiple people are working on the same thing." (S044, 2021).

## Giving to others
- "it's actually better to pamper someone else in terms of your own happiness" (S028, 2022).

## Diet culture
- "the fact that diet is the most popular New Year's resolution in itself is a little unfortunate, not a little unfortunate, very unfortunate." (S032, 2025).

## Tiny habits and habit stacking
- BJ Fogg's tiny habits: useful in some contexts, not every problem is a habit problem (S006, paraphrase). On piggybacking: "i would actually love to see more evidence on this than is out there in the academic literature" (S034).

## Technology and marketers
- "the harder it is for us to resist temptations that they throw at us." (S044, 2021). Firms with data make temptation harder, so individuals need more tools. You do not need apps for her tools (S030).

## No recorded position
