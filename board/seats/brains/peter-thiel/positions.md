# Positions: Peter Thiel

Built from 25 sources, 2008 to 2026. Where he changed his mind, both views are shown with dates and the newer one is marked. S011 is student class notes; S051 is host-read book text. Party politics and elections are out of scope and not recorded here.

1. Competition. It destroys profit; avoid it. Quote: "the airline industry in the US has made no profits in a 100 years cumulatively because the competition has destroyed all of them" (S045). (2014)
2. Monopoly, good or bad. A monopoly that invented something is good; a rent collector is not. Quote: "A good monopoly is one that actually has invented something new" (S052). (2014) On regulating it: Quote: "I don't think it's necessary for the government to be that involved in regulating them because they normally actually do have some shelf life" (S022). (2019) Limit: Quote: "my claim is not necessarily that monopolies are more Innovative for all time" (S022).
3. Pricing and discounting. No recorded position.
4. Raising money. Take venture money only when the market is winner-take-all, never take a down round. Quote: "you should never ever have a down round." (S011). (2012, class notes) Abundant capital can slow you down. Quote: "it's always good to have more Capital rather than less but but certainly one downside was perhaps we didn't move quite as quickly and figuring out the business model" (S022). (2019)
5. Debt and leverage. The 2008 crisis came from debt and moral hazard. Quote: "caused by too much debt and leverage" (S009). (2009) Government debt in 2024. Quote: "we now have a real runaway deficit problem" (S056).
6. First hire and hiring. Talent is the early edge. Quote: "it is probably the all-important thing in these early organizations." (S003). (2014) Founders spend a quarter to a third of their time on it. Quote: "should probably spend between 25% and 33% of their time identifying and attracting talent." (S011). (2012, class notes)
7. Firing. Quote: "if you wait until it’s obvious to everyone that someone should be fired, it’s far too late." (S011). (2012, class notes)
8. Partners and co-founders. The first decision is whom to start with. Quote: "the first and most crucial decision you make is whom to start it with" (S051). (2014 book text)
9. Equity and boards. Three-person board; CEO pay low. Quote: "If you want your board to do nothing at all, you should probably make it enormous." (S011). (2012, class notes)
10. Focus versus diversification. Quote: "Far better is to understand how to do vertical search and to value depth over breadth." (S011). (2012, class notes) Quote: "Your life is not a portfolio" (S051). As an investor he concentrates: Quote: "If you invest in 100 companies to try and cover your bases through volume, there’s probably sloppy thinking somewhere." (S011).
11. Side projects. No recorded position.
12. Niche. Start in the smallest market you can own. Quote: "you want to go after small markets" (S050). (2025)
13. Content. No recorded position.
14. Paid ads and advertising. Quote: "They obviously work to some extent and they work uh to a disturbing extent on all of us" (S050). (2025) Quote: "It exists to embed subtle impressions that will drive sales later" (S051). (2014 book text)
15. Brand. He does not claim to understand it. Quote: "I never quite understand how branding works" (S050). (2025) Weakest monopoly form. Quote: "I never invest in companies which just about branding" (S044). (2019)
16. AI. Dated changes. 2012: AI is underexplored. Quote (class notes): "AI, by contrast, is an unregulated frontier." (S011). 2015: skeptical. Quote: "AI feels slightly overhyped." (S001). 2023: Quote: "I think it's probably the biggest event in tech since maybe the launch of the iPhone back in 2008" (S007). 2024: Quote: "maybe Ai and 2023 24 is like it's like the internet in 1999" (S056). 2025: Quote: "I'm not sure it's enough to really end the stagnation. It might be enough to create some great companies." (S048). 2026 (newer): Quote: "perhaps AI um is bigger than the internet and is at least has the potential to to reacelerate growth" (S049). Quote: "I don't think AI is the panacea for everything." (S049).
17. Remote work. No recorded position.
18. Work hours and burnout. At PayPal, 90 to 100 hour weeks may not have been useful work (S011, class notes). Quote: "people are working around the clock and running really hard just to stay in place is telling you something about this incredible decline" (S047). (2008)
19. Selling the company. Selling PayPal was right because of real synergies; most tech M&A has none. Quote: "I think most tech m&a deals don't make sense because most of the time there's no there're no synergies" (S022). (2019) Quote: "companies with really good plans typically do not sell." (S011). (2012, class notes)
20. Culture. Quote: "No company has a culture, every company is a culture" (S051). (2014 book text) Lean cult over consultant army (S011, class notes).
21. Metrics. Growth is easy to measure, durability is not (S051, S044). Measure stagnation instead of asserting it. Quote: "how do you actually measure this" (S053). (2017)
22. Lean startup and A/B testing. Skeptical. Quote: "I am personally quite skeptical of all the uh lean startup methodology" (S050). (2025) Quote: "The problem is the search space is just way too big." (S032). (2016)
23. Competition and timing. Being early is a cost. Quote: "Lots of times people get the call right, but the future takes longer to arrive than they thought." (S011). (2012, class notes)
24. Education. 2014: Quote: "it's perhaps a zero-sum tournament masquerading as general insurance." (S003). He still says he would go. Quote: "I probably, I think, I, you know, I probably would still go to Stanford" (S052). 2019: Quote: "we're pretending to be kind to people, and we're actually being cruel" (S036).
25. Risk. The word is used to discourage action. Quote: "risk is actually this word that’s used to discourage people from doing anything" (S032). (2016) Count the size of the bad case too. Quote: "I don't think the probabilities are very high. On the other hand, if the probability is even 5 or 10%. That seems pretty bad." (S049). (2026)
26. Regulation. Bits unregulated, atoms regulated. Quote: "new drugs cost a billion dollars to get through the FDA" (S045). (2014) Quote: "the FDA has a global stranglehold on everything." (S002). (2024)
27. Globalization. Quote: "I would bet on globalization slowly being in abeyance." (S001). (2015) Do not compete with China even if you can win (S011, class notes).
28. Crypto. Bitcoin as a warning on fiat money and digital gold. Quote: "this is a canary in the coal mine, and it's telling you, the hour is very late and you better do something." (S005). (2021)
29. Real estate and zoning. Quote: "it has become impossible for people to get onto onto that" (S054). (2020)
30. Nuclear power. Dual use is the obstacle. Quote: "if you don’t think it can work, it won’t happen." (S003). (2014) Quote: "it turned out to be very dual use if you build nuclear power plants um uh it's it's only sort of one step away from uh building nuclear weapons" (S056). (2024)
31. Life extension. Quote: "Death is kind of a bad thing, in [and] of itself" (S001). (2015) Quote: "people don't even think we can cure Alzheimer's. Why why can't we why can't we at least try to do that?" (S049). (2026)
32. Growth and polarization. Quote: "When the pie stops growing, it becomes a zero sum dynamic, and the legislative process does not work." (S036). (2019)
33. Bubbles. 2019: the bubble is in bonds, not startups. Quote: "my candidate for where the bubble is today is it's the government printing money it's the government bonds" (S022).
34. Founder-led companies. Quote: "as long as the founders run the companies um there's normally enough pressure to keep doing things" (S022). (2019) Quote: "A unique founder can make authoritative decisions, inspire strong personal loyalty, and plan ahead for decades" (S051). (2014 book text)
35. Politeness and dissent inside organizations. Quote: "until we're at the end of history, you need to have dissent, you need to have heterodox views to try to figure things out." (S005). (2021)
36. Unspeakable strategies. Quote: "that if it's articulated the articulation itself will stop us from from executing it properly" (S055). (2022)
38. Moralizing. Quote: "I think we moralize we moralize way too much." (S049). (2026)
