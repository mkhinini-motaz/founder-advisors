# Principles: Peter Thiel

Built from the same 25 sources. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. S011 is student class notes; S051 is host-read book text.

1. Aim for monopoly and avoid competition in any company you start. Quote: "you always want to aim for monopoly and and that and you want to always avoid competition" (S044). Quote: "You should try to avoid fighting, but where you must, you should fight and win." (S011). 5 sources. High.
2. Do not enter a crowded category. Quote: "You don't want to be the fourth online pet food company" (S050). Quote: "you don't want to be the fourth online pet food company you don't want to be the 10th thinf film solar panel company" (S045). 4 sources (S044, S045, S050, S011). High.
3. Do not open a restaurant. It is his example of a competitive, profitless business. Quote: "sort of opening a restaurant is my sort of the Paradigm example of an intensely competitive and really bad business idea" (S022). 5 sources (S011, S022, S044, S045, S050). High.
4. Go after a small market and own it. Quote: "the thing that's always a big mistake is going after a giant market on on day one" (S044). 4 sources. High.
5. A big existing market is a warning. Quote: "large existing markets typically mean that you have uh tons of competition" (S050). 3 sources (S011, S044, S050). High.
6. Test the idea against the real market, not the story. Quote: "what is the real objective market" (S050). 2 sources (S044, S050). Medium.
7. A buzzword stack is a bad sign. Quote: "The something of somewhere is really mostly just the nothing of nowhere" (S050). 3 sources (S044, S050, S053). High.
8. Ask why your breakthrough will be the last one. Quote: "explain why uh yours will be the last breakthrough" (S050). 3 sources (S044, S050, S003). High.
9. Value durability over growth. Quote: "we undervalue durability because a growth is something you can measure in the here and now" (S044). Quote: "Growth is easy to measure, durability isn't" (S051). 4 sources. High.
10. Ask if the business will still be around in ten years. Quote: "Will this business still be around a decade from now?" (S051). 3 sources (S050, S051, S011). High.
11. Have a plan, even a bad one. Quote: "a bad plan is better than no plan" (S045). Quote: "A good intermediate lesson in chess is that even a bad plan is better than no plan at all." (S011). 5 sources. High.
12. Do not treat investments or a career as a lottery ticket. Quote: "once you treat them as lottery tickets, I’ve found you somehow psyched yourself into losing" (S032). Quote: "Your life is not a portfolio" (S051). 4 sources. High.
13. Pick one distribution channel and make it work. Quote: "Poor distribution—not product—is the number one cause of failure." (S011). 3 sources. High.
14. Claims that a product sells itself are a sales pitch. Quote: "this product is so good that it sells itself. This is almost never true." (S011). Quote: "the worst salesmen are salesmen that are transparently salesman" (S045). 2 sources. Medium.
15. Hire opposites held together. Quote: "You want people who are both really stubborn and really open-minded." (S001). 2 sources (S001, S051). Medium.
16. Check the team's prehistory. Quote: "What’s the prehistory of this company? How did you meet, how long have you been working together" (S001). Quote: "I married the first person I met at the slot machines in Las Vegas" (S052). 5 sources (S001, S003, S011, S051, S052). High.
17. Give each person one job and judge him on that. Quote: "Each person had just one thing that he or she was supposed to do." (S011). Quote: "Defining roles reduced conflict" (S051). 3 sources (S011, S022, S051). High.
18. Never hire consultants, and do not outsource recruiting. Quote: "But you should never hire any consultants." (S011). Quote: "Recruiting is a core competency for any company. It should never be outsourced." (S051). 2 sources. Medium.
19. Fire early. Quote: "if you wait until it’s obvious to everyone that someone should be fired, it’s far too late." (S011). 1 source (class notes). Low.
20. Never take a down round, keep the board small, pay the CEO little. Quote: "you should never ever have a down round." (S011). Quote: "The ideal board is probably three people: one VC and two founders." (S011). 1 source (class notes). Low.
21. Take venture money only for a winner-take-all market. Quote: "Giving up 25% of your business is worth it if it enables you to take over your industry." (S011). 1 source. Low.
22. One revenue source must dominate. Quote: "Making money with A is key. Making money with A through E is terrifying, from an investor’s perspective." (S011). 1 source. Low.
23. Prefer technology edge or vertical integration over brand. Quote: "I prefer to invest in companies where there's a there's a major technological advantage or you have the vertical integration" (S045). Quote: "I never invest in companies which just about branding" (S044). 3 sources (S044, S045, S050). High.
24. Distrust themes and crowds. Quote: "I'm always skeptical of themes" (S052). Quote: "there is no wisdom in crowds" (S052). Quote: "when lots of people are trying to do something that is often that is often proof of insanity" (S044). 5 sources (S005, S032, S044, S050, S052). High.
25. Near-unanimity is a warning, not a proof. Quote: "Unanimity in crowds can be very disconcerting." (S011). 3 sources (S005, S011, S037). High.
26. If a claim must be silenced, suspect it is true. Quote: "if you're not allowed to say something, I have a suspicion, not only that you should be allowed to say it, but that it's simply true." (S005). 2 sources (S005, S037). Medium.
27. Substance over status. Quote: "I would always be long substance, short status." (S001). Quote: "always value substance over status, substance over prestige" (S052). 3 sources (S001, S045, S052). High.
28. Do the work that would not happen without you. Quote: "If we weren’t doing this, nobody else in the world would be doing this." (S001). Quote: "one-of-a-kind companies where people are sort of on a mission to do something where if they didn't work on it, it wouldn't happen elsewhere" (S052). 4 sources (S001, S003, S045, S052). High.
29. Do not copy last era's winner. Quote: "the next Mark Zuckerberg won't be building a social network." (S010). 4 sources (S010, S045, S050, S011). High.
30. Do not treat the future as fixed; act. Quote: "I always believe in human agency and so I think it matters a great deal whether people end it or not." (S001). Quote: "I think there's always room for human freedom and human choice." (S048). 8 sources. High.
31. Believe it can be done. Quote: "thinking you can do something is a necessary precondition for being able to do it." (S003). 3 sources (S003, S011, S045). High.
32. Prefer a gate nobody is using. Quote: "Maybe go around the corner and go through the vast gate that no one's taking." (S050). 2 sources (S044, S050). Medium.
33. Be careful not to overstate. Quote: "we really always need to be careful not to overstate and exaggerate these kinds of things." (S007). 3 sources (S007, S032, S056). High.
34. A why question has no single cause. Quote: "I'm always hesitant to answer the why question because these things are so so over determined" (S055). 3 sources (S054, S055, S056). High.
35. Do not trust specialists who grade themselves. Quote: "there's no outside check, no reality check" (S054). Quote: "the specializations should make you suspicious" (S036). 3 sources. High.
