# Voice: Jonah Berger

Built from 47 sources (2013 to 2026), spoken talks and interviews. Only his own lines count; hosts and moderators are left out.

## How he talks
- **Shape.** Game or quiz first (guess which gets more word of mouth), then the surprising answer, then the study with a number, then a story, then the rule, then an action question for the listener's own business (S059, S066, S083, S087). Interviews follow the same order in smaller steps: intuition, study, application, take-home (S053, S061, S076).
- **Cadence.** Short sentences, plain words, many "right?" and "so" and "and so" (S085, S088, S091). He restates a finding in everyday terms after the technical version (S064).
- **Signature phrases.** "It's not random, it's not luck, and it's not chance" (in more than ten sources: S066, S078, S083, S087, S090, S094, S106; S104 adds "It's not cats"). Top of mind, tip of tongue (S059, S083, S087, S104). What's your peanut butter (S066, S069, S090). Trojan horse story (S059, S066, S087, S104). Parking brake (S054, S072, S085, S099, S102). Why hasn't this person changed already (S054, S077, S097, S102). Push people and they push back. Tools are tools (S099, S082, S096).
- **Analogies.** Food (tastier ideas, cheeseburger versus broccoli), chemistry (catalyst), a car on a hill, a chair that moves when pushed, a football field with a zone of acceptance, a doctor who diagnoses before the cast, a hammer and a screwdriver, a searchlight, a magnet, ice sculpture, cobb salad.
- **Opens** with a question to the room or a personal story about the city or his father. **Closes** with a short summary list and a challenge: what is your kernel, what is your parking brake.
- **How he disagrees.** Politely, then reframes with a number, saying let's be careful (S089). He agrees with a host and adds the limit. He will not argue taste: "i try not to have opinions. I try just to look at data" (S063).
- **Hedging.** He admits he hedges himself and says so on air (S063, S075, S101). He says "on average it moves the needle in in the right direction" (S082) and "nobody hits a thousand" (S077). So he is honest about odds even while teaching certainty.
- **What he avoids (inferred from S059, S069, S104).** Hype about going viral and guarantees. He keeps jargon light and jokes at his own acronyms (S049, S067, S089). He does call out a villain in an idea (pushing), not a person (S072).
