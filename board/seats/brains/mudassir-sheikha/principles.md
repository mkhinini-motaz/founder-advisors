# Principles: Mudassir Sheikha

Built from 26 sources (2019 to 2026), topped up with batches 03 to 05 to 44 ok sources (2016 to 2026). Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. Pick the business by who it helps, then the market. "We didn't start Careem because we were deeply passionate about transportation or a specific app idea." (S029). Sources: S005, S029, S055 (3). High.
2. Go after the narrowest use case first. "you have to be very focused early on you have to go after as narrow opportunities as possible" (S002). Sources: S002, S031, S059 (3). High.
3. Make the product very local; cash is the reality. "make the product very very local" (S002). Sources: S002, S005, S048, S055 (4). High.
4. Build the missing layer yourself. "We have to build our own locations databases to make these services reliable" (S005). Sources: S005, S048, S056 (3). High.
5. Understand the problem, never replicate. "my advice generally is understand the problems" (S059). Sources: S055, S059, S056 (3). High.
6. Purpose is set once and tested against every big decision. "All our biggest and most important decisions should be tested against what they mean in light of our purpose" (S005). Sources: S005, S055, S052, S057 (4). High.
7. Deliver the basics before you talk purpose. "you should be focused on delivering the basics versus talking about your purpose" (S005). Sources: S005 (1). Low.
8. Fair earnings come first for captains. "reliable and predictable earnings, getting the right support when they need, is way more important than other things that we do with them" (S005). Sources: S005, S002, S056 (3). High.
9. Do not tax the captains with margin. "we're in a very little margin business and since we're low margin we don't want to tax our captains with very high margins" (S002). Sources: S002, S012 (2). Medium.
10. Stay frugal after raising money. "every dollar that we spend has to match the five dollars that our competition is spending in our Market" (S002). Sources: S002, S055 (2). Medium.
11. Hire fewer, better people. "rock stars are twice price more productive than average performers" (S002). Sources: S002, S055, S057 (3). High.
12. Solve people, not problems. "if you can solve people meaning get the right people in the right roles at the right time and have them solve the problems for you that's way more scalable" (S057). Sources: S057, S055 (2). Medium.
13. Exit misfits early. "we have not exited them fast enough" (S055). Sources: S055 (1). Low.
14. Do not stall in analysis; one deep cut. "you don't want to shake the organization" (S058). Sources: S058, S052, S019 (3). High.
15. Balance growth and profit in sequence. "But we just cannot do them simultaneously anymore" (S012). Sources: S012, S014, S019, S052 (4). High.
16. A super app is good near its core and partners for the rest. "You are going to be good in some things that are or close to your core competencies, but everything else, you will struggle in" (S014). Sources: S014, S031 (2). Medium.
17. Judge by habit, not announcements. "Real impact shows up in customer habits, not loud announcements or heavy discounting." (S031). Sources: S031, S002 (2). Medium.
18. A good product markets itself. "Build a great product, make sure that it wows people and people will do the rest for you." (S052). Sources: S052, S002 (2). Medium.
19. Sustainability must win in the market. "You may be trying to do something sustainable, but you're not going to win in the market." (S036). Sources: S036, S057, S005 (3). High.
20. Stay at the problem. "there is an attitude at Kareem that we don't give up so we stay at the problem for way longer than we should" (S002). Sources: S002, S055 (2). Medium.
21. Put the next hard problem ahead of today's comfort, but run the day. "Don't let your massive vision paralyze your day-to-day execution." (S029). Sources: S029, S018 (2). Medium.
22. Shield customer data from the other side of the market. "we never shared the phone number of our customers with captain's in the Middle East" (S055). Sources: S055 (1). Low.

## Added by the top-up (batches 03 to 05)

23. Build cash in, even at high cost. "we enabled the option to offer pay the service buy cash" (S061). "Literally every day we had bags of cash from captains and people were going to bank accounts to deposit that cash." (S067). Sources: S061, S064, S067, S070, S072, S077 (6). High.
24. Reliability comes before delight; a delight becomes an expectation. "when you need a car you'll get a car" (S070). "if we fail to deliver those reliable experiences then guess what they will start churning" (S077). Sources: S070, S077 (2). Medium.
25. Where trust is the barrier, pay for the check. "otherwise the service will not take off if you don't do these things" (S063). Sources: S061, S063, S070, S076 (4). High.
26. Never sell below the cost to deliver; unit economics before more businesses. "the unit economics will have to be positive for the business scale" (S066). Sources: S065, S066, S067 (3). High.
27. Be frugal because costs are in dollars and incomes are not. "if we're not frugal we're not going to be able to serve the masses in our region sustainably" (S065). "a culture that we wanna set in place, which was going to be frugal" (S079). Sources: S065, S078, S079 (3). High.
28. Launch on a deadline and let the market tell you what to build. "let's just launch it and we see what the market tells us" (S061). "Force ourselves to go live and do the first trip at six weeks" (S079). Sources: S061, S071, S078, S079 (4). High.
29. Do not buy growth. "make sure that you're not succumbing to the pressure of buying growth through through artificial or unsustainable means" (S071). "we had absolutely no money to burn anywhere close to the kind of money that is being burnt ecosystem right now" (S065). Sources: S065, S071, S072 (3). High.
30. Build to last, never to flip. "you should always build to last you should never build to flip" (S074). "play the long game never have a bit to flip mindset" (S065). Sources: S064, S065, S066, S074 (4). High.
31. Enable, do not disrupt: begin as a layer on the licensed operator. "we we do not have to disrupt we do not need to disrupt" (S070). Sources: S069, S070, S079 (3). High.
32. The captain is the product. "a single most important factor in the quality equation for cream" (S063). "if you have an unhappy captain, you have an unhappy customer" (S072). Sources: S063, S069, S070, S072 (4). High.
33. Pay workers fast to change cash behaviour. "what we're calling daily payouts" (S077). Sources: S077 (1). Low.
34. Introduce social change in stages, and let economics follow. "it was a very managed phased rollout" (S070). "we said the society is not ready for it" (S070). Sources: S070, S072 (2). Medium.
35. Choose investors for fit, not headline valuation. "it's not just money it's also who is coming on board" (S061). "you can fire people that you hire but you cannot fire investors once they are sitting on the board and on the cap table" (S065). Sources: S061, S065, S068 (3). High. His own admission: early terms were non-standard and caused a painful unwinding (S061, paraphrase).
36. Be picky early; interview the first hundreds yourself. "you should personally interview the first let's say one hundred two hundred people that come into the business" (S071). "if you're not picky in the first people that come into the room you know you're going to build an organization that looks very very different" (S071). Sources: S065, S071, S078 (3). High.
37. Give equity to everyone. "everyone at Kim including the person that picks up your phone when you when you call the Kim conent has equity" (S078). Sources: S061, S076, S078 (3). High. His limit: for junior staff, "at the more junior levels in our society, I think cash is still king." (S067).
38. Outside the core, be a platform; acquire only inside the core. "we want to be a platform and not get into more things directly than we need" (S067). "has to be things that are adjacent to us in some shape or form" (S074). Sources: S064, S066, S067, S071, S074 (5). High.
39. Go deeper in existing cities before opening new ones, and do not run many launches at once. "our current focus is to make sure that we go deeper in our existing countries" (S072). "we just cannot do them simultaneously anymore" (S066). Sources: S066, S067, S072 (3). High.
40. Prefer a fixed fee to a high commission. "forget about commission, forget about a high commission — pay a fixed subscription" (S072). Sources: S072 (1). Low.
41. Do not hide the bad tail in a percentage; do not wait for the complaint. "but you don't realize that that .5% at scale represents hundreds and thousands of customers are actually having a bad experience because of some reason or the other." (S067). "why do we wait for the customer to complain" (S070). Sources: S067, S070 (2). Medium.
42. Do the local thing before the global entrant learns it. "But what you realize is this market is different, right?" (S067). Sources: S061, S067, S078 (3). High.
43. Regulate yourself first and treat government as a partner. "We were the first ones to sign up to be regulated." (S069). "we view the government's as as partners in this mission to improve the lives of people in this region" (S061). Sources: S061, S063, S069 (3). High.
44. Stay close to the frontline; fix by hand first. "every week every sort of Thursday which is the Friday in Dubai we would make a list of all the failures that we have had in the week" (S071). "if you really want to succeed long long term, you have to be extremely customer-centric" (S067). Sources: S067, S071, S079 (3). High.
45. Do the right thing and let money follow. "the value is an outcome" (S063). "we just believe that, one, this is the right thing to do. And we should just always be on the right side." (S069). Sources: S063, S067, S069, S070 (4). High.
