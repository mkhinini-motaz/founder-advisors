# Principles: Hamdi Ulukaya

Built from 46 ok sources, 2012 to 2026. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. Take care of your own people first, before profit. "In the new way of business, it’s your employees you take care of first, not the profits." (S014); "our business number one rule is to take care of your own people in every way you can" (S057). 6+ sources (S014, S055, S057, S031, S010, S016). High.
2. Pay is measured against a worker's life, not the market rate. "If you make $7 or $8 or $9 an hour, you can’t have a house." (S024); "you look at the people's living expenses in that community" (S061). 3 sources (S024, S058, S061). High.
3. Investing in staff is not a cost. "investing on your people is never an expense it is an investment" (S057). 2 sources (S057, S002). Medium.
4. Share ownership is recognition. "They earned it with their talent and with their hard work, and I don’t see any other way." (S014). 8 sources (S002, S014, S041, S055, S056, S058, S061, S066). High.
5. Mean it or leave it out. "this cannot just be for the show it's you have to mean it" (S058); "it's in part of their DNA it's not check the box" (S060). 4 sources (S016, S058, S060, S066). High.
6. A job beats a handout. "I don't believe in handout." (S016); "Not the handouts or what you can give them but what you can provide as a job" (S007). 4 sources (S007, S016, S031, S065). High.
7. Remove the practical obstacle. "So, I said, let’s get some buses." (S031); "obstacles are not big ones" (S064). 7 sources (S003, S031, S041, S055, S059, S061, S064). High.
8. Go to a community as a guest and ask what it needs. "businesses should go to the struggling communities and ask, "How can I help you?"" (S055); "Go to communities, ask for permission, be with them, and succeed together" (S037). 4 sources (S010, S014, S037, S055). High.
9. Look for what you share before what divides. "I always wanted to find similarities between me and the new places" (S065); "integration has to happen in both ways" (S064). 4 sources (S050, S058, S064, S065). High.
10. Do not use a spreadsheet to judge people. "Spreadsheets are lazy." (S055); "some of the most important thing cannot be captured in a spreadsheet" (S066). 4 sources (S014, S037, S055, S066). High.
11. Business must take a stand. "Silence is criminal these days." (S024); "Silence is not an option." (S040). 5 sources (S024, S040, S050, S056, S055). High.
12. Keep the business simple enough to understand. "I kept it extremely simple" (S003); "I was in QuickBooks until $670 million in sales." (S059). 3 sources (S003, S059, S070). High. His own limit: past that size he says he no longer understood the books (S003, S016).
13. Keep outside money out so decisions stay yours. "will they force me to do things that I don’t want to do?" (S003); "By 2012, Chobani was in billion in sales, and I had not even raised a penny." (S043). 5 sources (S003, S043, S059, S061, S069). High. Newer view in positions.md.
14. Make food you would serve your own child, at a price for all. "make the food that you can serve to your own children" (S057); "good food for all: delicious, nutritious, natural, and accessible." (S003). 6 sources (S003, S022, S047, S057, S060, S059). High.
15. Make it the owner's town too: first profits to the town. "I don't see Chobani any different than my family" (S058); "it cost us all the profits we had made up to that month" (S060). 4 sources (S058, S060, S064, S069). High.
16. Give only what you can keep giving. "I wanted to do things that I could continue to do for a long long long long time" (S058). 1 source. Low.
17. Keep people near who will correct you. "I give permission every single one of you to hit me on the face." (S059). 5 sources (S002, S003, S013, S040, S059). High.
18. Do not be captive to growth metrics. "We don’t have any external pressure or demand to go public. There is no cash-out scenario." (S025). 3 sources (S025, S060, S061). High.
19. Start without the full plan. "the worst thing you can do is have everything figured out." (S029); "I never locked myself into it. I never put myself into that prison." (S043). 3 sources (S002, S029, S043). High.
20. Act on a feeling the numbers do not explain, then own the work. "this was not going to be about if I could sell it. It was going to be about can I make enough." (S041). 4 sources (S024, S041, S058, S068). High.
