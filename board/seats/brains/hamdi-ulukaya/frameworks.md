# Frameworks: Hamdi Ulukaya

Built from 46 ok sources (talks, interviews, a pledge letter), 2012 to 2026. Quick depth. Scoring lines are guidance, not his words.

## 1. The anti-CEO playbook (his name)
Four parts: gratitude (employees first), community (go as a guest and ask how to help), responsibility (business as citizen), accountability (the CEO answers to the consumer). Use it for any choice between owner gain and the people around the business. Score: does the option put workers first, name a community partner, take a stand, and stay answerable to customers? Four yes is a go; a no on workers is a stop (guidance).
Quote: "In the new way of business, it’s your employees you take care of first, not the profits." (S014)
Also: "In my opinion, the CEO reports to consumers." (S014) Used in S014, S055, S037, S010, S043, S040 (6 sources).

## 2. Stakeholders before shareholders
Employees, community, consumers and humanity are the purpose; the shareholder gains when all of them do. Use it when someone says "maximize profit". Score: list who wins and who pays; any stakeholder who only pays is a flag (guidance).
Quote: "Because our future depends on everyone to succeed, not just, you know, shareholders, but every stakeholder." (S008)
Also: "I think that's the dumbest idea I've ever heard in my life." (S055) Used in S008, S014, S016, S025, S040, S055, S057, S066 (8 sources).

## 3. Right with your people, community and product
Do all three right and profit follows. Use it as a pre-launch check. Score: three yes or do not ship (guidance).
Quote: "If you are right with your people, if you are right with your community, if you are right with your product, you will be more profitable." (S014)
Used in S014, S055, S002 (3 sources).

## 4. A job ends refugee status: hire, train, use your supply chain
Remove the practical blocker (cars, buses, translators, training) and hire. Treat it as HR and community work, not charity. Use it for any hard-to-place group of local labour. Score: is the blocker one a bus, a translator or a course can fix? Then hire (guidance).
Quote: "it’s very simple: Hire them, train them, and use your supply chain" (S003)
Also: "Let's get buses, it's not a rocket science." (S055) Used in S003, S007, S031, S041, S055, S056, S059, S064, S066, S079 (10 sources).

## 5. Recognition, not a gift (workers' stake)
Work out what a worker's life costs in that town, then wage, leave, benefits and shares. The test is whether the owner will recognize it. Use it for pay, equity and leave. Score: does the lowest wage cover a house and food? Does the CEO take the same leave? (guidance)
Quote: "this is not a gift. This is the recognition." (S002)
Also: "it's not if they deserve it or not are you going to recognize this or not" (S058) Used in S002, S024, S041, S055, S056, S058, S061, S066 (8 sources).

## 6. Embedded on day one
Purpose built in at the start; culture is set in the first years and is lived stories, not wall posters. Use it for new ventures and for any plan to add the social part later. Score: is the promise printed or booked before the first sale? (guidance)
Quote: "it has to be embedded very, very early on. Day one." (S003)
Also: "we printed in Chobani cup the day one we said if we are successful 10% of our profit is going to go to the community first" (S064) Used in S003, S016, S059, S060, S064 (5 sources).

## 7. Shepherd community: the herd is made whole
When a family loses its flock, every family gives one, and gives its best. Use it for how a town, a group of businesses or a region handles one member's loss. Score: does the structure let a neighbour fall alone? (guidance)
Quote: "If a wolf attacked your herds, and you lost all of your sheep, each family would bring one. And the next day you would have all your sheep back." (S024)
Also: "they pick the best one they don't bring just the okay one" (S058) Used in S024, S031, S058 (3 sources).

## 8. Shepherds and warriors
A leader sometimes protects the flock and sometimes fights for it; business should be both. Use it for when to speak and when to stay quiet.
Quote: "Sometimes that meant we had to be shepherds. And sometimes it meant that we had to be warriors." (S031)
Used in S031 (1 source).

## 9. Open the four walls / hire local first
The plant belongs to the town: hire from the town first, widen only when it runs dry, open the doors on day one.
Quote: "we are not going to stay in these four walls we're going to open these four walls to everybody" (S069)
Also: "we will not go far away to get people to work here" (S066) Used in S066, S069, S041 (3 sources).

## 10. Giving can break something
Give as a partner, build a platform people use to build for themselves; do not give from above.
Quote: "creating a platform for others to build life for themselves is the best way of giving" (S065)
Also: "every time you give something breaks if it's not done right" (S060) Used in S060, S065 (2 sources).

## 11. Stay a real business, not an NGO
The company must pay for itself or it cannot keep its promises.
Quote: "I’m not an NGO. This is not an NGO. This is a business, and it has to self-sufficient itself." (S003)
Used in S003, S059 (2 sources).

## 12. Pass the threshold before the big guys come
Stay small or grow past the point where large rivals cannot hurt you; compete on manufacturing and supply chain.
Quote: "Either I want to stay small, or I have to pass certain level that they cannot hurt me anymore." (S003)
Used in S003, S059 (2 sources).

## 13. Elevated environment
Success is the environment the founder builds, with many people turning into solution makers.
Quote: "Ninety-nine percent of it is not you; it’s the environment that you created" (S003)
Also: "People become heroes, people become solution makers." (S059) Used in S003, S059 (2 sources).

## 14. Permission to be hit
Tell the people who work with you to call you back to reality if success changes you.
Quote: "if you ever see me act differently than what I normally am, I’m giving permission to every single one of you to hit me, shake me, tell me I’m losing it." (S013)
Used in S002, S003, S013, S040, S059, S061 (6 sources).

## 15. Stay on when the cameras are off
After a disaster, the question is how to keep helping once the news moves on.
Quote: "my biggest interest is how do we how do we stay on when cameras are off" (S060)
Used in S060, S043 (2 sources).

## Books

### Books: Hamdi Ulukaya

Built from 46 ok sources. He has no book of his own in this corpus. His major pieces and what he reads:

- The anti-CEO playbook, TED talk, April 2019. Thesis: gratitude, community, responsibility, accountability; employees first, the CEO reports to the consumer. Taught in S055, S014, S010, S037, S043.
- Giving Pledge letter, 28 May 2015. Most of his personal wealth to refugees; 10 percent of profits to charity since day one. S047.
- The Tent partnership, from 2016: a company pledge to hire and train refugees. S050, S064, S065.
- Commencement keynotes (Adelphi 2023, Northeastern 2022, Utica 2025): adapt, be curious, be kind, walk the way. S063, S079, S029.
- Reads: Rumi ("Everyone should read Rumi.", S043) and The Alchemist, re-read to tell stories to his sons (S043). Hafiz is named by an interviewer (S013).
