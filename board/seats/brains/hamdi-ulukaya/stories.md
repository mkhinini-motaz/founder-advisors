# Stories: Hamdi Ulukaya

Built from 46 ok sources, 2012 to 2026. Facts as he tells them.

1. The flyer. An ad for a fully equipped yogurt plant went in the bin; he fetched it back and bought the closing Kraft plant with an SBA loan (about $700,000 in his telling). A lawyer said they wanted an idiot. Lesson: act on a feeling the numbers do not explain. (S024, S058, S066, S069)
2. The 55 workers and the white walls. About 55 people were closing the plant with dignity; he hired four first, and the first idea was to paint the walls. Lesson: people given up on rebuilt it. "Honest to God, that was the only idea I had." (S014; also S055, S060, S070)
3. Hit me. In year two or three he told the factory staff they were his family and that each could hit him if he got a big head. Lesson: staff are the check on the owner's ego. (S003, S013, S059, S061)
4. Little League field. First profits built a field better than Cooperstown's in Norwich. Lesson: make the children feel worth it and the town wakes up. "so it wasn't directly the workers it was their children" (S060; also S002, S064)
5. The creek. Two years of the near-failed cheese business, crying by the creek, carrying others' livelihoods. "I would go there and cry and cry and cry" (S058; also S024)
6. Idaho. A place nobody looked at; no incentives asked. "We shook hands, we broke bread." Hundreds trained with a community college. (S014, S016, S041)
7. Utica. Refugees lacked language, cars, training. He rented cars, buses and translators. Lesson: an HR job, not charity. (S003, S058, S059)
8. The wolves. Each family brings its best sheep to the one that lost its flock. Lesson: community is the real wealth. (S024, S031, S058)
9. The credit line. Bankers offered 250 million in 2011; he did the sum and said no in fifteen minutes. Lesson: do not borrow what you cannot explain. (S058)
10. The refused offer. Pressed to walk away, he would not: he would have died a little every day. (S013)
11. The two Afghan sisters. They reached Twin Falls, were trained at the plant, worked, then college. "I did not give them anything, just provide them work." (S016, S031)
12. Lesbos and Tent. He expected tents and soup, found none; he started Tent to bring companies in. Over 300 companies by 2023. (S040, S065)
13. Parental leave. After his son was born he found the company had none; now six weeks for every parent, himself included. (S058)
14. The ShopRite buyer. Laughed at "we're going to give you the factory", called two weeks later unable to keep the product on the shelf. (S068, S016)
15. Pandemic. Plants never stopped; staff wrote protocols; a truckload of food went to food banks daily. (S057, S060)
16. Adelphi. Arrived with $3,000, money for two months, a stranger's tip to go to America. (S063, S066, S043)
