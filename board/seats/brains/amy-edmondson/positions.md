# Positions: Amy Edmondson

Stances on founder questions, with source and year. Where no source speaks, the topic says no recorded position. She is an academic, not an operator: she has never run a startup.

## Failure and risk
- **Fail fast, fail often.** Fine in the right place, wrong everywhere else. 2023: "I'm a fan of fail fast fail often in the right conditions, right in the scientific laboratory" (S069). Also "it's context dependent" (S071). 2025: "Silicon Valley happy talk" (S094).
- **Failure is not an option.** A dangerous slogan. "It's a dangerous slogan" (S069), 2024.
- **Move fast and break things.** "I don't really love break things" (S071), 2023.
- **Risk.** Avoiding it is a risk. "If you don't like taking a risk, you are taking a risk." (S050), 2024.
- **Regret.** "we are more likely to regret not taking risks than having taken a risk and failed at something." (S050), 2024.
- **Pilots.** Should fail small. "good pilots should fail." (S099), 2026.
- **Failure parties.** Yes, if the failure is intelligent. Where culture refuses, celebrate the pivot. "he says I celebrate the pivot" (S074), 2025. Warns of a "failure fad" (S096).
- **Quitting a project.** Stop doomed projects early; "we will celebrate you when you call it." (S101), 2026.

## People, hiring, firing, culture
- **Hiring and talent.** Talent needs good use. "the talent is meaningless if you can't put it to good use in an interdependent setting" (S067), 2016.
- **First hire.** No recorded position.
- **Firing.** Not a topic she argues directly. Misconduct is treated harshly so limits are known: "it makes the workplace more psychologically safe, not less" (S047), 2020. A truly blameworthy failure gets a matching response (S096).
- **Layoffs.** Separate from safety. "please do not confuse the issue of layoffs with the concept of psychological safety" (S066), 2023.
- **Culture.** Local climate, not company mood. "It's not culture, it's climate." (S069), 2024. "culture has been defined as sort of what's valued around here" (S102).
- **Culture change.** Faster than people think, with many small actions at once. "easier and faster than most of us think" (S094), 2026.
- **Brilliant jerks.** "not ending up promoting the jerks and not rewarding the good managers" (S098), 2022. Steve Jobs could get away with it; most cannot (S067).
- **Remote and hybrid.** Lowers safety on average. "remote work and hybrid work on average are having have a negative effect on psychological safety" (S076), 2025. Be deliberate and structured (S065).
- **Burnout and work hours.** Weak evidence she admits. "busyness isn't something to be proud of" (S070), 2023.
- **Diversity.** Pays only with safety, from one study of 62 innovation teams. "diversity is no good unless people are able to express it and wrestle with it." (S086), 2026. She calls it "just that one study" (S104).
- **Unequal license to fail.** "there's an unequal unlevel playing field for failure" (S101), 2026.

## Management and accountability
- **Accountability.** Not blame. "accountability is a privilege accountability is your power to own your contribution to outcomes" (S076), 2025.
- **Performance reviews.** Give evidence, not conclusions, and hold them often; a forward-looking "performance preview" (S070, S081).
- **Targets.** "hit your targets one of the most destructive phrases I I think that's still out there" (S075), 2026. Routine manufacturing still fits stopwatch logic (S075).
- **Forced ranking.** Kills sharing and helping (S067), 2016.
- **Boss.** "the idea of boss as boss, I think, is old fashioned and obsolete and unhelpful" (S098), 2022.
- **Command and control.** Works only where cause and effect are predictable (S084).
- **Hierarchy.** Not removed, handled. "the more an organization takes hierarchy seriously, the more psychological safety is threatened" (S062), 2026.
- **Metrics.** Error counts are reported counts; the seven-item survey is free and a conversation starter (S070, S098).

## Strategy, speed, focus
- **Experimentation.** Role-sized: "what's big for me um might be very small for you and vice versa." (S101), 2026.
- **Scaling.** Companies fail when they cannot shift from experimenting to scaling; no one speed (S102), 2025.
- **Competition.** "nothing fails like success" (S080), 2024. Competition inside a team should be discussible (S075).
- **Purpose.** "your purpose cannot be simply making money or eventually someone will replace you" (S068), 2026.
- **Focus.** "saying no is part of how you say yes" (S068), 2026. Asked to say no with kindness (S070).
- **AI.** No strong stance. 2025: "I don't have a magic wand or a crystal ball" (S094). 2026: use it to prompt better questions (S062, S068). Admits she has not thought deeply (S102).
- **Pricing, discounting, raising money, debt, equity, partners, paid ads, brand, selling the company, niche.** No recorded position.
- **Timing.** No recorded position beyond "It will look like it's working until it isn't and then it's then it's too late" about playing safe (S068).

## Founder and leader as a person
- **Humility.** A skill. "I like to think of humility not as a trait, but as a skill." (S047), 2020.
- **Perfectionism.** "perfectionism is a kind of crippling belief that I cannot make a mistake." (S071), 2023.
- **Impostor syndrome.** "it is a kind of disease that leads you to do things that are not in your best interest" (S075), 2026.
- **Leadership.** "Leader is a position, leadership is an activity. Anyone can exercise leadership." (S056), 2021.
- **Anger.** "When I'm angry, I am not really open to the possibility that I may be missing something" (S071), 2023.

## A change of mind, side by side
- **Name of the idea.** 2016: "sometimes I've misnamed the concept" (S067). 2020: "I sometimes think I aired in labeling it this way" (S091). By 2022 to 2026 she prefers the phrase permission for candor (S080) and says not to sell the term (S065, S076). Newer view: talk about the work, not safety.
- **Failure celebration.** 2020 she warns "not so fast" about loving failure (S092); 2023 on she gives it criteria (S074, S099).
