# Stories: Amy Edmondson

Cases she reuses, with the lesson. Paraphrase only; quotes carry source ids.

1. **The reversed hypothesis (medication errors, her PhD).** She expected better hospital teams to make fewer errors and found they reported more. Rated blind, the units' openness matched the reports. Lesson: reports are not errors, and a failed hypothesis can open a better door. "To my great chagrin, the data seemed to be saying that better teams had higher, not lower error rates." (S050). Sources: S048, S050, S055, S058, S060, S063, S069, S073, S079, S080, S086, S087, S092, S095, S101, S103. Told in nearly every talk.
2. **Columbia shuttle, 2003.** Engineer Rodney Rocha saw the foam strike on video and felt too low in the hierarchy to push. Lesson: weak signals need a voice. "It's not terror or fear. It's more like, I just can't." (S050). Sources: S050, S074, S079, S083, S092, S099.
3. **Alan Mulally at Ford.** Weeks of all-green charts; then one executive reported a problem, and Mulally thanked him for "that clear line of sight". Lesson: the first honest report is the test. Sources: S092, S093, S105.
4. **The telco pilot.** A pilot rigged with the best reps and suburbs was a smashing success; the launch missed most commitments and satisfaction fell from the 90s to 13. "failed by succeeding it should have succeeded by failing" (S083). Sources: S074, S083, S085, S094, S099.
5. **Citibank $900 million.** A clerk ticked the wrong box and wired the principal. Lesson: basic does not mean small. Sources: S050, S061, S072, S074, S083, S094, S099.
6. **Eli Lilly Alimta.** A failed trial, a failure party, then data showed low-folic-acid patients did not respond; adding the vitamin made a multi-billion drug. Lesson: learning does not guarantee success but skipping it guarantees none. Sources: S074, S083, S092, S094, S099.
7. **Ray Dalio, 1982.** A huge contrarian bet lost everything; he borrowed from his father. She says it fails the no-bigger-than-necessary test, so it was a failure he learned from, not an intelligent one. Sources: S055, S071, S074, S094, S096, S099.
8. **Torrey Canyon, 1967.** Many small things plus a blamed captain. Lesson: complex failures offer many chances to break the chain, and one visible person is not the cause. Sources: S073, S074, S083, S094, S099.
9. **Volkswagen and Wells Fargo.** Stretch goals and fear led staff to cheat rather than say no. "it wasn't a lie because they were bad people it was a lie because he had communicated in no uncertain terms" (S078). Sources: S078, S079, S087, S088, S092, S093.
10. **Toyota's Fujio Cho and the Andon cord.** A boasting manager is met with puzzlement; problems are what the team works on. Pulls are cheap and thanked, and most end without a stop. Sources: S050, S087, S093, S094, S101.
11. **Google's Project Aristotle.** 180 teams; safety explained team differences. She was scooped and delighted; the finding came "not with my help" (S105). Sources: S047, S056, S066, S068, S079, S084, S092, S098, S105.
12. **Pixar's Brain Trust and Ed Catmull.** Peer critique of the film, not the person; the owner keeps the decision. Sources: S077, S079, S082, S093.
13. **The 23 ICUs.** Flat, inclusive units had an 18 percent better improvement in morbidity and mortality than units with a status gap; the medical director's inclusive words erased the gap. Sources: S049, S084, S092.
14. **Cynthia Carroll at Anglo American.** Closed the mines for workers to design safety. Sources: S056, S079, S087.
15. **Buckminster Fuller.** Her first boss, in his late 80s, told his collapsed dome with relish. "The only mistake we make is thinking we shouldn't make mistakes." (S068). Sources: S047, S063, S068, S071, S077, S096.
16. **Chilean mine rescue, 2010.** A 23-year-old's drilling idea became Plan B because the leader listened. Sources: S067, S082.
17. **United captain Ben Berman.** Opens each crew briefing: "I have never flown a perfect flight and it won't happen today either I need to hear from you" (S074). Sources: S074, S083, S094, S099, S104.
18. **Jennifer Heemstra's lab.** About nine of ten experiments fail; she tells students they will fail all day. Sources: S074, S083, S086, S094, S099.
19. **Alcoa and Paul O'Neill.** A zero-injury goal, with his home number given to every worker; income grew fivefold. Sources: S099.
20. **The electric maze.** Students step like storks; the fix is to reward the beep. "It's just data, right?" (S094). Sources: S074, S083, S094, S099.
