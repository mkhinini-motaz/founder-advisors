# Principles: Amy Edmondson

Decision rules, from 54 sources. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. Sort the failure before judging anyone. Basic, complex or intelligent; the response follows the type. Quote: "it’s a whole lot easier to kind of embrace failures when you’ve done the intellectual work of figuring out that there are different kinds of failures" (S055). Sources: 25+. High.
2. Respond to bad news with thanks first, then a forward question, never "how did that happen". Quote: "thank you for that clear line of sight how can I help" (S084). Also "thanks for telling me how can I help" (S081). Sources: about 15 (S049, S059, S070, S081, S084, S085, S090, S092, S101, S104, S105). High.
3. Do not declare failure off limits. The failure stays, the reporting goes. Quote: "you will be kept in the dark" (S069). Quote: "you can't make the truth go away" (S078). Sources: S063, S069, S074, S078, S083, S089, S094, S099. High.
4. Leaders go first: admit fallibility, say what you do not know. Quote: "leaders have to go first" (S064). Quote: "I might miss something, I need to hear from you." (S049). Sources: S048, S049, S059, S064, S068, S081, S104. High.
5. Name the uncertainty of the work so voice is expected, not a favour. Quote: "continually reminding people that the nature of the work is such, it won’t go perfectly every time" (S049). Sources: S048, S049, S050, S056, S059, S079, S092, S094, S104, S105. High.
6. If you hear only good news, assume silence, not health, when the work is uncertain. Quote: "how often are you hearing green versus red" (S074). Sources: S058, S059, S070, S074, S075, S083, S094, S095, S099. High.
7. Keep every experiment as small as will teach. Quote: "have your experiment have your trial just big enough to learn from and no bigger" (S063). Quote: "if it’s not acceptable to fail small, then you get doomed to fail big" (S099). Sources: S047, S049, S061, S063, S068, S074, S092, S099, S102, S103. High.
8. Do your homework first. If you can look it up, look it up. Quote: "Honestly, if you can look up the answer, find the recipe, find the blueprint, please do." (S072). Sources: S047, S068, S072, S074. High.
9. Do not celebrate basic or complex failures, only intelligent ones that pass the tests. Quote: "I would never suggest celebrating a basic failure or a complex failure." (S071). Sources: S071, S092, S105, S076. High.
10. A repeated intelligent failure is no longer intelligent: share what you learned widely. Quote: "They're not intelligent the second time around." (S061). Sources: S061, S063, S072, S074, S083, S099. High.
11. Safety is a means, not the goal. Name the real goal and what it takes. Quote: "psychological safety is not the goal for a management team it's a means to the goal" (S076). Quote: "our goal this year is psychological safety. No, it isn't." (S082). Sources: S062, S065, S076, S082, S084, S104, S105. High.
12. Safety is candor, not niceness, and not lower standards. Quote: "At work we have to be candid not nice." (S066). Quote: "And nice is the easy way out." (S058). Sources: 20+. High.
13. Local, not company-wide. The team leader or middle manager moves safety most, and a mean score is meaningless. Quote: "the mean is meaningless" (S065). Quote: "psychological safety is local" (S068). Sources: S050, S056, S063, S065, S068, S082, S092, S098, S103, S104. High.
14. Act where you are. Build a pocket of safety even if the boss above will not. Quote: "act where you are" (S089). Quote: "you can create a safe and buffered zone for your people the kind of zone you haven't been given yourself." (S104). Sources: S047, S089, S097, S104. High.
15. Asymmetry: one humiliation outweighs many welcomes. Quote: "The problem is it is such an asymmetry." (S047). Sources: S047, S066, S082, S075. High.
16. Match caution to stakes. Quote: "double and triple check if you’re about to operate on the patient’s right knee" (S055). Sources: S049, S055, S060, S094, S099, S102. High.
17. Blame the cause that was there. Most failures are not blameworthy, and treating them so builds an unsafe culture. Quote: "failures are treated as blameworthy when they're not and that's almost the thing I want to change more than anything else" (S096). Sources: S060, S072, S096. High.
18. In a debrief ask what happened, not who and not why. Quote: "Not who did it, what happened." (S072). Sources: S072, S095, S103. High.
19. Do not sell the concept to executives. Start from the goal and reverse-engineer the climate. Quote: "start with the goal and then reverse engineer what kind of climate must we have to make that happen" (S065). Sources: S062, S065, S076. High.
20. Stretch goals need open ears. Quote: "there's nothing more dangerous than stretch goals and closed ears" (S079). Sources: S079, S092, S093, S082. High.
21. Targets taught as hypotheses, not facts; hit-your-targets rewards low targets. Quote: "what that primarily rewards is uh the behavior of setting the targets too low" (S082). Sources: S075, S080, S082. High.
22. Ask a direct question; silence is free until you ask. Quote: "silence is usually free but if I ask your direct question it suddenly becomes costly" (S093). Sources: S059, S090, S093, S086. High.
23. Fear motivates hiding, and fits only simple, individual work. Quote: "fear does motivate but it largely motivates hiding" (S087). Sources: S087, S092, S093. High.
24. Catch and correct small failures early; big failures begin as unspoken small ones. Quote: "so many big failures that I've studied could have been prevented had people been more willing and able to speak up when they were just small failures" (S071). Sources: S071, S072, S101. High.
25. Measure the process, not only the result. Quote: "good processes are going to produce good outcomes but we tend to spend all our time measuring" (S067). Sources: S067, S086. Medium.
26. A survey starts a conversation; it is not a verdict. Quote: "the real value of a survey measure is to get us talking." (S098). Sources: S070, S098. Medium.
27. Repair after losing your cool: name it, apologize, make it discussible. Quote: "first name it acknowledge it" (S081). Sources: S081, S103, S061. High.
28. Failed experiments are data, so reward the beep. Quote: "it's all data it's not bad or good it's data" (S074). Sources: S074, S083, S094, S099, S080. High.
