# Positions: Nassim Nicholas Taleb

Built from 14 sources (2004 to 2024). Dates are the source dates. Lines credited to "the paper" are co-authored (S034, S035, S048). Topics with no evidence say so.

- **Raising money and venture capital**: "Packaging, because they're trying to sell it to another person. It's a beauty contest." (S045, 2024). Funding became selling to the next buyer (S011, 2023). Old rule: "I come from old finance, old-school finance where you haven't really succeeded until the company gets a strong cash flow base." (S045, 2024)
- **Debt and leverage**: leverage is the opposite of redundancy (S017, 2009). "Redundancy, on the other hand, is the equivalent of de-leveraging" (S017).
- **Focus versus diversification**: "I’m a one-trick pony, I think of nothing else but tail risk." (S011, 2023). For money: a barbell, not the middle (S032, 2018).
- **Growth and expansion**: "You cannot be a promiscuous entrepreneur if you're an artisan." (S032, 2018). Consultants "want you to expand until you end up in bankruptcy." Paraphrase of S032.
- **Company size**: "come back as a decentralized system of cockroaches rather than elephants." (S012, 2019). "The life cycle of large corporations is compressed." (S032, 2018)
- **Small caps and dominant firms**: "I like small caps regardless of the returns." (S032, 2018). He says he has not tested it on data (S032).
- **Business ideas**: "You're not going to make money on a business that makes sense, because a lot of people have tried it." (S045, 2024)
- **Restaurants**: do not open one; prizes go to places that then close (S012, 2019). Successful owners open a second and go to the edge of bankruptcy (S032, 2018).
- **Selling the company**: "Someone else will buy our company or we’re packaging a company to sell it to someone else." (S011, 2023), said as a criticism.
- **Planning**: "A rigid business plan gets one locked into a preset invariant policy, like a highway without exits" (S041, undated). Plan short, keep exits.
- **Forecasting and prediction**: "I've never seen a rich forecaster." (S045, 2024). "predictions are self-fulfilling and also self-canceling." (S011, 2023)
- **Metrics and statistics**: "Conventional metrics based on type 1 randomness fail to produce reliable results" (S017, 2009). "Big data may mean more information, but it also means more false information." (S052, 2013)
- **Risk management and banks**: "the healer killing the patient" (S016) of quants. Banks lost more than they ever earned (S016). "Since 2008, risk has migrated from banks to hedge funds." (S011, 2023)
- **Bonuses and incentives**: managers "do not have to pay out reverse bonuses for negative performance" (S035, the paper). Pay windows shorter than the risk reward hiding it (S016).
- **Consultants and experts**: "it's much easier to macro BS than micro BS" (S012, 2019). Experts are those with feedback from reality (S010, 2018).
- **Hiring**: "If they make money at something and you don't understand how, hire them" (S012, 2019). A correlation cannot select one hire: "You can't use it for any individual hire. You need a batch." (S045, 2024)
- **Your own work and hours**: "Eliminate from your life any kind of dull work. In other words, if work is not intense, just don't do work." (S032, 2018). Artisans need no personal assistant (S032).
- **Burnout and stress**: add variation, not comfort: "it's about subjecting yourself to variation." (S032, 2018). The danger is the dose: "The danger is in the dose." (S011, 2023)
- **Supply chains and globalization**: "it’s the size of the door that matters when you want to get out, not the size of the theater." (S011, 2023)
- **Regulation**: Brussels regulates vacuum cleaners and misses borders and pandemics (paraphrase, S011). Prefers rules that need no state: "A heuristic, unlike a regulation, does not require state intervention for implementation." (S035, the paper)
- **Pandemics**: cut connectivity early, lower the scale (S045, 2024). "If I die of COVID, the odds of my neighbor dying of COVID has increased." (S011)
- **GMOs and technology**: GMOs fall under precaution because they spread; nuclear power in small amounts does not (S034, the paper, 2014). Contested; take as a method, not a business position.
- **AI**: not a precaution case now; "You can't put constraints ahead of time on research." (S045, 2024). "ChatGPT is designed to tell you what makes sense based on current information." (S045)
- **Reputation**: "I only care about my reputation in some circles." (S011, 2023). "Today, you are vulnerable to reputational violence." (S032, 2018)
- **Marketing and press**: "You can bypass conventional media by staying in the online field." (S032, 2018). "Haters are going to hate." (S011)
- **Social media**: good, a gossip machine (paraphrase, S010, 2018).
- **Partners and equity**: no recorded position.
- **Pricing and discounting**: no recorded position.
- **First hire, firing, culture, remote work**: no recorded position.
- **Metrics for a startup (growth, unit economics)**: no recorded position beyond cash flow and tail exposure.
- **Competition and timing**: imitators "will also -- of course -- be late in exiting." (S032, 2018). No recorded view on market timing.
- **Changes of mind**: on mass hysteria, in 2018 he says he made mistakes in Fooled by Randomness (S032). On the vaccine, he waited for data from about a billion jabs (S011, 2023). In 2024 he says he changed his mind on "one sentence" only (S045).
