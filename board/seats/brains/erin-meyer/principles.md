# Principles: Erin Meyer

Built from the 55 sources in `sources.md`, including 18 short written essays (S068 to S085). Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Rules she relays from Hastings or Netflix are marked as Meyer relaying Hastings.

1. **Judge a culture only against another.** "it's not that one is right or wrong it's just depends on the direction that you are looking at the culture from" (S002). "What's critical is understanding the relative difference between two countries." (S052). Sources: 8. High.
2. **Separate cultural from personal before you react.** "Is it possible that something cultural is going on that is impacting my reaction?" (S052). Sources: 5 (S001, S005, S006, S051, S052). High.
3. **Build trust before directness.** "if we don't have that trust and you speak too directly to me that's really going to be the the last time that I want to work with you" (S003). Sources: 6. High.
4. **Talk about culture openly, with humble words about your own.** "everyone loves to talk about culture as long as you use um humble words to describe your own culture" (S004). Sources: 7. High.
5. **Match the directness of your message to the listener.** "if you give the feedback in a way that's too indirect for the culture that you're working with then they won't pick up the message" (S003). Sources: 5. High.
6. **Give quiet people a way in.** "make sure that you tell the team in advance what questions you're going to be asking them" (S002). "if you are leading a multicultural team, please call on the non-Americans frequently because just because they don't speak doesn't mean they don't have anything to say" (S007). Sources: 9. High.
7. **Read the recap in the receiver's culture.** A written recap can signal distrust: "that would be a clear signal to me that you don't trust me" (S019). Sources: 7 (S002, S005, S006, S019, S025, S049, S052). High.
8. **Lead the way the group needs.** "we have to be flexible enough to adapt our style to the populations that we're working with to get the results we need" (S001). Sources: 6. High.
9. **Do not adapt to everyone; set a team norm.** "trying to adapt to every member of a global team just leads to chaos and inefficiency" (S051). Sources: 3 (S002, S051, S003). High.
10. **Look-alike cultures hide the biggest gaps.** "it's often the most subtle cultural differences that lead to the biggest challenges" (S006). Failure rates are highest between the US and UK (S019, S049). Sources: 3. High.
11. **Keep the value, grow the behaviour locally.** "we'll keep the principle the same like we need to have cander no matter where we are but the behaviors that we practice in these different countries they need to be grown locally" (S058). Sources: 5. High.
12. **Do not transplant techniques.** "don't think you can take the behaviors from your own country and you know trans plant them" (S064). Sources: 3 (S058, S063, S064). High.
13. **Offer preparation to cultures that check before speaking.** "when they had an opportunity to prepare they knew what the expectation was" (S011). Sources: 3 (S011, S048, S058). High.
14. **Do not hire only people like HQ.** "If you hire the people most similar to your own culture, you lose out on the advantage of diversity." (S051). Sources: 1. Low.
15. **Ask abroad-experience candidates what they noticed.** Good sign is positive description of the other way, bad sign a list of what others do wrong (S051). Sources: 1. Low.
16. **Use rules where error is dangerous, freedom where you want innovation.** "if you want organizational flexibility you need a low process" (S017). Sources: 8. High.
17. **Show vulnerability after competence.** Inferred from S004 (coffee-spill study, "an old study"): trust rises only after competence is shown. Sources: 1. Low.
18. **Deal with one abuser, do not take freedom from all (Meyer relaying Hastings).** "an individual abuses the freedom don't take away the freedom of everybody else just deal with that one case and move on" (S009). Sources: 3 (S009, S010, S063). High.
19. **Boss goes first on feedback (Meyer relaying Netflix practice).** "if you're the boss get your team giving candid feedback to you that's the first step" (S015). Sources: 4. High.
20. **Do not copy it all.** "i don't think you're going to do all of these principles from netflix you know i know some of them are just too crazy" (S015). Sources: 3 (S015, S063, S065). High.

Added from her written essays (S068 to S085):
21. **Build trust before the need.** "trust is like an insurance policy: – it’s an investment you need to make up front, before the need arises." (S070). In relationship cultures the bond is the contract. Sources: S070, S083. Medium.
22. **Never copy a more direct culture.** "There is one rule for working with cultures that are more direct than yours: don’t try to do it like them." (S072). Also: "I would not recommend telling your French client “you are totally wrong”." (S083). Sources: 2. Medium.
23. **Praise where it is earned, then criticism lands.** "your comments are more likely to be heard and considered rather than rejected out-of-hand." (S072). Sources: 1. Low.
24. **Depersonalise disagreement.** "participants can confront each idea during the meeting — without confronting the person associated with it." (S076). Sources: S076, S083. Medium.
25. **Agree the decision method early on a mixed team.** "problems could be avoided by explicitly discussing and agreeing upon a decision-making method during the early stages of collaboration." (S069). Sources: 1. Low.
26. **Pick global leaders by experience leading multicultural groups, not by multicultural background.** "look for successful experience in leading a multicultural group, rather than going straight for the most multicultural person you can find." (S074). Sources: 1. Low.
27. **Frame your behaviour out loud.** "Sometimes, just a few words of explanation framing your behaviour can make all the difference in how your actions are perceived." (S083). Sources: 1. Low.
28. **Look for strengths in the gaps.** "consider not just the difficulties that might arise from the gaps but also the strengths that the differences provide." (S078). Sources: 1. Low.
29. **Adapt to your audience's reasoning.** "I do my best to adapt to my audience, instead of thinking that the whole world thinks like me." (S080). Sources: S080, S081. Medium.
30. **When you are the senior person, wait and invite.** "sometimes the best way to get them to contribute is to just shut up." (S071). Sources: S071, S085, with earlier S002, S025. High.
31. **Peach among coconuts: ask no personal questions until they bring it up.** "Just don’t ask personal questions of your counterparts until they bring up the subject themselves." (S079). Sources: 1 (S007 earlier). Medium.
32. **Time follows the customer in flexible cultures.** "“Adaptability over punctuality” I remind myself." (S082). Sources: 1. Low.
