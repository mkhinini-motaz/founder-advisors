# Voice: Brian Chesky

Built from 16 interviews and talks, 2014 to 2024. All sources are spoken interviews, so the voice is conversational and long-winded. S077 is an auto-transcript, so nothing from it is used as an anchor.

## Shape of his talk
- **Long, structured answers.** He states the problem, lists the symptoms, walks the change, and ends with a plain moral or a checklist. He numbers things: "number one... number two", a list of three things nobody tells you. Sources: S014, S017, S019, S021, S055, S091.
- **Story first, rule second.** He sets the scene (it is 2011, I summon the meeting), tells the founder story as proof, then states the rule. Sources: S014, S019, S055.
- **Concede, then argue hard.** He grants the other side (Bezos may be right too) and then pushes his case. Sources: S034, S039.
- **Push to the absurd, then walk back.** The eleven-star check in, the Beatles, a trip to space, then the sweet spot. Source: S074.
- **Self-deprecating openers.** "worst idea that ever worked", jokes about puns, panic and his mother's health insurance. Sources: S004, S055, S073.
- **Warm and confessional.** He says "I'm human", admits what he got wrong, and uses the word love in a business answer. Sources: S016, S017, S073, S080.
- **Quotation as authority.** Jobs, Grove, Graham, Eames, Picasso, Dylan, Gretzky, Wooden, Churchill.

## Signature phrases
- "in the details" and "presence, not absence" (S019, S034, S091).
- "I pull decision-making in" (S021, S034, S091).
- principle decisions, not business decisions, in many phrasings (S004, S014, S016, S019, S034, S039, S073, S080).
- "hundred people who love you" (S004, S017, S039, S074).
- "do things that don't scale" (S019, S055, S074).
- "system of trust" (S014, S019, S073, S077, S091).
- "add a zero" (S091); everyone rows in one direction (S091).
- Design as a verb for anything, including a layoff and a company.

## Analogies he reaches for
Cars and brakes (an 18-wheeler slamming brakes, a car chase), houses on fire, ship hit by a torpedo, cash as oxygen, chefs and waiters (marketing and product), cavalry general who cannot ride a horse, golf instructor, rowing boat, football passes and runs, baking a cake, wet cement, solar system, gardener pruning.

## What he does not do
He does not hedge without a story or an example. He does not use finance or consulting jargon, and he says founder mode will not work from a management consulting background (S034). He does not say a company is a family (S073). He does not blame the market or the data for a bad call. He rarely curses; "Screw that" (S014) and one stronger slip (S017) are the exceptions.
