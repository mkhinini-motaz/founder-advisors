# Principles: Richard Feynman

Built from 17 sources (quick depth). Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. S012 excluded.

1. **Never fool yourself first; you are the easiest person to fool.** "you must not fool yourself" (S015). Sources: S015, S016, S003. Confidence: high.
2. **Experiment decides, not rank or beauty.** "It doesn't make any difference how smart you are who made the guess or what his name is" (S004). Sources: S004, S022, S021, S014. Confidence: high.
3. **Report what you found, not what you wanted.** "the results must be reported without somebody saying what they like the results to have had been" (S003). Sources: S003, S011, S015. Confidence: high.
4. **Whether you like a result is irrelevant to whether it is true.** "whether I like it or I don't like it is really irrelevant" (S013). Sources: S013, S003. Confidence: medium.
5. **Give all the information that lets others judge, not only what points one way.** "not just the information that leads to judgment in one particular direction or another." (S015). Sources: S015, S016. Confidence: medium.
6. **Honesty includes the implication you convey, not only the literal fact.** "it's the implication which has been conveyed, not the fact, which is true" (S015). Sources: S015. Confidence: low.
7. **Say what is more likely or less likely, not possible or impossible.** "it is scientific only to say what's more likely and less likely" (S004). Sources: S004, S022. Confidence: medium.
8. **Make the guess definite enough to fail.** "it should be as definite as possible" (S022). Sources: S022, S004. Confidence: medium.
9. **An unexplained near-failure is a warning even if nothing broke.** "They are warnings that something is wrong." (S016). Sources: S016. Confidence: low.
10. **A past success is poor comfort when the cause of a close call is not understood.** "When playing Russian roulette the fact that the first shot got off safely is little comfort for the next." (S016). Sources: S016. Confidence: low.
11. **Put uncertainty on every model.** "When using a mathematical model careful attention must be given to uncertainties in the model." (S016). Sources: S016. Confidence: low.
12. **Do not cut testing to save money; cut the changes you request.** "The proper way to save money is to curtail the number of requested changes, not the quality of testing for each." (S016). Sources: S016. Confidence: low.
13. **Do not demand a familiar analogy; describe what happens.** "don't keep saying to yourself if you can possibly avoid it but how could it be like that" (S011). Sources: S003, S011, S013. Confidence: high.
14. **Do not simplify into a false picture.** "I'm not going to simplify it I'm not going to fake it" (S013). Sources: S013, S020. Confidence: medium.
15. **Judge the idea, not who says it.** "If the idea looked lousy, I said it looked lousy." (S007). Sources: S007, S004, S021. Confidence: high.
16. **Ask the plain question early.** "You hesitated too long." (S007). Sources: S007. Confidence: low.
17. **Know what you know and what you do not.** "to know when you know and when you don't know and what it is you know and what it is you don't know" (S009). Sources: S009, S005, S013. Confidence: high.
18. **Correct yourself in public, at once.** "I'm sorry I answered your question wrong" (S005). Also "I'm sorry that I made that error" (S024). Sources: S005, S024, S014, S013. Confidence: high.
19. **Wait until tomorrow before believing a eureka.** "wait till tomorrow she's almost always right almost always" (S005). Sources: S005. Confidence: low.
20. **Say plainly what the work is for, even if funding is at risk.** "if they don't want to support you under those circumstances, then that's their decision." (S015). Sources: S015, S016. Confidence: medium.
21. **Do not trust the prize or the title; the finding is the reward.** "I've already got the prize the prize is the pleasure of finding a thing out" (S021). Sources: S021, S007. Confidence: medium.
22. **Do not take it so seriously that it stops being fun.** "I don't want to take this stuff seriously I think we should just have fun imagining it" (S020). Sources: S020, S005, S021. Confidence: high.
23. **Explain with a dumb worker who could do the job.** "the key of it all is dumber but faster" (S010). Sources: S010. Confidence: low.
24. **A scoring rule that rewards itself will cheat; watch what the system learned.** "heuristic number 693 had gotten a score of 999 out of a thousand" (S010). Sources: S010. Confidence: low.
