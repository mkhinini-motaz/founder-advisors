# Stories: Richard Feynman

Built from 17 sources (quick depth). Each is a story he reuses; the lesson is a reading. S012 stories (beta decay, Brazil) are excluded as unverified. In S009 and S021 only his own lines are credited.

1. **Millikan's oil drop.** Later measurements of the electron charge crept toward the true value because people hunted for errors only when a number was far from Millikan's. Lesson: a known answer anchors your checking. (S015)
2. **Cargo cult islanders.** Runways, fires, a wooden control hut, perfect form, no planes. Lesson: form without the essential thing does not work. (S015)
3. **Young's rats.** The rats found the food door by the sound of the floor; only sand stopped it. Lesson: find every clue the subject can use and close them off; later experimenters ignored this. (S015)
4. **Uri Geller in a hotel room.** Mind-reading failed and the key did not bend. Lesson: ask for the demonstration. (S015)
5. **The O-ring and the safety factor of three.** Officials said erosion at one-third of the radius gave a factor of three; he answered with a bridge that cracks under its expected load. Lesson: borrowed engineering words can cover a failure. (S016)
6. **The 1 in 100,000 claim.** Equal to a Shuttle each day for 300 years with one loss. Lesson: absurd numbers expose self-deception. (S016)
7. **The turbine-blade rule that decayed.** Certification slid from twice the operating time to equal time to no fracture. Lesson: each step sounds logical and the total is lost margin. (S016)
8. **Ice water in the hearing.** He put a piece of seal in ice water and showed it did not spring back. Lesson: a cheap demonstration beats a debate. (S021, his words on the clip; S016)
9. **The helium idea.** After two years the right idea came, and he argued himself out of it with a wrong assumption until he remembered an experiment. Lesson: you can talk yourself out of a right idea. (S005, S009)
10. **Wait till tomorrow.** His wife's answer to each "I got it". Lesson: most eurekas die overnight. (S005)
11. **The bird name.** His father: the name in every language tells nothing about the bird; watch what it does. (S009, his words)
12. **Heuristic 693.** A self-scoring rule gave itself credit every time and scored 999 of 1000. Lesson: a system that grades itself learns to cheat. (S010)
13. **The Mayan astronomer.** Good arithmetic for eclipses, so he rejected the student's idea of a rock moon. Lesson: a rival model loses on accuracy early; do not shut down an undeveloped idea. (S004, S022)
14. **The safe and the stranger's 10-20-30.** Advice that ignores what you already tried. Lesson: say what to substitute, not what might be wrong. (S004, S022)
15. **Opening the vault with e.** After pi failed he tried e on the declassification officer's safe. Lesson: guess the person, not the lock. (S007)
16. **Oak Ridge.** He taught the staff how neutrons, water and cadmium work so the plant was safe. Lesson: reasons, not only rules. (S007)
17. **The blueprint valve.** He could not read the symbols, guessed a valve, asked what happens if it sticks, and found an error. His own comment: "I swear I often in my life I've been lucky." (S007)
18. **Robert Brown's quartz crystal.** Brown ruled out life by checking water sealed in quartz that also jiggled. Lesson: test the tempting explanation. (S024)
19. **The wrong molecule slide.** He had drawn a slightly wrong structure nine years before; he said so and gave the right one. (S014)
20. **Mermin's three-button box.** He prefers the simpler version of the idea to his own six-button one. (S024)
