# Principles: Ben Horowitz

Built from 28 sources (2010 to 2026). Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. Decide, even when both options hurt. "any decision, even the wrong decision, is better than no decision." (S055) "if you wait too long before you pull the trigger, you're not smart anymore." (S024). 6 sources. High.
2. Run at the thing you are avoiding. "It's running towards the darkness." (S007) "You have to run at the pain in darkness." (S024). 6 sources. High.
3. If everyone likes the decision, you added nothing. "If everybody agrees with the decision, then you didn't add any value because they would have done that without you." (S023) Also S007. 2 sources. Medium.
4. Make bad news cheap to deliver. "you're not allowed in the staff meeting unless you tell me something broken in the organization." (S046) "bad news travels fast." (S010). 5 sources. High.
5. Tell the company the truth about a threat. "I called an all-hands and told the whole company that we were getting our asses kicked, and if we didn’t stop the bleeding, we were going to die." (S003) Also S004, S046. 3 sources. High.
6. Define the behaviour, not the value. "you can't define like the ideal of something you want you have to define the exact behavior" (S014). Also S007, S010, S022, S026, S043, S085. 8 sources. High.
7. Put the system above one person's feelings once the culture is set. "you have to value the system and value that above that person’s feelings." (S007). 1 source. Low.
8. Never criticize an entrepreneur in a moment of weakness. "We don't criticize entrepreneurs in their, like, moment of weakness." (S022) "do not lay them out on Twitter that's off culture" (S043). 4 sources with S024, S026. High.
9. Do not develop executives you cannot teach. "Your results will suck while you work on a task that you cannot complete." (S055) Also S010, S014, S023. 4 sources. High.
10. Learn the job before you hire for it. "just try to do the job yourself, act in that role" (S010) "get trained up on like, what is that job" (S046). 4 sources with S007, S055. High.
11. One person decides. "you need somebody who's going to break the tie" (S026) "the CEO position is a chain of command position" (S041). 6 sources. High.
12. Companies are not democracies. "Companies are not democracies." (S026) "a dictatorship always beats a democracy" (S026). Culture by staff vote is wrong. 2 sources with S027 ("benevolent dictatorship"). Medium.
13. In wartime, drop the old plan and skip the vote. "it's better to be right than consistent." (S022) "you can't listen to your team because they're on the old plan, in the old structure with the old idea." (S022). 3 sources with S001, S061. High, with the caveat that it is for the war period only.
14. Judge by strength. "we're investing in strength, not lack of weakness." (S023). 4 sources. High.
15. Squash conflict early. "the deeper you bury them, the hotter they get." (S064) "So there is no problem that gets better over time in terms of conflict." (S064). 2 sources with S007. Medium.
16. Do not expect fairness. "do not expect anything to be fair." (S023) "Life isn't fair." (S024). 2 sources. Medium.
17. Give no credit for predicting rain. "no credit will be given for predicting rain, only credit for building an arc." (S023) Also S055. 2 sources. Medium.
18. Control your destiny with cash. "I want to control my destiny." (S055) "there's only one unforgivable sin in business, and that's running out of money" (S026). 2 sources. Medium.
19. Hold the big ambition, because the work is the same. "it is just as hard to start a small boutique thing that means nothing in the world and build it as it is to build the world-dominating monster." (S041) "You work the same amount of hours. And so you might as well go for doing something important." (S043). 2 sources. Medium.
20. Think for yourself. "Don’t listen to your friends. Think for yourself." (S055) "anybody great really thinks for themselves." (S010). 3 sources with S061. High.
21. Follow your contribution, not your passion. "follow your contribution." (S055). Also S021 ("what can you contribute"). 2 sources. Medium.
22. Do not hedge on principle. "I don't like to hedge it." (S023) "you can't outlaw math" (S087). Also S027, S085. 4 sources. High.
23. Train people; it is the highest-leverage task. "Training is, quite simply, one of the highest-leverage activities a manger can perform." (S005) "teach them" at scale in S024. 3 sources with S007. High.
24. Teach, do not yell. "If you find yourself yelling at people, like, you probably have to explain what you want." (S023) Also S002, S007. 3 sources. High.
25. Keep your word and be on time. "first class business and only in a first class way" (S027). Also S024. 2 sources. Medium.
26. Do not hire from monopolies for selling. "companies who try and recruit their sellers out of monopolies is just silly because that's not really selling. That's just, you know, accepting orders." (S046). Also S010, S055. 3 sources. High.
27. A failed company does not mean a bad CEO. "a failed company to me doesn't mean that the CEO is no. good." (S046). 1 source. Low.
28. Early is not wrong. "if you're too early, you can make the bet again." (S021) Also S087. 2 sources. Medium.
