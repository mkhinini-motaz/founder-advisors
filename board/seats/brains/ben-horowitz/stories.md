# Stories: Ben Horowitz

Built from 28 sources (2010 to 2026). One or two lines each, with the lesson.

1. **The IPO from hell (Loudcloud, 2001).** Public at 18 months old with about $2M trailing revenue, 340 employees, weeks of cash, the press mocking it. Lesson: the bad decision beat no decision. A call from his father-in-law's hospital came mid-IPO; he stayed. (S003, S023, S046, S061)
2. **Three layoffs, 400 people, then a $1.6B sale to HP.** Doug Leone said no company recovers from consecutive layoffs; Opsware did. Lesson: how you lay off decides the culture after. (S004)
3. **Andy Grove and the late employee.** Grove told a latecomer that time was all he had and the man was wasting it; nobody was late again. Lesson: one vivid public rule sets culture. (S007, S010, S046)
4. **Toussaint Louverture.** A slave who built an army culture of trust: officers could not cheat on their wives, and he imported leaders from the target culture. Lesson: shock rules change culture. (S007, S022, S024, S061, S085)
5. **The $10 a minute fine.** A 45-minute wait as an entrepreneur led to a late fee for any a16z partner late to an entrepreneur meeting. Lesson: tie a value to a behavior and a penalty. (S014, S022, S061, S064)
6. **The Opsware offer near $4 a share.** He could not say why it was wrong, said no six times, sold at $14.25. Lesson: gut is synthesis, but only after experience. (S046, S055)
7. **Okta's first head of sales.** He pushed for the seller who qualified him, with references from people who owed him, over the enthusiastic one. Lesson: hire on courage and references, not charm. (S010)
8. **Databricks.** The founders asked for $200,000; he offered $10 million. He made the VP of Engineering, Ali Ghodsi, CEO and later told him a CEO cannot make people great. His board seat, he says, kept the company from selling for about $4B. Lesson: professors think small; decide. (S023, S026, S064)
9. **Zuckerberg 2007.** Traffic flat, the executive staff pushing a sale; Ben asked why and learned new engineers wrote straight to the database. Lesson: at 800 people nothing is shared, so teach. (S024)
10. **Why I did not go to jail.** A CFO's stock option practice he nearly adopted later sent her to jail; his General Counsel reporting to the CEO saved him. Lesson: design the organization so the lawyer can say no. (S055)
11. **The $820M raise that vanished.** June 2000 raise at $820M post-money; by year end no price worked and he went public at $560M. Lesson: capital markets are emotional. (S055)
12. **The Blind MC.** His friend Seth was shot in the face in 1986; Ben mailed him DJ tapes and they formed a rap group. The origin of the hip-hop in his posts. (S055, S024)
13. **Good PM / Bad PM.** Written at Netscape after yelling at his team; it spread across the Valley. Lesson: even smart people need expectations written down. (S002, S007, S023)
14. **Bill Campbell and the EDS deal.** Told him not to fly to New York for the announcement but to stay and tell employees where they stand. Lesson: be present with the people who stay. (S007, S055)
15. **Cash collection and the 8 a.m. meeting.** A NationBuilder CEO fixed collections with a daily "where's my money" meeting, a Grove technique. Lesson: fix communication by hand, unscalably, until it works. (S024)
16. **Skype.** A quarter of the first $300M fund went in; eBay owned the company but not the IP. Lesson: know what others do not. (S026)
17. **Panic on AI and crypto without a committee.** Daily panic call, a research team, every GP tested on AI; crypto approved in one conversation among three people. Lesson: one decision point makes you fast. (S043)
18. **Isaac Newton at the Mint.** He fixed counterfeiting from ground truth backwards; Ben compares Musk. Lesson: first principles. (S025)
