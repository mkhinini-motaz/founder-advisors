# Positions: Don Norman

Built from the 91 sources in `sources.md`. Where his view changed, both dates sit side by side. Dates are the recorded or posted date of the source.

## Human-centered versus activity-centered design
- 2005 (S015): "Great design, I contend, comes from breaking the rules"
- 2010 (S067): "Great designers do not use HCD."
- 2012 (S053): "Instead, I have argued for Activity-Centered Design, where the activity dictates the design."
- 2019 (S049): "Both are needed. Each supplements and complements the other."
- Reading, inferred from S015, S067, S053, S049: he moved from attacking HCD for mass products to a combined, community-centered view.

## Human-centered design, later correction
- 2020 (S009): "Many disciplines are good at problem solving, but few ask whether they are solving the correct problems."
- 2024 (S090): "Humanity centered design, if human centered design is HCD, humanity is HCD Same principles, but a bit more."
- 2019 (S049): "Today, I would modify the recommendations by adding lessons discussed below"

## Affordance
- 1999 (S035): "My fault: I was really talking about perceived affordances, which are not at all the same as real ones."
- 2008 (S081): "Designers of the world: Forget affordances provide signifiers."
- 2010 (S067): "“Affordances” is a useful concept gone wrong"
- 2013 (S048): "Affordances define what actions are possible. Signifiers specify how people discover those possibilities"

## Human error
- 1990 (S065): "One major step would be to remove the term “human error” from our vocabulary and to re-evaluate the need to blame individuals."
- 2010 (S067): "When in doubt, blame the victim."
- Reading, inferred from S065, S067: the position is stable; the 2010 line names the habit he opposes.

## Personas
- 2004 (S068): "So to me, the Persona is a tool for focus and an aid to communication"
- 2005 (S015): "honestly, now, did they really inform your design?"
- 2019 (S062): the authors cite a heading by Myerson against personas. That is Myerson's line, not his.

## Simplicity
- 2007 (S052): "Simplicity is highly overrated."
- 2010 (S067): "Things that are understandable are judged to be simple."
- Reading, inferred from S052, S067: he separates simple from understandable, and later says simplicity should not be the goal.

## Automation
- 2001 (S012): "Having a person around just-in-case of failure of an automated system is the worst of worlds."
- 2014 (S019): "Aim for collaboration, not supervision."
- 2017 (S036): "We need to think less about the design of human-machine interfaces (HMI) and more about the design of human-machine teamwork."
- 2024 (S091): "I'm then still in charge."
- Reading, inferred from S012, S019, S091: from automate fully or support the human, to an AI assistant with the person in charge.

## Emotion and beauty
- 2002 (S013): "Negative affect can make it harder to do even easy tasks: positive affect can make it easier to do difficult tasks."
- 2013 (S048): "There was no discussion about pleasure, enjoyment, or emotion." (about the first edition, 1988)
- 2002 (S013): "equal to beauty, equal to function: equal, but not superior."

## Metaphor
- 1999 and later (S080): "I personally believe that metaphors are more harmful than useful"
- Also in S080 he lists metaphor among four principles for new screen users. Both positions sit in one essay.

## Design research and breakthroughs
- 2010 (S033): "design research is great when it comes to improving existing product categories but essentially useless when it comes to new, innovative breakthroughs."
- 2010 (S023): "we have many myths about the power of design research, brainstorming, rapid prototyping, iterative test and design, but zero evidence."
- 2011 (S075): "Always be researching. Always be acting."
