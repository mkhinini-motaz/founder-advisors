# Positions: Nir Eyal

Built from 240 of his essays on nirandfar.com, 2012 to 2025. Newest view first where he changed his mind. Where the corpus is silent the entry says no recorded position.

## Is habit design ethical?
- 2012-07-03: "The matrix seeks to help you answer not, “Can I hook users?” but “Should I attempt to?”" (S003)
- 2018-01-05: "Getting people to do something they didn’t want to do is no longer persuasion — it’s coercion." (S042)

## Should businesses encourage addiction?
- 2012-08-28: "And while businesses should never try and encourage addiction, the fact is that like slots, technology products with finite variability do not form long-term habits in most users." (S344)

## Are tech companies responsible when users abuse a product?
- 2014-06-02: "Standing idly by while reaping the rewards of users abusing their product is no longer acceptable — it is exploitation." (S015)
- 2017-05-03: "it is unethical to accept patronage from someone a company knows wants to stop using your product but can’t." (S019)

## Is social media addictive?
Changed view.
- 2013-09-19: the S014 essay argued a phone addiction story. His later note on it: "I no longer agree with this article." (S014)
- 2021-07-21: "The overwhelming majority of social media users are NOT pathologically addicted." (S016)

## What is addiction?
- 2018-06-04: "Addiction is a compulsive harmful behavior." (S110)
- 2020-02-10: "Addiction is a pathology. It is not simply liking something a lot." (S139)

## Is technology mind control?
- 2021-07-19: "we are far more powerful than the technology that is supposedly mind-controlling us. It’s not even close." (S072)

## Should governments regulate tech addiction?
- 2021-03-09: "Moral panics are often based on half-truths." (S113)
- 2021-05-23: he backs targeted rules (an offer of help, easy cancel) and warns against rules that make products worse. Paraphrase of S254.

## Does Hooked teach the right thing?
Changed view, with doubt.
- 2016-03-05: "However, the byproduct of making technology better is that sometimes it’s so good people can’t seem to put it down." (S160)
- 2018-05-21: "A few years ago I wrote a book, Hooked, about how technology can be used to change our habits. I intended the book to teach startups how to build healthy habits, but now I’m not so sure." (S158)

## Is he himself hooked?
- 2014-12-22: "There’s just one problem–I can’t put my technology down." (S240)

## Should every product be habit-forming?
- 2016-09-14: "Not every business needs to be habit-forming." (S007)
- 2017-03-16: "Sorry, you don’t meet the test. Your product will never become a habit." (S191)

## Are habits permanent?
- 2012-08-28: "behaviors are LIFO — last in, first out." (S344)

## Habit or routine for hard work?
- 2021-11-29: "A habit is an impulse to do a behavior with little or no conscious thought." (S024)
- 2023-04-24: "A habit is a behavior that requires no conscious thought, and that is certainly not writing." (S134)

## What is the opposite of distraction?
- 2020-10-26: "The opposite of distraction is not “focus.” It’s “traction.”" (S069)

## What drives behavior?
- 2021-06-14: "Motivation is the desire to escape discomfort." (S063)
- 2023-03-13: "the root cause of human behavior is to relieve discomfort" (S067)

## Dark patterns and subscriptions
- 2021-05-30: "make it as easy to cancel a subscription as it is to start one" (S253)

## Infinite scroll
- 2012-08-21: "Like a loose slot machine, the infinite scroll gives users fast access to variable rewards." (S058)

## Habit testing
- 2012-04-11: "if at least 5% of your users don’t find your product valuable enough to use as much as you predicted they should, you have a problem" (S054)

## Habit Zone
- 2012-09-04: "The Habit Zone,” as I call it, is where an action occurs with enough frequency and perceived utility for it to become the default behavior." (S049)

## The Hooked loop in one line
- 2012-03: "a trigger to begin using the product, an action to satisfy the trigger, a variable reward for the action, and some type of investment that, ultimately, makes the product more valuable to the user" (S057)

## Habits as exploitation
- 2012-03: "If used to exploit, habits can turn into wasteful addictions." (S057)

## Screen time for kids
- 2020-09-21: his advice (S040) is to meet the child's needs for autonomy, competence and relatedness rather than only cap hours. Paraphrase.
- 2018-05-21: he doubts the claim that screens alone cause depression. Paraphrase of S158 and S041.

## Ego depletion
- 2019 to 2022: he rejects the idea that willpower is a limited tank. Paraphrase of S065 and S066.

## Meetings
- 2024-03-25: he treats boredom in meetings as an attention problem to design for. Paraphrase of S104. See also S038.

## Venture capital
- 2023-07-17: he tells founders what VCs look for in a habit-forming product. Paraphrase of S076.

## Marketplaces
- 2013-06-18: network effects are a curse as well as a gift. Paraphrase of S329.

## Scarcity
- 2013-07-09: scarcity boosts desire when used honestly. Paraphrase of S328.

## Users who cannot stop
- 2017-05-03: the Q-tip effect and the call to help users. Paraphrase of S019 (see the quote above).

## Willpower, pain and desire
- Wants and pain: he says desire is partly pain. Paraphrase of S332.

## Stack Overflow style usage limits
- Paraphrase of S338: limits on use may help prevent exploitive addiction.
