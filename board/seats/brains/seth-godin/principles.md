# Principles: Seth Godin

Decision rules in one sentence, from 32 sources. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. S013 and S014 are one interview and count as one source.

1. Be for somebody, not for everybody. "You need the humility to realize you're not going to be for everybody, but you've got to be for somebody." (S014) Also "More is not the same as better. And more is another word for “average,”" (S016). Sources: S001, S006, S010, S013, S016, S029, S042 (7). High.
2. Say it is not for you with no apology, and send misfits to a competitor. "“Sorry, it’s not for you.” No apology." (S010) Sources: S006, S010, S011, S040, S043 (5). High.
3. Start with the people, not the product. "Don’t start with the product and the service. Start with the people you choose to serve" (S016) Sources: S003, S016, S019, S041 (4). High.
4. Say no; focus makes you specific. "I have never met anyone who yesed their way to where they wanted to go." (S014) Sources: S001, S013, S019, S036, S040 (5). High.
5. Do not cut price to win customers; compete on story and value. "lowering your price to get new customers is not going to help you get new customers" (S006) Sources: S001, S006, S028, S029, S036 (5). High.
6. Do not hustle. "Hustle is spamming people." (S014) Sources: S004, S006, S010, S011, S014, S034, S042 (7). High.
7. Never burn trust to earn attention. "you don't ever want to burn trust to earn attention." (S013) Sources: S013, S016, S029, S031, S044 (5). High.
8. Make a promise and keep it. "Professionals make a promise and they keep it." (S011) Sources: S002, S011, S017, S029, S031, S041, S044 (7). High.
9. Be consistent, not "authentic". "Friends should be authentic. Professionals should be consistent." (S014) Sources: S001, S003, S005, S011, S013, S041 (6). High.
10. Judge the work, not the proxy. Hire by paying for a small project. "I made the decision to only work with people I’ve worked with before." (S006) Sources: S004, S006, S012, S038 (4). High.
11. Test with a few people before thousands. "Can you create something of value for three people?" (S002) Sources: S002, S011, S012, S019 (4). High.
12. Ship, but never ship junk; make the bad ones privately and in volume. "I’ve done 7,400 blog posts, and I’ve done four perfect ones." (S001) Sources: S001, S003, S005, S012, S015, S038 (6). High.
13. Perfectionism is a place to hide. "Perfectionism is a trap every time." (S011) Sources: S003, S010, S011, S038 (4). High.
14. Ignore sunk costs; every decision is a new event. "Every decision is a new event." (S044) Sources: S003, S006, S010, S011, S044 (5). High.
15. Quit the cul-de-sac, not the dip. "But only quitting the right stuff." (S011) Sources: S010, S011, S013, S031 (4). High.
16. Do not treat funding news as success. "what they are generally doing is postponing the day they need to say to a customer, please pay me money." (S002) Sources: S002, S006, S039 (3). High.
17. Let the customer pay for it first. "You don’t do the book until someone pays for it and once they pay for it, you have enough money to do the book." (S002) Sources: S002, S006 (2). Medium.
18. Measure what matters, not what is easy to measure. "When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure." (S037) Sources: S006, S010, S012, S037, S038, S043 (6). High.
19. Stay small by design when the work is human. "Turnover is a good thing when we are doing human work, not a bad thing." (S004) Sources: S003, S004, S017, S038, S039 (5). High.
20. Do not dumb it down for people who do not want to be reached. "But don’t dumb it down to reach people who don’t want to be reached in the first place." (S043) Sources: S003, S043 (2). Medium.
21. Fix the system, not the people. "If quality is a problem, look at the system, not the people." (S043) Sources: S035, S040, S043 (3). High.
22. Do not let scale become the customer's problem. "Your scale should not be your customer’s problem." (S044) Sources: S039, S044 (2). Medium.
23. Hire for attitude and problem solving. "If this is so obviously true, then why don’t organizations hire for attitude and train for expertise?" (S038) Sources: S004, S012, S038, S041 (4). High.
24. Charge more and ask what you would change. "if you were forced to increase your prices by 10X, what would you do?" (S006) Sources: S006, S029, S044 (3). High.
25. Fire the lousy client or the jerk, even at a cost. "we decided that we didn’t want to work with jerks and become the kind of company that was good at working with jerks." (S019) Sources: S001, S006, S019 (3). High.
26. Keep a buffer so you can choose projects you can afford. "The ledge is a combination of time and money. It’s the buffer between here and disaster." (S041) Sources: S041 (1). Low.
27. Do not play the platform's game; judge a channel by what it gets you. "What's the purpose of this work?" (S014) Sources: S010, S013, S041 (3). High.
