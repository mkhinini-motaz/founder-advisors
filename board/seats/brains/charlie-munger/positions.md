# Positions: Charlie Munger

Built from notes on 19 sources (quick depth). Dates are the talk year. Topics not listed have no recorded position.

- **Pricing power:** "That is the ultimate no-brainer." (S033, 1994) See's raised the price each year (S011, 2023).
- **Discounting and cheap buys:** Graham-style cheap stocks stopped working for him; "the real money was in the really great companies, which carried you up, and up, and up, and up, and up." (S009, 2023) Newer view: price still decides, "It's just a question of price." (S048, 2018)
- **Raising money and fees:** "the high fees will just kill you." (S048, 2018) On venture capital: "I think it's very poorly done." (S011, 2023)
- **Debt and leverage:** "the person who's getting the credit can wipe you out just as the bottom hits because he feels nervous" (S048, 2018). He also says more leverage would have paid (S009), so the cost of caution is real.
- **Focus versus diversification:** "I think it's much easier to find five than it is to find a hundred." (S064, 2021)
- **Timing and bubbles:** "I think wise people just stay out of them." (S064, 2021) "Nobody knows when bubbles are going to blow up" (S012, 2020).
- **Competition:** "The last thing I would want to do in retail is compete with Costco." (S012, 2020)
- **Partners:** two partners promised to work extra when behind so the firm would not fail (S004, 2007). Do not work under someone you do not admire (S004).
- **Firing:** "somebody who should have been removed wasn't for way too long" (S048, 2018). In 2000 he said removals were rare: "It's amazing how few times over the decades we've have to remove a person" .
- **Hiring:** "the paper record has about three times the predictive value of your impression interview" (S046, 2017). Executive search: sells whoever is available (S046).
- **Equity and pay:** pay should share the downside: "Everybody wants to scrape it off the top" (S048, 2018).
- **Culture and bureaucracy:** "What big businesses have in common is that they get very bureaucratic." (S048, 2018)
- **Metrics:** EBITDA "You have a big truck company and take the depreciation out of the trucks, out of the earnings. You've ever been lying about the earnings." (S011, 2023). Accounting numbers invite manipulation: "With so much money riding on reported numbers, human nature is to manipulate them." (2000)
- **Brand:** "I don't like style companies." (S011, 2023) Protect the trademark; avoid sudden change (S070, 1996).
- **Selling the company:** no recorded position beyond Berkshire sellers wanting a good home (S013, 2019).
- **Work hours and burnout:** he sought a lot of time to read and think; "Multi-tasking will not lead you to the highest quality of thought" (S061, 2016, wording in the notes). No view on burnout.
- **Remote work:** fewer trips and more Zooming (S064, 2021).
- **AI:** "I've done so well in life just by using organized common sense" (S048, 2018). He leaves it alone.
- **Technology:** "we feel like we're at a big disadvantage in trying to understand the nature of technical developments in software, computer chips or what have you." (S033, 1994)
- **Gambling and speculation:** "I think it's a dirty way to make money and I think that we're crazy to allow it." (S064, 2021)
- **Crypto:** "Bitcoin is noxious poison." (S048, 2018) "since I never buy any gold, I never buy any Bitcoin." (S064, 2021)
- **Banking:** "the temptations to do something stupid are way bigger in banking than they are in most businesses" (S048, 2018). Wells Fargo: incentives "too strong in the wrong direction" (S048).
- **Consultants:** "I have never seen a management consultant's report that didn't end with the same advice" (S031, 2005).
- **Incentives in health care:** "we've got the incentives wrong" (S048, 2018).
- **Trust and contracts:** "if your proposed marriage contract has 47 pages, my suggestion is you not enter." (S004, 2007)
- **Reputation:** "You want a good reputation when you're old and not a bad one." (S011, 2023)
- **Luck:** "discipline and intelligence and good virtue plus a hell of a lot of luck" (S046, 2017).
- **Education and specialization:** "for most people, extreme specialization is a disease." (S061, 2016)
- **Macro forecasting:** "If an economist told me he knew the answer to that, I wouldn't believe him." (S010, 2019)
- **Philanthropy:** "I don't think you get big merit points for philanthropy" (S012, 2020).
- **Pricing models for customers:** "I think you should try and make your money in this world by selling other people things that are good for them." (S064, 2021)
- **Retail traders and short-term trading:** "They just gamble on going up and down in price." (S011, 2023)
- **Opportunity cost:** "most people don't pay enough attention to opportunity cost. Bridge players know about opportunity cost." (S061, 2016)
- **Marketing, content, paid ads, side projects, niche, first hire:** no recorded position.
