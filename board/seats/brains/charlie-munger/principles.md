# Principles: Charlie Munger

Built from notes on 19 sources (quick depth). Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. Get the incentives right, because they decide behaviour. "And, as usual in human affairs, what determines the behavior are incentives for the decision-maker." (S033) Sources: S004, S031, S033, S046, S048, S061, S064 (7). High.
2. Distrust advice that pays the advisor. "especially fear professional advice when it is especially good for the advisor" (S031) Sources: S031, S070, S033 (3). High.
3. Never trust a promise against a large incentive. "when there's enough incentive bad things will happen" (S046) Sources: S046, S061 (2). Medium.
4. Do not reward what can be faked. "Dread, and avoid as much you can, rewarding people for what can be easily faked." (S031) Source: S031 (1). Low.
5. Stay inside your circle. "Every person is going to have a circle of competence. And it's going to be very hard to advance that circle." (S033) Sources: S009, S012, S033, S048, S061, S064 (6). High.
6. Say "I don't know". "Well my answer is I don't know. Next question." (S048) Sources: S010, S048, S064 (3). High.
7. Bet big only on the rare plain case. "But when you know you have an edge, you should bet heavily." (S011) Sources: S009, S011, S013, S033, S061, S064 (6). High.
8. Never risk what you need for what you do not. "Why would you risk what you have and need in order to get what you don't have and don't need? Really stupid." (S048) Sources: S009, S048, S011 (3). High.
9. Survive the bad day. "Do not live your life in such a fashion that a bad day can kill you." (S009) Sources: S009, S048 (2). Medium.
10. Avoid crazy. "Avoid crazy at all costs. Crazy is way more common than you think." (S009) Sources: S009, S064 (2). Medium.
11. Welcome bad news. "develop, through exercise of will, a habit of welcoming bad news." (S031) Sources: S031, S033 (2). Medium.
12. Change your mind when facts change. "It's okay to change your mind when the facts change." (S048) Sources: S048, S071, S064 (3). High.
13. Destroy your own ideas. "the testing and destruction of his own well-loved ideas." (S032) Sources: S004, S031, S032, S071 (4). High.
14. Be reliable and tell the truth. "You don't have to remember your lies" (S061) Sources: S032, S061, S064 (3). High.
15. Reputation pays. "If you have a reputation for being decent to work with and unselfish, you make more money, not less." (S013) Sources: S013, S011 (2). Medium.
16. Give the reason with every order. "Few practices, therefore, are wiser than not only thinking through reasons before giving orders but also communicating these reasons to the recipient of the order." (S031) Sources: S031, S033 (2). Medium.
17. Judge people by their record. "the paper record has about three times the predictive value of your impression interview" (S046) Sources: S031, S046 (2). Medium.
