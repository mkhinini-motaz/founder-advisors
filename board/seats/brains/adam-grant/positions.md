# Positions: Adam Grant

Built from 66 usable public sources, 2013 to 2026. Quick depth: only topics with evidence are listed. Topics not listed (pricing, discounting, raising money, debt, equity, partners, paid ads, brand, AI, selling the company, competition as strategy, timing) have no recorded position in this corpus.

- **Hiring and interviews**: "We’re better off asking behavioral questions." (2020). Judge for the job, not eye contact. Work samples and seeing questions in advance: look for slow, deep thinkers (S126, 2021). Interviews show someone at their best, the job needs the typical day (S155, 2021).
- **First hire / who to bet on**: "I will bet on the person who wins the most improved award, over the person who, who wins the superstar award." (S141, 2024).
- **Firing and feedback**: avoiding feedback is selfish: "I’ve realized that’s a selfish decision." (S126, 2021). Ask permission first; skip the sandwich.
- **Culture**: "Toxic culture was ten times more important than anything else in predicting whether people were going to leave or stay in their jobs." (S132, 2022).
- **Takers and givers at work**: "one bad apple can spoil a barrel, but one good egg does not make a dozen." (S130, 2015). Long run: "in the short run, takers often outperform givers. But in the long run, that actually flips." (S141, 2024).
- **Work hours and hustle**: "why do we have to worship at the altar of hustle?" (S132, 2022). Productivity is a means: "I think if productivity is your goal, maybe you’ve got the wrong goal." (S117, 2019).
- **Remote and hybrid work**: hybrid is the future, "they were more productive, more satisfied, more likely to stay" (S132, 2022). He says his view of pandemic priorities is anecdotal.
- **Autonomy at work**: "Granting people freedom on the job is a moral responsibility." (S084, 2018).
- **Passion**: loving a job creates a tax: "It's a passion tax, and it needs to be eliminated if you don't want your most motivated people to burn out." (S150, 2019).
- **Decisiveness**: "I think decisiveness is overrated, but I reserve the right to change my mind about that." (S126, 2021).
- **Hard work**: changed. Earlier, a virtue in itself (his own view as he describes it in S121). Newer: "I think that hard work is a means to an end and it’s only virtuous if the ends are worthy." (S121, recorded 2023, posted 2025).
- **Advice from experts**: changed. He used to say go to the biggest superstar: "I always thought you should go to the highest performer, the best expert, the biggest superstar" (S138). Now: one or two steps ahead (S138, S141). Earlier essay: "the best doers are often the worst teachers." (2018).
- **Data-driven decisions**: reconsidered. "I’ve had to rethink this a little bit lately" (S141, 2024).
- **Counterintuitive insights**: changed. "I was wrong to place such a high premium on the unexpected." (S010, 2019).
- **People pleasing**: "The root of chronic people pleasing is not concern for others. It’s concern for their acceptance and approval." (S109, 2026). Revised his own label.
- **Perfectionism**: "when you expect to be flawless, you stop trying anything where you might fail or struggle." (S138, 2023-24). The rise predates smartphones (S111, 2026).
- **Networking**: "I've always found that the best networking happens when people gather for a purpose other than networking." (S151, 2017). Dormant ties give the most valuable help (2020).
- **Intuition**: "intuition is that it's just subconscious pattern recognition." (S141, 2024). Test it in unstable settings (S121).
- **Execution vs ideas**: "execution is massively underrated as a driver of, of whether people are able to change the world around them." (S141, 2024).
- **Forecasting**: certainty about the future is unearned (2024). Superforecasters update more often (S155).
- **Personality tests**: "The MBTI is astrology for nerds." (S152, 2018).
- **Generational claims**: "a lot of the claims that we make about generational differences are stereotypes." (S132, 2022).
- **Disagreement**: "A good debate isn’t about one person declaring victory, it’s about both people making a discovery." (S087, 2022).
- **Leaders**: "We have too many leaders who operate like takers instead of givers." (S141, 2024).
- **Mindfulness**: "Much of the good in the world comes from living in the future and the past, not in the present." (S151, 2017).
- **Long career**: "Every career has a second act—and a third." (S150, 2019).
